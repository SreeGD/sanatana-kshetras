At Thenthiruperai in Pandya Nadu, the Lord is worshipped as Magara Nedungkuzhaikkaadhar, and the shrine is venerated as one of the 108 Divya Desams sung by the Alwars. The unusual name of the presiding deity refers, in tradition, to the great fish-shaped earrings (makara kundalas) that adorn the Lord's long ears, an iconographic distinction from which Thenthiruperai draws its special renown. Set among the southern Divya Desams of the Thamiraparani belt, near the birthplace of Nammalwar, this temple shares in the deep devotional heritage that the Alwars wove around the rivers and villages of the far Tamil south. It was Nammalwar, the chief of the Alwars, who alone consecrated this place in song within the Naalayira Divya Prabandham, the four thousand sacred verses; through his hymns the form of the Lord wearing the makara earrings entered the perpetual liturgy of the Srivaishnava tradition. According to the lore preserved by devotees, the Goddess of this shrine is held in particular affection, and the place is counted among those where the divine consort's presence is especially gracious. The serene setting of Thenthiruperai, like its sister shrines along the river, has long been a station on the pilgrim path that winds through the nine Divya Desams of the region. Worshippers come to gaze upon the Lord adorned with the long, fish-formed earrings and to seek, through the mediation of Nammalwar's verses, the same nearness to the divine that the Alwar himself attained in this beloved country of his birth.

## Festivals

Thenthiruperai, sung in the Nalayira Divya Prabandham, traditionally keeps the annual Brahmotsavam as its foremost festival, with Magara Nedungkuzhaikkaadhar (Senthamarai Kannan) drawn in procession on successive vahanas including the garuda sevai, amid Veda parayanam and Divya Prabandham recitation. Vaikunta Ekadasi in Margazhi is observed with great devotion, accompanied by the ten-day Pagal-pathu and Ra-pathu recitals customary at the Tamiraparani temples. Pavithrotsavam, the festival of purification, and a float festival (Theppotsavam) are held where local usage maintains them. The Tamil months of Purattasi, sacred to Vishnu, and Margazhi draw special worship, while the consort Thirukkurugur Nachiyar (Senthamarai) is honoured with her own observances during the festival cycle.

## Location & Access

This Divya Desam is located at Thenthiruperai in the Thoothukudi district of Pandya Nadu, set among the Tamiraparani river temples close to Alwarthirunagari and Thirukkolur. The nearest major towns and transport hubs are Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi, from which the village is reached by road. In keeping with most Vishnu temples, it generally observes morning and evening darshan hours with a midday closure.
