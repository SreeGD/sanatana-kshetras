The most important Hindu temple in Sri Lanka's Tamil-majority north, Nallur Kandaswamy has roots traceable to the 15th century (with the current structure substantially rebuilt in the 20th century after earlier destructions, including by Portuguese colonial forces in the 17th century). It remains the spiritual center of Sri Lankan Tamil Shaivism and a major pilgrimage site for Tamils across the island and diaspora.
