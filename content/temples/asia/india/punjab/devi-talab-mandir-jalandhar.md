Built around an ancient sacred tank in the heart of Jalandhar, Devi Talab Mandir is dedicated to the goddess Durga and is counted by tradition among the 51 Shakta Pithas -- sites said to mark where a part of the goddess Sati's body fell after her self-immolation, per the well-known Puranic legend shared across all Shakti Peethas. The temple structure itself is not precisely dated in available records, though the tank beside it is documented as at least two centuries old, and the sanctum's interior is noted for gold-leaf work on its walls and dome.

Over time the complex has grown into more of a civic religious campus than a single shrine, adding a scaled replica of the Amarnath cave shrine, a separate Kali Mata temple, and a charitable hospital alongside the original Durga sanctum. Navratri and Durga Puja draw the temple's largest crowds each year, when the tank and surrounding lanes fill with pilgrims.
