Guyana has one of the highest proportions of Hindus of any country outside India (historically around a quarter of the population), descended from indentured laborers brought from India between 1838 and 1917 under British colonial rule. Temples such as this one, often affiliated with the Guyana Sanatan Dharma Maha Sabha (the country's main Hindu religious body), anchor a vibrant, continuously-practiced Hindu community on the South American mainland.
