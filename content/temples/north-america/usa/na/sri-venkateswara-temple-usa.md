Founded from a shrine established in a store basement in 1972, the Sri Venkateswara Temple in Pittsburgh broke ground in Penn Hills on 17 April 1975 and was completed and consecrated on 8 June 1977 — widely regarded as the first Hindu temple in the United States built by Indian immigrants, and a model for the many that followed. Modeled after the Tirumala Venkateswara Temple (and remodeled in 2005 to resemble it more closely), it has served as a focal point for the growing Indian-American Hindu community since the 1970s wave of post-1965 immigration.
