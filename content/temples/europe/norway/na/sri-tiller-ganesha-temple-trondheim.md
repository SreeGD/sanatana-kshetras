The Sri Tiller Ganesha Temple, at Djupmyra 5 in the Tiller district of Trondheim, is dedicated to Ganesha and is one of the Hindu temples that Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in Norway identifies as currently active in the country. Its presence in Trondheim — Norway's third-largest city, roughly 500 km north of Oslo — extends this directory's Norway coverage beyond the capital region, where the already-documented Sanatan Mandir Sabha (Slemmestad) and Sivasubramanayar Alayam (Ammerud, Oslo) are both located, showing that organized Hindu worship in Norway is not confined to Oslo alone.

The temple was built by Eelam Tamilar Sangam, a Sri Lankan Tamil community association established on 1 January 1981, with the temple building itself opening in 2005. It holds daily evening puja, with special services on Fridays and during festivals, and serves a membership of roughly 380, while also hosting guided educational visits from local schools. Named individual founders and a current festival calendar remain unconfirmed; fuller documentation of the temple's congregation would need to be confirmed directly with the community.
