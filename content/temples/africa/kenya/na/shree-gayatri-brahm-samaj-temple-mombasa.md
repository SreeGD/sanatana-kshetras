Listed among Mombasa's places of worship on the city's Mainland side, the Shree Gayatri Brahm Samaj Temple (also referred to as "Shree Mombasa Brahm Samaj") stands on Saramala Street and takes its name from the Gayatri mantra and its associated goddess, alongside "Brahm" (Brahma), the creator deity. Temples specifically oriented around Gayatri or Brahma worship are rare within the broader Hindu temple landscape — most traditions centre on Vishnu, Shiva, or Devi forms — making this institution's dedication a distinctive rather than typical one, likely reflecting a Brahm Samaj/Arya Samaj-influenced reform tradition's emphasis on Vedic worship of Gayatri.

Beyond its Wikipedia listing, multiple independent Kenya business directories corroborate its Saramala Street address, and local social media evidence - including posts describing a "Gayatri Havan" celebration at the temple - confirms it as an active site of ongoing Gayatri-focused devotional worship, moving its theological orientation from name-only inference toward directly observed practice. No founding date, precise deity iconography beyond the temple's name, or fuller institutional history was located during this research pass.
