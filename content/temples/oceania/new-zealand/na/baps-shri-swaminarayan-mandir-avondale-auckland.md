BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir in Avondale, Auckland, is described in available sourcing as the second mandir of the Swaminarayan Sampraday tradition established in New Zealand, following the Papatoetoe-based Shri Swaminarayan Mandir run by the International Swaminarayan Satsang Organisation (ISSO) — a related but organizationally separate Swaminarayan body. This Avondale mandir instead belongs to the Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha (BAPS), the same global organization operating mandirs already documented elsewhere in this directory across Australia and New Zealand, including in Christchurch and Rotorua.

Like other BAPS mandirs, it follows the Akshar-Purushottam theological lineage within Swaminarayan Vaishnavism, centering worship on Bhagwan Swaminarayan. Beyond its existence and Auckland suburb, further institutional details — its exact address, founding or consecration date, and regular festival calendar — were not confirmed in the source consulted this pass.
