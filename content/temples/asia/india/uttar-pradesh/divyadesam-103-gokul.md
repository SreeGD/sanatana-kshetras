Thirukkokulam, the Gokulam of the north sung in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham, is counted among the 108 Divya Desams of the Vaishnava tradition, with the Lord here adored as Navamohana Krishnan. Situated in the Vada Nadu, the northern region, in the cowherd country of Gokul along the Yamuna, this is the hallowed land associated with the boyhood lilas of Sri Krishna. According to the sthala purana and the wider Krishna tradition, it was in Gokulam that the infant Krishna was carried across the river and raised among the cowherds of Nanda and Yashoda, lifting the Govardhana hill, subduing the serpent and the demons sent to destroy him, and enchanting all who beheld him. The presiding deity's name itself, Navamohana, evokes the Lord who newly and endlessly captivates the hearts of devotees. The Alwars, though native to the Tamil south, sang the glories of these northern abodes of Krishna with intense devotion. Periyalwar, who beheld the Lord as the divine child, and his daughter Andal, who loved Krishna as her bridegroom, poured forth verses steeped in the sweetness of Gokulam's cowherd lilas, while Thirumangai Alwar too sang of this sacred place. Through their hymns the land of Krishna's infancy was woven into the sacred geography of the Divya Desams, allowing the devotees of the Tamil land to worship at the cradle of the Lord's most beloved earthly play.

## Festivals

At this Krishna kshetram in the Braj country, the great annual cycle of Sri Krishna's life is observed with deep devotion, above all Sri Krishna Janmashtami, when the Lord's midnight birth is celebrated with abhishekam, song and rejoicing. Govardhan Puja and the autumn festivals associated with Krishna's childhood at Gokul are also kept with fervour in the surrounding region. In the Srivaishnava tradition the temple honours the usual sacred days of the Vishnu calendar, including a Brahmotsavam, Vaikunta Ekadasi in the cold season, and Pavithrotsavam for ritual purification. Through the year the festivals recall Krishna's playful childhood among the cowherds, drawing pilgrims who come to behold the Lord praised by the Azhwars as Navamohana Krishnan.

## Location & Access

The temple stands at Gokul (Thirukkokulam) in the Braj region near Mathura, in the northern Vada Nadu tradition of Uttar Pradesh, set in the Krishna heartland beside the Yamuna. Mathura is the nearest major city and transport hub, well connected by road and rail, with Agra and Delhi the larger gateways further out.
