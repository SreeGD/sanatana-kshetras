    description: "Satyaloka, also called Brahmaloka, is the highest plane within the material universe, the abode of Brahma the creator and the realm from which souls who attain it are traditionally held to achieve liberation directly at the universe's dissolution rather than being reborn, without needing to pass again through lower creation (krama-mukti, gradual liberation). It marks the boundary of material cosmology — the spiritual world (Vaikuntha, Goloka) described elsewhere in this section lies beyond it, outside material creation altogether."
