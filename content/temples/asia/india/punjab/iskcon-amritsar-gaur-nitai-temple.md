ISKCON Amritsar's Sri Sri Gaur Nitai Temple, dedicated to Chaitanya Mahaprabhu and Nityananda Prabhu, is located in the Vrindavan Gardens area on Fatehgarh Churian Road, part of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness founded by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. The temple maintains daily arati and darshan, a weekly Sunday Feast program, and a guesthouse, alongside community initiatives including the ISKCON Sri Gokul Goshala for cow protection and a Food for Life meal-distribution program.
