The Bhaktavatsala Perumal Temple at Thirukkannamangai, in the Chola Nadu region of the sacred Tamil land, is counted among the 108 Divya Desams, the holy abodes of Sriman Narayana celebrated by the Alwar saints. The presiding deity, the Moolavar, is worshipped here as Bhaktavatsala Perumal, a name that itself proclaims the Lord's defining grace as the one who cherishes His devotees with a mother's tenderness. The very place-name Thirukkannamangai, evoking the dark, beautiful lustre of the Lord, points to His standing in a serene, gracious form to receive those who take refuge in Him. Tradition holds that the Lord here is especially the protector and beloved of His bhaktas, and the temple is regarded by devotees as a place where surrender (prapatti) bears its sweet fruit. Among the Alwars, it is Thirumangai Alwar, the prolific saint-poet whose verses crown so many of the Chola country shrines, who sang the glory of this Divya Desam in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham, securing its place within that fourfold corpus of four thousand sacred Tamil hymns. Through his paasurams, the form, the compassion, and the sanctity of Thirukkannamangai entered the eternal liturgy of the Srivaishnava tradition, and the temple has since drawn pilgrims who recite those verses before the Lord. Standing in this hallowed Chola heartland near the Kaveri delta, Bhaktavatsala Perumal continues to be venerated as a refuge for the faithful, the Divya Desam whose very names of place and deity together affirm the Lord's loving regard for all who call upon Him.

## Festivals

The temple at Thirukkannamangai observes the festival cycle dear to Srivaishnava tradition. A grand annual Brahmotsavam is celebrated for the presiding Lord Bhaktavatsala Perumal, with the utsava deity taken in procession on the customary mounts and the Garuda Sevai drawing devotees from the surrounding Chola Nadu villages. Vaikunta Ekadasi is marked with special devotion, the Lord granting darshan during the sacred days associated with the recitation of the Tiruvaymoli and the Tamil Prabandham. Pavithrotsavam, observed to purify and sanctify the year's worship, and a Float festival (Theppotsavam) on the temple tank are traditionally held. The Tamil month observances, Pongal, and the recitation festivals honouring the Alvars are also kept with reverence.

## Location & Access

The Bhaktavatsala Perumal Temple stands at Thirukkannamangai in the Chola Nadu region of the Cauvery delta, in Tiruvarur district of Tamil Nadu. It lies a short distance from Tiruvarur town, the nearest major hub, which is reached by road and rail, with Thanjavur and Nagapattinam as larger junctions in the wider area. Like most Vishnu temples, the shrine generally observes morning and evening darshan hours, remaining closed through the midday interval.
