Geneva hosts a Hare Krishna (ISKCON) temple or centre, corroborated independently of Wikipedia's own listing by a feature article from Swiss public broadcaster SRF, which states that the International Society for Krishna Consciousness maintains centres in Switzerland "including in Zurich and Geneva, where religious practices and gatherings take place." Wikipedia's own "Hinduism in Switzerland" article separately lists a "Hare Krishna Temple - Geneva" among the country's 22 documented Hindu temples as of 2016, alongside the better-documented Zurich temple (established 1980 and already covered elsewhere in this directory).

Beyond confirming its existence and ongoing activity, no source located in this research pass gives Geneva's temple an address, founding date, named founder, or its own official website, and it is not clear whether it operates as a standalone temple building or a smaller devotee centre. This entry is therefore recorded with confirmed current activity but otherwise thin documentation, pending fuller sourcing directly from ISKCON Switzerland.
