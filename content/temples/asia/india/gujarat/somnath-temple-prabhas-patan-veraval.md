Traditionally the first among the twelve Jyotirlingas, Somnath on Gujarat's Saurashtra coast has an unusually well-documented history of repeated destruction and reconstruction. It was sacked at least four times — by Mahmud of Ghazni in 1026 CE, by Alauddin Khalji's forces under Ulugh Khan in 1299, by Muzaffar Shah I in 1395, and by Aurangzeb's order in 1706 — and rebuilt multiple times in between, notably by Kumarapala around 1169 and Mahipala I in 1308. Its present structure was reconstructed beginning in 1950 at the initiative of Indian statesman Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, with the temple consecrated in May 1951.
