Papanasam, at the foot of the Podhigai hills in the Western Ghats near Ambasamudram in Tirunelveli district, is the first of the nine temples of the Nava Kailasam group and is associated with the Sun (Suriyan) among the Navagrahas in that group's tradition. This is a distinct group from the better-known Navagraha temple circuit near Kumbakonam further north in Tamil Nadu; the Nava Kailasam temples instead run along the Thamirabarani river through Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi (Tuticorin) districts.

The presiding deity is worshipped here as Papanasanathar (also rendered Papanasaswamy), "the Lord who destroys sin," at a site closely tied to the nearby Agasthiyar Falls, where the Thamirabarani river tumbles down from its source in the Podhigai hills. According to the shared legend of the Nava Kailasam group, the sage Agastya's disciple, Uromacha Munivar, was instructed to set nine flowers afloat on the Thamirabarani; at each spot a flower came to rest, a Shiva lingam was installed as Kailasa Nathar, and after venerating all nine and bathing where the river meets the sea, the disciple attained liberation. Separately, Tirunelveli district's own tourism materials associate the Agasthiyar Falls site with a legend of the divine wedding of Shiva and Parvathi; the sources checked for this entry do not reconcile these two traditions, so both are recorded here as they appear rather than combined into a single account.

No source consulted for this entry supplies a founding date, ruling dynasty, or inscriptional history for the temple itself, so those specifics are left unstated rather than assumed. The temple's consort is worshipped as Arultharum Ulagammai, and worship follows the Kaamiga Agamam; sacred waters associated with the site include the Thamirabharani river, Vedha Theertham, and Pazhaya Papanadha Theertham. The temple has run a daily Annadhanam (free meal) programme for devotees since 15 September 2011 under the Tamil Nadu government's Chief Minister's Annadhanam scheme.

## Festivals

The temple's notable festivals include Chithirai Visu, Margazhi Urshvam, Kandha Sasti Thiruvizha, and Sivaratri Thiruvizha.

## Location & Access

Papanasam lies about 5 km from Ambasamudram in Ambasamudram Taluk, Tirunelveli district. Bus services run via Cheranmahadevi and Kalloor, with train access through Tenkasi; Tirunelveli town is the nearest major transport hub. The temple follows a detailed daily ritual schedule from about 6:00 AM (Thiruvanthal) to 8:00 PM (Arthasaamam). Verify current darshan timings and travel details with the temple or official sources before visiting.
