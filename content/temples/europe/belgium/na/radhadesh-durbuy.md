A 19th-century château in the Belgian Ardennes acquired by ISKCON and developed into one of continental Europe's principal Gaudiya Vaishnava communities and a base for the Bhaktivedanta College seminary, Radhadesh has served as a hub for Hare Krishna practice, education, and public festivals in Belgium and neighboring countries since the 1980s.
