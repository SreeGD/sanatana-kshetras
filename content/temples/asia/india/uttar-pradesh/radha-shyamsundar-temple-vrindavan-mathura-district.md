Radha Shyamsundar Temple stands in the Seva Kunj locality of central Vrindavan, close by the Radha Damodar Temple, and is traditionally counted among the historic "Seven Goswami Temples" alongside Radha Vallabh, Radha Damodar, Radha Madan Mohan, Radha Raman, Radha Govindji, and Radha Gokulnandan. Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition attributes its founding to Shyamananda Prabhu, an associate of Jiva Goswami, though a firm founding date and independent documentation of this account could not be confirmed from the sources consulted for this entry — it is recorded here as tradition rather than established fact.

What is independently documented is the temple's later association with Baladeva Vidyabhushana, the influential 18th-century Gaudiya Vaishnava philosopher and commentator on the *Vedanta Sutra*, who is credited with installing large deities of Radha-Shyamasundar here; his own samadhi stands at the rear of the temple. Some devotional accounts also place Jiva Goswami's samadhi at this temple, but this conflicts with Wikipedia's own article on the Radha Damodar Temple, which locates Jiva Goswami's samadhi there instead — this entry does not assert a claim either way pending clearer sourcing. Given the comparative thinness of independently verifiable material on this specific temple, this entry is marked pending rather than verified, notwithstanding its well-attested place among Vrindavan's historic Goswami temples.
