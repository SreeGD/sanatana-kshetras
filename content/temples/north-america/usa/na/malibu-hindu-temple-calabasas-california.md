Despite its popular name, the Malibu Hindu Temple sits just outside Malibu proper, on Las Virgenes Road in the Santa Monica Mountains within Calabasas, California. Construction began in 1981, and the complex was built in South Indian Dravidian style — rendered in concrete rather than traditional stone — with two main shrines: an upper temple to Venkateswara (a form of Vishnu) and a lower temple to Shiva, along with subsidiary shrines to Rama, Lakshmi, Bhoodevi, Krishna, and Hanuman. Run today by the Hindu Temple Society of Southern California, it has become one of the most visible Hindu temples in the Los Angeles area, drawing both the region's Indian-American community and, occasionally, celebrity visitors and press attention for its scenic mountain setting.
