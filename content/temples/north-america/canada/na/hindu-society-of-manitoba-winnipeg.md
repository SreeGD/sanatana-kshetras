The Hindu Society of Manitoba is Winnipeg's principal Hindu religious organization, operating at least two facilities — a Hindu Temple and Dr. Raj Pandey Hindu Centre, and a separate Hindu Temple and Cultural Centre — serving the city's Hindu community as sites of worship, cultural programming, and community gathering. Its existence and activity are corroborated independently by a 2010 recognition noted in Wikipedia's article on the Sankurathri Foundation, alongside its listing in Wikipedia's directory of Canadian Hindu temples. No founding date, presiding deity, or specific address could be confirmed this pass; this entry fills what had previously been a gap in this directory's coverage of the Canadian Prairies, where Winnipeg is a notable Hindu community center outside the Toronto-Calgary-Montreal corridor already represented here.
