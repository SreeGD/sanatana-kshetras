A Shiva Shakti Mandir in Tokyo serves devotees of Shiva and the Goddess (Shakti) within Japan's small, diverse Hindu diaspora community, alongside the ISKCON centers, Shirdi Saibaba temple, Vedanta Society, and BAPS mandir also documented separately in this directory. It is named in Wikipedia's survey of Hinduism in Japan as one of a handful of organized Hindu temples and centers in the country, serving communities of Nepali, Indian, and Sri Lankan descent.
