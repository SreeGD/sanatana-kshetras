Housed in the Fisher Mansion, a historic residence on Detroit's east side near the Detroit River, ISKCON Detroit describes itself as the first Vedic temple to open in Michigan. The temple, also known as the Devasadan Mandir, worships the presiding Deities Sri Sri Radha Kunjabihari and hosts regular yoga classes, Sunday feasts, and educational programs, alongside major festivals such as Ratha Yatra. Author and ISKCON devotee Stephen Knapp has headed the temple in recent years.
