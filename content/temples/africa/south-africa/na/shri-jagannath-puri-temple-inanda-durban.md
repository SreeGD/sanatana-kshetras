Dedicated to Jagannath — the form of Vishnu/Krishna venerated as "Lord of the Universe" at the great Puri temple in Odisha, India, and a genuinely rare dedication for a temple outside that tradition's home region — the Shri Jagannath Puri Temple in Inanda, Durban, was founded by Pandit Shiskishan Maharaj, a Hindu priest who immigrated to South Africa in 1895. It was formally recognised as a KwaZulu-Natal provincial heritage site under a 1979 Government Gazette notice.

That heritage listing describes a richly ornamented structure with a 23-metre-high shikhara (tower), a single entrance capped by an octagonal, dome-topped form, and a surrounding moat — architectural features that distinguish it among South Africa's older Hindu temples. Beyond its founder, heritage status, and architectural description, further detail on the temple's precise founding date, current worship schedule, and festival life was not located during this research pass.
