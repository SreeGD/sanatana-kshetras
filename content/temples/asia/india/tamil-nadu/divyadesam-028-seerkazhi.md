The Trivikrama Narayanar Temple at Seerkazhi, known in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham as Kazhicheerama Vinnagaram, is one of the 108 Divya Desams revered by the Alwars, situated in the Chola Nadu country of Tamil Nadu. The presiding deity (Moolavar) is Trivikrama Narayana, the Lord worshipped in the towering form of Trivikrama, the gigantic stride of Vishnu. According to the sthala purana, this form recalls the celebrated Vamana avatara, in which the Lord, having appeared as a dwarf brahmana before the generous king Mahabali, expanded into the cosmic Trivikrama and measured the three worlds in three strides, thereby restoring the order of the universe and subduing the king's pride through grace. The temple shares its sacred ground with the renowned Saiva shrine of Seerkazhi, the birthplace of Thirugnana Sambandar, making this a town of deep devotional significance to both traditions. Among the Alwars, it is Thirumangai Alwar, foremost among the saints in the number of Divya Desams he sang, who hallowed this shrine with his Tamil verses, securing its place forever within the Divya Prabandham. The town of Seerkazhi has long been celebrated in the religious life of the Chola country, and the worship of Vishnu as Trivikrama here invites devotees to contemplate the Lord who, though approaching in humility, encompasses all the worlds. As one of the 108 sacred abodes, Kazhicheerama Vinnagaram stands as a place where the boundless majesty of Narayana is made manifest, drawing pilgrims to behold the Lord who fills heaven and earth.

## Festivals

At this temple in Seerkazhi the principal annual celebration is the Brahmotsavam, traditionally observed over several days with festal processions of the utsavar through the streets. As one of the eleven Thirunangur Divya Desams, the temple takes part in the renowned Thirunangur Garuda Sevai, when the processional deities of the surrounding temples are borne together on their Garuda mounts during the Tamil month of Thai, a gathering that draws large crowds of devotees. Vaikunta Ekadasi in Margazhi is observed with special reverence and the chanting of the Tiruvaymoli, alongside the broader Srivaishnava cycle of festival days. Pavithrotsavam, the festival of purification, and a Float festival (Theppotsavam) are traditionally kept as well, with daily worship enriched during these seasons.

## Location & Access

The temple stands at Seerkazhi (Kazhicheerama Vinnagaram) in the Chola Nadu region of Tamil Nadu, in the Nagapattinam district near the cluster of Thirunangur Divya Desams. Seerkazhi is reached by road and rail and lies within reach of the larger towns of Mayiladuthurai, Sirkazhi and Chidambaram, with broader transport connections through the Nagapattinam and Thanjavur region.
