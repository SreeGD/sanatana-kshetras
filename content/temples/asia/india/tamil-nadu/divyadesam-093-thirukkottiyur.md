Thirukkottiyur, in the Pandya country, enshrines the Lord as Sowmya Narayana Perumal, the serene and gracious form of Narayana, and ranks among the 108 Divya Desams sung by the Alwars in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. According to the sthala purana, this is an ancient and deeply revered abode where the Lord manifests in his peaceful aspect, and the place is honoured in tradition as a centre of great sanctity in the Vaishnava world. Thirukkottiyur holds a singular place in the later history of Srivaishnavism as the abode associated with the teacher Thirukkottiyur Nambi, from whom the great acharya Ramanuja is traditionally said to have received, after repeated entreaty, the sacred esoteric meaning of the Tirumantra; this living memory lends the kshetra a profound importance in the sampradaya beyond its Prabandham glory. The shrine is hymned by the early Mudhal Alwars Bhoothath Alwar and Pey Alwar, by Thirumazhisai Alwar, by Periyalwar, and by Thirumangai Alwar, whose verses together establish its standing within the divine corpus. Worshipped as the calm and benevolent Narayana attended by his consort, the Lord of Thirukkottiyur is revered for bestowing tranquillity and grace upon those who seek refuge. As a temple woven into both the hymns of the Alwars and the foundational tradition of Ramanuja's teaching, Thirukkottiyur remains one of the most spiritually significant of the Pandya Nadu Divya Desams, cherished by devotees and acharyas alike.

## Festivals

Thirukkottiyur, hallowed in Srivaishnava tradition as the place where Acharya Ramanuja received and openly shared the sacred Tirumantra, observes the annual Brahmotsavam as its principal festival, with the Lord Sowmya Narayana taken in procession on the customary vahanas amid Divya Prabandham recitation. Vaikunta Ekadasi during Margazhi is kept with special darshan, and the temple traditionally observes Pavithrotsavam and a Float festival (Theppotsavam). Days associated with Ramanuja and the great acharyas hold particular significance here given the temple's role in the disciplic tradition. The broader Vaishnava festival cycle, including Pongal, Tamil New Year, Krishna Jayanthi, Navaratri and Deepavali, is also marked with abhishekam, alankaram and devotional singing.

## Location & Access

Sowmya Narayana Perumal Temple lies at Thirukkottiyur in the Pandya Nadu region, within the Sivaganga district, set in a town some distance from Madurai, which is the nearest major city and transport hub; Sivaganga and Karaikudi are the closer towns served by road and rail. Buses and taxis reach Thirukkottiyur from these centres. Like most Vishnu temples, it generally observes morning and evening darshan hours with a midday closure.
