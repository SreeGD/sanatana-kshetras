ISKCON Brisbane operates the Sri Sri Gaura Nitai Temple at 8 Lee Road in the Darra suburb of southwest Brisbane, part of the worldwide International Society for Krishna Consciousness founded by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. The temple centers its worship on Sri Sri Gaura Nitai — the paired deity forms of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu (regarded in Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition as a combined incarnation of Krishna and Radha) and his companion Nityananda (identified with Balarama) — reflecting the Bengali bhakti lineage from which ISKCON descends.

The center runs an active weekly program built around a Saturday evening feast at 5pm, featuring kirtan (devotional chanting), a spiritual class, evening aarti (lamp ceremony), and a vegetarian prasadam dinner served 6-7pm, alongside daily Srimad Bhagavatam study classes (held both via Zoom and in person on weekend mornings) and Friday evening Harinam Sankirtan (public chanting processions) from King George Square in the Brisbane CBD. The temple describes its recently opened building as "more than a place of worship" — a community gathering space centered on mantra meditation, devotional music, and service — complementing the affiliated Govinda's vegetarian and vegan restaurant it runs in the Brisbane CBD.
