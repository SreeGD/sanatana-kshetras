ISKCON maintains an active presence in Kuala Lumpur and elsewhere across Malaysia, part of the wider Gaudiya Vaishnava community that has grown in the country since the movement's international expansion under Founder-Acharya A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. As in other Southeast Asian countries with significant Hindu minority populations (largely of Tamil and North Indian descent), ISKCON in Malaysia operates alongside other Krishna Consciousness-adjacent groups, including followers associated with the Ritvik and Hansadutta Swami lineages and the older Gaudiya Math and Saraswat Math traditions from which ISKCON itself descends.

Public information confirming a single named flagship temple, exact address, or founding date for ISKCON's Kuala Lumpur operations was not found during this research pass; this entry documents the confirmed fact of ISKCON's ongoing presence and temple activity in the city, pending a more specific source that can confirm the details of a particular building.
