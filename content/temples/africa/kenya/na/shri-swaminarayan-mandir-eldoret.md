Eldoret, a major city in Kenya's Rift Valley region with a long-established Gujarati Hindu trading community, is home to a tri-spired Shri Swaminarayan Mandir built in 2011 and affiliated with the NarNarayan Dev Gadi branch of the Swaminarayan sampradaya, per Wikipedia's inventory of Swaminarayan temples worldwide.

No address or further institutional detail beyond its 2011 construction date and tri-spire architectural design was located during this research pass; it is documented here only via a bare listing in Wikipedia's Swaminarayan temple inventory, but it fills a genuine geographic gap as the only currently documented Hindu temple in this directory for Eldoret.
