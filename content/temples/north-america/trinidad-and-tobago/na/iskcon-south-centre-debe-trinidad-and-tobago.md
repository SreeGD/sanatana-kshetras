ISKCON South Centre, on Orion Drive in Debe, is documented in Wikipedia's "List of Hindu temples in Trinidad and Tobago" as one of at least two ISKCON (International Society for Krishna Consciousness)-affiliated centers in Trinidad, alongside the Sri Sri Radha Gopinath Mandir in Longdenville. Debe sits within south Trinidad's Penal-Debe region, one of the island's most heavily Indo-Trinidadian areas, consistent with ISKCON's broader pattern of establishing preaching centers within existing concentrations of diaspora Hindu population across the Americas.

As with ISKCON centers generally, worship here would be expected to center on Radha-Krishna in the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition founded by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, with congregational kirtan, festival celebrations (notably Janmashtami), and prasadam distribution. However, Wikipedia's own source list flags this specific entry as needing stronger citation, and no founding date, founders, or independent second source could be located for it in this pass; it should be treated as provisional.
