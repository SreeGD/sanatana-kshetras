Inaugurated by ISKCON founder A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada on Ram Navami, 20 April 1975, with the installation of Krishna-Balaram, Radha-Shyamasundar, and Gaura-Nitai deities, Sri Krishna Balaram Mandir is one of the principal ISKCON temples worldwide. Prabhupada's own samadhi (memorial shrine), built of white marble, stands at the entrance of the temple complex — he passed away at this site on 14 November 1977. The temple maintains a tradition of continuous (24-hour) kirtan.
