Founded in 1905 in Marabastad — historically Pretoria's "Asiatic Bazaar," a mixed Indian, Malay, and Black South African trading quarter under apartheid-era racial zoning — the Mariamman Temple is recorded as the oldest Hindu temple in the city. It is dedicated to Mariamman, the South Indian goddess widely worshipped as a protector against smallpox and other infectious diseases, reflecting the temple's origins among Pretoria's Tamil-descended Hindu community.

The temple is documented in South African Heritage Resources Agency (SAHRA) records and remains a landmark of Marabastad, a neighbourhood whose Wikipedia entry describes the temple as arguably its most prominent religious landmark alongside the area's mosques and churches. Beyond its founding date and dedication, detailed information on its architecture, current worship schedule, and festival calendar was not located during this research pass.
