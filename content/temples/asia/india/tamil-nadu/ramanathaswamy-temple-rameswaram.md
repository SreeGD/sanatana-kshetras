One of the twelve Jyotirlingas and one of the Char Dham of Hindu pilgrimage, Ramanathaswamy is traditionally held to enshrine a linga installed by Rama himself (with Hanuman's help) to atone for killing the Brahmin Ravana, on his return from Lanka. The temple's present form, especially its famous 1,200-meter pillared corridor, was substantially built up over centuries under Pandya, Chola, and Setupati (Ramnad) patronage.
