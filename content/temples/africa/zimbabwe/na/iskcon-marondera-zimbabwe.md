Described in Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in Zimbabwe simply as a centre of "the Krishna-oriented ISKCON" (International Society for Krishna Consciousness), this Marondera institution is one of only two Hindu places of worship the source names for the entire country - the other being the much older Omkar Temple in Harare, already documented in this directory, whose foundation stone was laid in 1929.

Marondera, a town in Mashonaland East Province roughly 70km southeast of Harare, thus hosts what available sources indicate is Zimbabwe's second organised Hindu place of worship, reflecting ISKCON's global pattern of establishing centres even within small diaspora Hindu communities. No founding date, address, or further detail on this centre's history or current activities was located during this research pass.
