Coordination manifest for the Malai Nadu region group of the 108 Divya Desams
(the Kerala/hill-country temples of the old Chera kingdom). Final `seq`
numbers (1-108), `locked: true`, and individual `content/divya-desam/*.md`
files are assigned centrally by the reviewer merging all region-group
manifests; this file is not itself a Divya Desam entry and does not
conform to `divya-desam.schema.json`.

Two of the thirteen traditionally-grouped temples above --
`adikesava-perumal-temple-thiruvattar` and
`thiruvazhmarban-temple-thiruppathisaram` -- are physically located in
Kanyakumari district, Tamil Nadu today (filed under
`content/temples/asia/india/tamil-nadu/`), even though they belong to the
Malai Nadu region group by tradition; see each file's `review_flags` for
detail.
