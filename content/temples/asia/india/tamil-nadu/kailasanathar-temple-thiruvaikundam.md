Srivaikuntam (also known as Thiruvaikundam), on the north bank of the Thamirabarani river in Thoothukudi (Tuticorin) district, is the sixth of the nine Nava Kailasam temples and is associated with Saturn (Sani) among the Navagrahas in that group's tradition — a distinct tradition from the separate Navagraha temple circuit near Kumbakonam further north in Tamil Nadu.

The town is notable for holding two major temples side by side: the Vaishnava Vaikuntanathar (Kallapiran) temple, one of the Nava Tirupathi cluster of the 108 Divya Desams sung by Nammalwar, and — separately — this Shiva Kailasanathar temple of the Nava Kailasam group. Wikipedia's article on the town, consulted for this entry, explicitly distinguishes the two and records that the Kailasanathar temple "contains six inscriptions of the times of Kattabomman," referring to the era of the 18th-century Panchalankurichi chieftain Veerapandiya Kattabomman, which is the most specific historical detail confirmed for this temple among the nine Nava Kailasam sites researched.

As with the wider group, tradition connects the nine temples to the sage Agastya's disciple Uromacha Munivar, said to have installed a Shiva lingam as Kailasa Nathar at each of nine places along the Thamirabarani where a floated flower came to rest, attaining liberation after venerating all nine. The temple's consort is worshipped as Sivagami Amman, its sacred tree is the iluppai (Indian butter tree), and worship follows the Karana Agamam tradition; local devotional belief holds worship here to be equivalent in merit to worshipping Shiva at Thirunallar near Karaikal.

## Festivals

The temple observes monthly Pradosham and a special Sani Peyarchi (Saturn's periodic sign-change) worship, reflecting its standing as a Sani (Saturn) sthalam within the Nava Kailasam group.

## Location & Access

Srivaikuntam lies about 22 km from Tirunelveli on the Tirunelveli-Thiruchendur road, on the north bank of the Thamirabarani river in Thoothukudi district. Bus services run from Tirunelveli, Thoothukudi and Tiruchendur, with Srivaikundam's own railway station and Thoothukudi's airport the nearest transport links; lodging is available in Tirunelveli. The temple observes darshan from about 6:00 AM to 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM, and is administered by an Executive Officer registered at Srivaikundam Nagar and Taluk, Tuticorin district - 628 621. Verify current darshan timings and travel details with the temple or official sources before visiting.
