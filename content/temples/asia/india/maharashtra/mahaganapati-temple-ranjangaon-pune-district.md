Tradition holds that the Mahaganapati form of Ganesha here aided Shiva in the destruction of the demon Tripurasura's three fortress-cities (the Tripura legend). Wikipedia's account of the site dates the temple's construction to between the 9th and 10th centuries, with the Peshwa ruler Madhavrao later adding a basement chamber to house a Ganesha idol. On the Pune-Ahmednagar highway, it is among the more easily accessible and frequently visited of the eight Ashtavinayak shrines.
