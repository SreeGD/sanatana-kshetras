Opened in 1913 in the Temple Valley suburb of Verulam, on South Africa's KwaZulu-Natal coast north of Durban, the Sri Gopallal Hindu Temple holds particular historical distinction: Wikipedia's article on Verulam records that it was opened by Mahatma Gandhi himself, during his decades of activism among South Africa's Indian community before his return to India in 1914-1915. It is described as one of the oldest Hindu temples in South Africa.

More than a century later, the temple remains active, still used for both regular prayer and wedding ceremonies according to available sources. Its name, "Gopallal," evokes Krishna in his cowherd form, though the specific iconography and founding community behind the temple are not further detailed in the source located this pass.
