The Motishwar Mandir, a Shiva temple in Muscat, is one of the oldest and most historically significant Hindu temples anywhere in the Middle East, reflecting a long-established Hindu merchant community in Oman that predates the country's modern development. Sources describe it variously as roughly 125 to 200 years old, though no single confirmed founding date could be established for this entry — what is clear is that it has functioned as a center of Hindu worship in the Omani capital for well over a century.

The temple gained wider international attention on 12 February 2018, when Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi performed an Abhishekam (a ritual anointing of the Shiva lingam) here during a state visit, underscoring both the temple's historic standing and the broader diplomatic significance of Oman's small but long-tolerated Hindu community. It is one of two officially recognized Hindu temples in Muscat, the other being a separate Krishna Temple; together they serve an Omani Hindu population estimated at roughly 260,000 as of 2020, drawn primarily from Indian expatriate and long-settled merchant communities.
