Consecrated in 2009, the Sri Muthumariamman Temple in Hanover, Lower Saxony, was established by Germany's Sri Lankan Tamil migrant community — part of the same wave of Tamil refugee settlement that founded the much larger Sri Kamadchi Ampal Temple in Hamm, already documented elsewhere in this directory. The temple is dedicated to Muthumariamman, a South Indian and Sri Lankan Tamil form of the goddess Mariamman (identified with Parvati/Shakti), particularly associated with protection from disease and with village and community welfare in Tamil folk-Shakta tradition.

Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in Germany notes that the temple has, since its founding, also drawn native German worshippers alongside its core Sri Lankan Tamil congregation — an indication of the wider local interest Tamil Hindu temples have attracted in the parts of Germany, like North Rhine-Westphalia and Lower Saxony, where Sri Lankan Tamil refugee communities settled in significant numbers from the 1980s onward. Beyond its 2009 consecration date and Tamil Shakta dedication, further detail on the temple's founders, building, and current congregation could not be confirmed in the source available for this entry.
