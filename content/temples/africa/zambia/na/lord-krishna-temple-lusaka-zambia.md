Named and pictured, but not otherwise described, in Wikipedia's overview of the Indian community in Zambia, the Lord Krishna Temple in Lusaka serves the country's small Hindu community, most of whom descend from Gujarati traders who began arriving in what was then British North-Eastern Rhodesia from 1905 onward. No source located this pass gives a founding date, founder, or detailed history for the temple itself; what can be confirmed is limited to its name, its dedication to Krishna, and its location in the Zambian capital.
