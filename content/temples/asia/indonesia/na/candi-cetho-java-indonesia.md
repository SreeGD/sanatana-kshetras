Candi Cetho stands on the western slope of Mount Lawu at roughly 1,495 meters elevation, near the Central Java/East Java provincial border and a short distance uphill from the similarly dated Candi Sukuh, already documented separately in this directory. Like its neighbor, Cetho dates to the 15th century, the final flowering of Hindu temple-building in Java under the fading Majapahit empire, and shares a broadly comparable architectural character: a multi-tiered, terraced complex of courtyards and statuary ascending the mountainside.

Available sourcing for this pass could not pin down Cetho's precise presiding deity, exact founding date, or original ceremonial purpose with the same specificity available for Sukuh, nor could it confirm whether organized Hindu worship continues at the site today. This entry documents Candi Cetho's confirmed existence and its place within the same Mount Lawu complex of late Majapahit-era Hindu heritage sites as Candi Sukuh, pending fuller independent sourcing on its religious specifics.
