Named among Mombasa's places of worship in the Nyali neighbourhood, the Shree Ramdev Pir Temple is dedicated to Ramdev Pir (Baba Ramdev Pir), a revered folk saint-deity of Rajasthan whose devotees regard him as an incarnation of Vishnu associated with justice and protection for the marginalised. A temple to this specific folk deity is unusual outside Rajasthan and its diaspora communities, making this a distinctive rather than typical addition to East Africa's Hindu temple landscape.

Beyond its name and Nyali location, no dedicated source describing this temple's founding, architecture, or current worship activities was located during this research pass; it is documented here only via a bare listing in Wikipedia's overview of Mombasa's religious institutions.
