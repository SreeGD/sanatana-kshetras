Built in 1855 by Rani Rashmoni, a wealthy widow of the Mahishya caste who, defying the era's caste conventions, financed and dedicated one of Bengal's most important Kali temples. It is closely associated with the 19th-century mystic Ramakrishna Paramahamsa, who served as a priest here and whose teachings, transmitted through his disciple Swami Vivekananda, carried the temple's influence worldwide.
