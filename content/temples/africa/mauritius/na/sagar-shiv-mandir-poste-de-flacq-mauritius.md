A modern addition to Mauritius's Hindu temple landscape, Sagar Shiv Mandir was established in 2007 at Goyave de Chine on the coast of Poste de Flacq, funded substantially by donor Vikash Gunowa. Its most striking feature is a 108-foot bronze-coloured statue of Shiva overlooking the lagoon and surrounding mangroves, a setting that has drawn comparisons to Trinidad and Tobago's well-known "Temple in the Sea." The temple serves as a place of worship for Mauritius's Hindu community as well as a popular stop for tourists touring the island's east coast.
