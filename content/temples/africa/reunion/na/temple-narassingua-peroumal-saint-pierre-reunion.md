Situated in the Ravine Blanche neighbourhood of Saint-Pierre, on Réunion's southern coast, the Temple Narassingua Peroumal takes its name from Narasimha ("Narassingua"), the man-lion avatar of Vishnu, worshipped here under the honorific "Perumal" - a genuinely rare avatar dedication for a temple outside India, and a distinctive addition alongside the many Murugan-, Mariamman-, and Kali-dedicated temples more typical of the Tamil diaspora's temple-building in Réunion and elsewhere.

The temple gained wider public recognition when it was featured during the 2020 season of the French television programme "Le Monument préféré des Français" ("The French People's Favourite Monument"). It is distinct from the already-documented Temple des Casernes, also located in Saint-Pierre but in a different neighbourhood. Beyond its location and television feature, French Wikipedia's stub article gives no founding date or further institutional history.
