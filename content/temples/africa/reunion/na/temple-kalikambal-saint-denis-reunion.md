The Temple Kalikambal stands on Rue du Maréchal-Leclerc in downtown Saint-Denis, Réunion's capital, one of the island's Tamil Hindu temples serving a community descended largely from nineteenth-century indentured laborers brought to Réunion's plantations after the abolition of slavery in 1848. Its name, Kalikambal, is a South Indian Tamil epithet for the goddess Kali as universal mother - the same name carried by the well-known Kalikambal Temple in Chennai - suggesting a Shakta dedication to the goddess, though this specific temple's presiding deity is not independently confirmed beyond its name.

Both French Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons document the temple's location precisely but describe it only in stub form, with no construction date, founder, or detailed history available in sources accessible for this entry. Its continued presence as a named, photographed place of worship in the heart of Saint-Denis nonetheless confirms it as an active site within Réunion's wider, long-established Tamil Hindu community.
