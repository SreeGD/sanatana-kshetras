The Shri Muthukumaraswamy Alayam, on Rue des Solitaires in Paris's 19th arrondissement, is dedicated to Murugan under his name Muthukumaraswamy ("pearl-like youthful lord"), one of several forms of the deity venerated across the network of Tamil temples that has grown up in Paris and its northern suburbs since Sri Lankan Tamil refugees began settling in the region from the 1980s onward. It joins the already-documented Sri Manicka Vinayakar Alayam and the newly added Sri Ayyappan Temple, both also in Paris proper, as part of a cluster of Tamil Shaiva shrines serving the city's South Indian and Sri Lankan Tamil Hindu community.
