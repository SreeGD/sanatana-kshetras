ISKCON's Osaka Center serves Japan's Hare Krishna community in the country's second-largest metropolitan area, alongside the movement's better-known Tokyo center (ISKCON New Gaya, already documented separately in this directory). Both are named together in Wikipedia's survey of Hinduism in Japan as the movement's two organized centers in the country, part of the wider Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition serving Japan's Nepali, Indian, and Sri Lankan migrant communities under the international Founder-Acharya A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada.

No founding date, exact address, or further descriptive detail could be confirmed from the source checked in this pass; this entry documents the confirmed existence and city of the Osaka center pending more specific sourcing.
