Laudium, a historically Indian residential suburb of Pretoria established under apartheid-era Group Areas Act zoning, is home to a large number of Hindu temples and mosques serving its Gujarati and Tamil communities. Among these, Wikipedia's article on the suburb identifies the Shree Pretoria Hindu Seva Samaj, on Jewel Street, as Laudium's oldest temple.

Beyond its name, street location, and status as the area's oldest temple, no dedicated source describing this institution's founding date, presiding deity, architecture, or day-to-day worship life was located during this research pass; this entry should be treated as a starting point for future verification rather than a fully documented record.
