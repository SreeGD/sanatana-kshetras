Standing on a hilltop in the Tumba Muerto district of Panama City, the Panama Hindu Temple — operated by the Hindustani Society of Panama and also known locally as the Templo Hindu de Tumba Muerto — is described by Wikipedia as one of only two Hindu temples in the entire country. This entry represents this directory's first confirmed temple in Panama, home to a small Hindu community of South Asian descent. Sourcing for this specific site is thinner than ideal — Wikipedia's own citation is a personal travel blog post rather than an institutional or press account — so its founding history, founders, and presiding deity remain unconfirmed and should be treated as provisional pending fuller verification. Wikipedia separately names a "Krishna Radha Temple Society" among Panama's Hindu associations, a plausible but unconfirmed candidate for the country's unnamed second temple.
