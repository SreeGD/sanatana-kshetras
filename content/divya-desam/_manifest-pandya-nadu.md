Coordination manifest for the Pandya Nadu region group of the 108 Divya
Desams (the temples of southern Tamil Nadu historically ruled by the
Pandya dynasty: the Nava Tirupathi cluster along the Thamirabarani river
in Tirunelveli/Thoothukudi districts, plus temples spread across
Virudhunagar, Madurai, Sivaganga, Ramanathapuram, and Pudukkottai
districts). Final `seq` numbers (1-108), `locked: true`, and individual
`content/divya-desam/*.md` files are assigned centrally by the reviewer
merging all region-group manifests; this file is not itself a Divya Desam
entry and does not conform to `divya-desam.schema.json`.

Nine of the eighteen entries above (Adhinathar Temple through Vanamamalai
Perumal Temple / Thiruccheeravaramangai) form the traditional
"Nava Tirupathi" sub-group, each identified with one of the nine
classical grahas (planets) and glorified together by Nammalvar in
Thiruvaimozhi.

`vaishnava-nambi-temple-thirukkurungudi` (Thirukkurungudi) sits at the
foothills of the Western Ghats near the Kerala border, which makes it a
plausible point of confusion with the separate 13-temple Malai Nadu
(Chera Nadu) group; no source consulted classifies it under Malai Nadu,
and it is administratively in Tirunelveli district, but see its file's
`review_flags` for a note on this boundary. `sathyamurthy-perumal-temple-thirumeyyam`
(Thirumeyyam, Pudukkottai district) similarly sits at the eastern edge of
the traditional Pandya Nadu cluster and has a 9th-century Pallava-era
(not Pandya-era) founding inscription, though dedicated Divya Desam
sources consistently group it under Pandya Nadu; see its file's
`review_flags` as well.
