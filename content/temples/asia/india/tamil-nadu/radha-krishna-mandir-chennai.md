One of ISKCON's centers in South India, the Radha Krishna Mandir in Chennai serves the city's Gaudiya Vaishnava congregation alongside regular public programs of kirtan, scripture classes, and prasadam (sanctified food) distribution characteristic of ISKCON centers worldwide.
