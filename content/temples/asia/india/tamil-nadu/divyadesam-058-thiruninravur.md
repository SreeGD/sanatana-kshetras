At Thiruninravur in the ancient Thondai Nadu region stands the Bhaktavatsala Perumal Temple, counted among the 108 Divya Desams sung in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham of the Alwars. The presiding deity, the Moolavar, is revered here as Bhaktavatsala Perumal, the Lord whose very name proclaims His tender affection for His devotees, drawn as a calf is drawn to its mother. The Lord is enshrined in a standing posture, and the place-name Thiruninravur itself, meaning 'the sacred town where the Lord stands,' echoes this form of grace. Tradition holds that the Lord lingers here in compassion, ever near to those who seek refuge, and the Mother is venerated alongside Him as the embodiment of intercessory grace. The glory of this kshetra was sung by Thirumangai Alwar, the warrior-poet among the twelve Alwars, whose hymns secured its place in the sacred canon of the Divya Prabandham and brought its sanctity to the wider community of Srivaishnavas. Pilgrims journeying among the Divya Desams of the Tamil land have long included Thiruninravur in their circuit, drawing near to a Lord who, according to the sthala purana, abides here chiefly to demonstrate His boundless love for His bhaktas. The temple thus stands as a living testament to the central Srivaishnava conviction that the Lord delights in His devotees and remains forever accessible to them, a truth that the name Bhaktavatsala enshrines in stone and song alike.

## Festivals

The temple at Thiruninravur traditionally follows the Srivaishnava festival cycle centred on an annual Brahmotsavam, observed over several days with the procession of the utsava deity through the streets on a succession of vahanas including the Garuda and Hanumantha mounts, culminating in the ceremonial Ther (chariot) journey. Vaikunta Ekadasi in the Tamil month of Margazhi is kept with great devotion, the Lord granting darshan through the symbolic gateway of Paramapadam. The recitation of the Nalayira Divya Prabandham during the Adhyayana Utsavam, the annual float festival (Theppotsavam) on the temple tank, and the consecratory Pavithrotsavam are also observed in keeping with established temple custom, alongside the cycle of monthly and Tamil New Year observances.

## Location & Access

Bhaktavatsala Perumal Temple stands in the town of Thiruninravur in the Thondai Nadu region, within Tiruvallur district to the west of Chennai and well served by the suburban rail corridor that links the area to the city. The temple lies close to the local railway station and is readily reached from Chennai, the nearest major transport hub. Like most Vishnu temples it generally observes morning and evening darshan hours, remaining closed during the midday interval. Verify current darshan timings and travel details with the temple or official sources before visiting.
