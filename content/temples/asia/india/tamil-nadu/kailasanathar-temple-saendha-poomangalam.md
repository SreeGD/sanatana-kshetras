Serndhapoomangalam (recorded elsewhere, including in the Wikipedia Nava Kailasam group table, as Saendha Poomangalam), a village about 2 km east of Authoor in Thoothukudi (Tuticorin) district, is the ninth and final temple of the Nava Kailasam group and is associated with Venus (Sukran) among the Navagrahas in that group's tradition — a distinct tradition from the separate Navagraha temple circuit near Kumbakonam further north in Tamil Nadu.

The presiding deity is worshipped here as Kailasanathar (Arulmigu Kailasanathar), with his consort worshipped as Arulmigu Soundhara Nayaki; the temple's sacred tank is Thamira Pushkarani and its sacred tree Vilvam (bael). Local devotional belief holds that worship here is equivalent in merit to worshipping Shiva at Kanchanoor near Thanjavur. Tradition holds that the nine Nava Kailasam sites trace to the sage Agastya's disciple Uromacha Munivar, instructed to set nine flowers afloat on the Thamirabarani river and to install a Shiva lingam wherever each one came to rest; the disciple is said to have attained liberation only after venerating all nine of these shrines and bathing where the river meets the sea, making this ninth and final temple the traditional completion of the pilgrimage circuit.

## Festivals

The temple's festival calendar includes monthly Pradosham, Maha Shivrathri (February-March), and Aipasi Annabishekam (October-November).

## Location & Access

The temple stands about 2 km east of Authoor, over 20 km from Thoothukudi on the route toward Tiruchendur, in Thoothukudi district. Bus services run from Thoothukudi and Tiruchendur, with the nearest railway stations at Tiruchendur and Thoothukudi, and the nearest airport at Thoothukudi; accommodation is available in both towns. The temple observes darshan from about 7:30 AM to 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM to 7:30 PM. It is administered by an Executive Officer registered at Serndhapoomangalam - 628 151, Tuticorin district. Verify current darshan timings and travel details with the temple or official sources before visiting.
