Agneeswarar Temple, in Kanjanur village on the northern bank of the Kaveri river some 18 km northeast of Kumbakonam, is the Navagraha shrine associated with Shukra, or Venus, within Tamil Nadu's nine-temple planetary circuit centered on the Cauvery delta. As at most of the other Navagraha sites, Shiva is the temple's principal deity, worshipped here as Agneeswarar, with the Shukra shrine maintained as a subsidiary sanctum drawing devotees seeking favorable influence over prosperity, relationships, and material well-being, the domains traditionally associated with Venus in Hindu astrology.

Little temple-specific historical detail could be confirmed during verification: general sources describe most of the Navagraha temples other than Suryanar Kovil as older Shivalayas, roughly datable to the 7th-9th centuries, that separately acquired their planetary association over time, but no source consulted gave a dynasty, founding century, or origin legend specific to Kanjanur itself. This entry is therefore presented as provisional, confirmed only in its location and its role within the wider Navagraha pilgrimage tradition, pending further independent sourcing on the temple's own history.
