Serving the central New Jersey Hindu community since 2004, ISKCON of Central New Jersey operates the Sri Sri Radha Shyamsundar Temple and Cultural Center in Plainfield. The temple worships three deity groups — Sri Sri Radha Shyamsundar, Sri Sri Jagannath Baladev Subhadra Devi, and Sri Sri Gaura Nitai — and offers a regular Sunday Feast Program alongside cultural events, a children's program, and a vegetarian restaurant (Govinda's) on site, distinct from the much larger BAPS Akshardham temple elsewhere in the state.
