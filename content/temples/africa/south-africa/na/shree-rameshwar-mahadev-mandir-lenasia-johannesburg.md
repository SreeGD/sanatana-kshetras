Built by Lenasia's Hindu community in 1988, this temple is dedicated to Shiva under the Rameshwar Mahadev name — the same epithet associated with the Rameshwaram Jyotirlinga in Tamil Nadu, reflecting the South Indian Tamil heritage of much of the area's Hindu population. Lenasia was established southwest of central Johannesburg under apartheid-era group areas legislation as a designated area for Indian residents, and its temples, including this one, have since served as anchors of religious and community life for Johannesburg's Indian-origin Hindus. The temple continues to host regular worship, festivals, and charitable activities for the local community.
