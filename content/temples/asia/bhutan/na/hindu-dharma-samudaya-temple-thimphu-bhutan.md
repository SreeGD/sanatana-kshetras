The Hindu Dharma Samudaya Temple in Kuensel Phodrang, Thimphu, is associated with the Hindu Dharma Samudaya, a Hindu religious organization established in 2009 and formally registered with the Chhoedey Lhentshog, Bhutan's Commission for Religious Organizations. It serves Bhutan's Hindu community, concentrated historically in the country's southern districts among ethnic Nepali (Lhotshampa) residents, though this Thimphu temple extends that community's presence into the capital itself.

Bhutan's government authorized the construction and renovation of Hindu temples beginning in the early 1990s, part of a broader, gradual formalization of religious accommodation for the country's Hindu minority within a predominantly Vajrayana Buddhist state. Bhutan is also home to at least one other notable Hindu religious site, the Bhairabkunda Shiva Mandir, described in some sources as the only Shakti Peetha associated with Bhutan, though detailed, independently verifiable information about that site was not available for this entry.
