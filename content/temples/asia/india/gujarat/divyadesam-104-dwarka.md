Kalyana Narayanar Temple at Dwarka (Thiruthuvarakai) is one of the 108 Divya Desams — the sacred abodes of Vishnu glorified in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. It is sung in mangalasasanam by 5 Alvars: Thirumazhisai Alwar, Nammalwar, Periyalwar, Andal, Thirumangai Alwar. It belongs to the Vada Nadu (Northern Region) group of Divya Desams.
