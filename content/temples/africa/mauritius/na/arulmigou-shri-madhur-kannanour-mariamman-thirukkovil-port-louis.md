Named in Wikipedia's overview of temples dedicated to the goddess Mariamman worldwide, the Arulmigou Shri Madhur Kannanour Mariamman Thirukkovil is a Tamil Hindu temple in Port Louis, Mauritius's capital, dedicated to Mariamman - the South Indian Devi widely venerated as a protector against disease and as a guardian goddess of Tamil diaspora communities across the Indian Ocean and beyond.

It stands distinct from the already-documented Sockalingum Meenatchee Ammen Kovil (the "Kaylasson Temple") also located in Port Louis, reflecting the presence of more than one South Indian Devi-tradition temple in the city centre. Beyond its name and general Port Louis location, no dedicated source describing this temple's founding, precise address, or current worship activities was located during this research pass; it is documented here only via a single-line mention in a general Wikipedia article on the Mariamman tradition.
