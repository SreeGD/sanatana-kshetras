ISKCON of Houston operates a Hare Krishna temple on W 34th Street, offering daily deity worship, a program built around kirtan and meditation, teaching from the Bhagavad Gita, and cultural festivals with vegetarian cuisine. Alongside temple worship, the community runs affiliated projects including a vegetarian restaurant (Govinda's), an academy, and an urban farm. It observes the standard Gaudiya Vaishnava festival calendar, including Janmashtami and Dussehra.
