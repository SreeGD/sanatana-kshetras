Thiruayodhi, the sacred Ayodhya of the north, is venerated as a Divya Desam where the Lord is worshipped as Raghunayagan, the chief of the Raghu line, that is, Sri Rama himself. As the celebrated birthplace and royal city of Rama, the seventh avatara of Vishnu, Ayodhya holds a place of supreme honour in the Vaishnava imagination, and it is counted among the 108 Divya Desams sung in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. According to the Ramayana and the tradition flowing from it, this was the capital from which the righteous Lord ruled, the city that grieved his exile to the forest and rejoiced at his return, and the seat of the ideal kingdom known as Rama Rajya. The Alwars sang the glory of the Lord of Ayodhya with deep devotion, and this northern abode was praised in the Divya Prabandham by Nammalwar, Kulasekara Alwar, Periyalwar, Thondaradippodi Alwar, and Thirumangai Alwar, a remarkable assembly of saints whose verses bind the city of Rama into the sacred geography of the Tamil canon. Kulasekara Alwar in particular, himself of royal lineage, poured out his longing for the Lord of the Raghus. As one of the few Divya Desams lying far to the north and as the very cradle of the Rama avatara, Thiruayodhi stands as a hallowed abode where devotees adore Raghunayagan, the embodiment of dharma and the protector of all who take refuge in him.

## Festivals

As the celebrated birthplace of Sri Rama, Ayodhya observes a devotional calendar centred on the Lord's life. Sri Rama Navami, marking the birth of Rama in the month of Chaitra, is the foremost festival, drawing vast gatherings for elaborate worship, processions and the recitation of the Ramayana. Vijayadasami, recalling Rama's victory, and Deepavali, commemorating his triumphant return to Ayodhya after exile, are kept with great splendour and the lighting of countless lamps along the Sarayu river. In keeping with the Srivaishnava tradition that venerates Raghunayagan among the Divya Desams, devotees also observe Vaikunta Ekadasi and the chanting of the Tamil Prabandham. Annual Brahmotsavam-style processions, ceremonial bathing of the deity, and the festive recitation of Rama's glories mark the principal occasions of the year.

## Location & Access

The temple is at Ayodhya (Thiruayodhi) on the banks of the Sarayu river in Uttar Pradesh, in the far northern region honoured among the Divya Desams. Ayodhya lies close to Faizabad and is connected by rail and road, with Lucknow serving as the nearest major city and air hub. Like most Vishnu temples, worship is generally offered during morning and evening hours, with timings often extended on major festival days.
