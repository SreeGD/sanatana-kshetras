ISKCON Brisbane operates the Sri Sri Gaura Nitai Temple at 8 Lee Road in the Darra suburb of southwest Brisbane, part of the worldwide International Society for Krishna Consciousness founded by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. By its own account, "Sri Sri Gaura Nitai have been worshipped at ISKCON Brisbane for almost 50 years," placing the congregation's roots at roughly the mid-1970s, though its exact founding date and any earlier premises are not documented in sources consulted this pass. The temple centers its worship on Sri Sri Gaura Nitai — the paired deity forms of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu (regarded in Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition as a combined incarnation of Krishna and Radha) and his companion Nityananda (identified with Balarama) — reflecting the Bengali bhakti lineage from which ISKCON descends.

Its current Darra building, a former church purchased for $1.7 million (financed with a $1.4 million loan) and rezoned as a place of worship, opened on Saturday 28 February 2026 during Gaura Purnima week — timed, per the temple, to its "Golden Jubilee Year." The center runs an active weekly program built around a Saturday evening feast at 5pm, featuring kirtan (devotional chanting), a spiritual class, evening aarti (lamp ceremony), and a vegetarian prasadam dinner served 6-7pm, alongside daily Srimad Bhagavatam study classes (held both via Zoom and in person on weekend mornings) and Friday evening Harinam Sankirtan (public chanting processions) from King George Square in the Brisbane CBD, complementing the affiliated Govinda's vegetarian and vegan restaurant it runs in the Brisbane CBD.
