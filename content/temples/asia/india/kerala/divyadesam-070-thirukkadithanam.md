The Adbhuta Narayanan Temple at Thirukkadithanam (Thrikodithanam), in the Malai Nadu (Kerala) country, enshrines the Lord Vishnu as Adbhuta Narayanan — documented locally as Athpudha Narayanan or Amirutha Narayanan — the "Wonderful One," standing six feet tall in Vishvarupa (cosmic-form) pose facing east, with his consort worshipped as Karpagavalli. The temple is counted among the 108 Divya Desams revered in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham, its glory sung by Nammalwar, foremost of the Alwars, and by Thirumangai Alwar. It is also one of five documented shrines along the Pamba river in Kerala's Kottayam-Alappuzha-Pathanamthitta region traditionally credited to the five Pandava brothers: temple legend holds that after crowning Parikshit king and setting out on pilgrimage, the brothers each established one of these shrines, with Thirukkadithanam specifically attributed to Sahadeva, the youngest.

Built in Kerala temple style, the shrine has a two-storey gopuram, a rectangular enclosing wall, a metal-plated flagpost (dvajasthambam), and a separate light-post (Deepastamba); its circular sanctum, raised on a granite base with a laterite superstructure, is capped by a conical terracotta-tiled roof typical of the region.

## Festivals

As one of the Malai Nadu Divya Desams revered in the Nalayira Divya Prabandham, Thirukkadithanam traditionally follows the Srivaishnava festival cycle observed across the Kerala region, including Vaikunta Ekadasi and the recitation of the Tamil Prabandham. A monthly Sravana Deepam light festival is also documented, and worship here traditionally includes performances in the Kutiyattam dance-drama tradition. As with other Kerala Vaishnava shrines, the local festival calendar and Onam-season observances blend with this Srivaishnava heritage.

## Location & Access

The Adbhuta Narayanan temple stands at Thirukkadithanam in Kottayam district, Kerala, among the Pamba-river Pandava shrines, near Changanassery, which has the nearest railway station; Cochin International Airport is the nearest airport. Darshan is generally offered from about 4 AM to 11 AM and 5 PM to 8 PM. Verify current darshan timings and travel details with the temple or official sources before visiting.
