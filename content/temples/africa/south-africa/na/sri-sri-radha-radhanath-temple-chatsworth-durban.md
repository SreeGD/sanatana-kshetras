A center of Gaudiya Vaishnava (Hare Krishna) worship serving South Africa's large Indian-origin community, itself descended largely from 19th-century indentured laborers (see the separate Sri Siva Soobramaniar Temple entry in this directory for that broader community history). ISKCON established a presence in South Africa in the 1970s-80s, and Durban's Ratha Yatra festival, organized by the local ISKCON community, has grown into one of the city's notable annual public events.
