Kailash Mandir stands on Temple Street in Belmont — Port of Spain's oldest suburb, settled at the foot of the Laventille Hills in the city's north-east — making it, so far as documented in this directory, the only entry located within the Trinidadian capital itself. Its name, invoking Mount Kailash, the Himalayan abode of Shiva in Hindu cosmology, suggests a likely association with Shiva worship, though this was not independently confirmed.

Like many of Trinidad's neighborhood mandirs, it most likely serves as a local congregational temple for Port of Spain's Indo-Trinidadian Hindu residents, consistent with the pattern of Sanatan Dharma worship documented elsewhere in this directory's Trinidad and Tobago entries. No founding date, founders, or specific presiding deity could be confirmed for this temple in this pass, and it should not be assumed to share institutional history with any other Kailash-named temple elsewhere.
