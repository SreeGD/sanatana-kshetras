The Arulmihu Sivan Temple was established in 1994 as a non-profit foundation by the Saiva Tamil Sangam, one of two Hindu temples founded that same year to serve Switzerland's growing community of Sri Lankan Tamil refugees (the other being the Sri Sivasubramaniar Temple in nearby Adliswil). It occupies a converted warehouse in the industrial quarter between Glattbrugg and Seebach, close to Zürich Airport, and is dedicated to Shiva. The Sangam describes its founding purpose as providing for the spiritual and cultural care of Tamil people in Switzerland and preserving Tamil culture within the diaspora, and the temple holds pujas twice daily.

Beyond its role as a place of worship, the temple has occasionally served as a venue for wider civic and interfaith engagement: in March 2018, Zürich's Neumarkt Theatre staged a performance inside the temple involving participants from Muslim, Hindu, Jewish, Orthodox, Free Church, and atheist communities, reflecting the temple's place within Zürich's broader interreligious landscape alongside its core role serving the Tamil community.
