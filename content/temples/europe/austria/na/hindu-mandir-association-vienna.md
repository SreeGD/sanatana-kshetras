Founded in 1980 by Dr. Bimal Kundu, an engineer originally from Bengal, the Hindu Mandir Association began as informal gatherings and was formalized as an organization in 1990 - the first organized Hindu religious body established in Vienna, initially operating out of the city's Afro-Asian Institute. Its umbrella body, registered under Austrian law as the Hinduistische Religionsgesellschaft in Österreich (HRÖ), gained recognition as a religious community by the late 1990s. Described by Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in Austria as "one of the oldest organisations in Vienna," it has long played what the same source calls "a major role in presenting Hinduism to the public" in the Austrian capital, operating a mandir (temple) alongside its public-facing educational and cultural activities.

Today the association operates from Lammgasse 1 in Vienna's 8th district (Josefstadt), holding Sunday services from 15:00 to 20:00 plus additional festival-day observances, per HRÖ's own current congregation listing. It remains one of twelve HRÖ-affiliated Hindu congregations across Austria, alongside other Gaudiya Vaishnava and Sanatana Dharma organizations in and around Vienna. Beyond this institutional history, the sources available for this entry do not describe the mandir's building or its specific deity images in detail, leaving some aspects of Austria's only confirmed Hindu temple entry in this directory still pending fuller documentation directly from the association.
