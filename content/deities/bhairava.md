Bhairava is a fierce protective form of Shiva particularly significant in Kashi (Varanasi), where Kal Bhairav is traditionally regarded as the city's guardian, and across the Ashta Bhairava (eight-Bhairava) circuit and various Himalayan and Nepali shrines.
