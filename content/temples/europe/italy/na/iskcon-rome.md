ISKCON's (International Society for Krishna Consciousness) first foothold in Italy was Rome, where the movement arrived in 1973 and opened its first Italian center — the country's earliest organized Hare Krishna presence, predating both the Matha Gitananda Ashram in Altare (founded 1984) and Villa Vrindavana near Florence, both already documented elsewhere in this directory. The following year, in 1974, the movement's founder, A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, personally visited Rome and delivered a public lecture there, an early milestone in the movement's spread across Italy.

Beyond this founding date and Prabhupada's 1974 visit, the sources available for this entry give no further detail on the Rome center's exact address, its continuity as an institution over the following decades, or its current facilities; fuller documentation would need to be confirmed directly with ISKCON Italy.
