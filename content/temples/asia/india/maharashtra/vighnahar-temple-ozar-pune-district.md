Tradition holds that Ganesha here defeated the demon Vighnasura, who had been disrupting the sacrificial rites (yajnas) of sages; before being vanquished, the demon is said to have requested that his name be invoked whenever Ganesha himself was worshipped, giving rise to the widely used epithet 'Vighnaharta' ('remover of obstacles'). The temple, distinguished by a gold-plated dome, has historically been enclosed within moat-like fortification walls, and — like several of the Ashtavinayak shrines — received patronage during the Peshwa era.
