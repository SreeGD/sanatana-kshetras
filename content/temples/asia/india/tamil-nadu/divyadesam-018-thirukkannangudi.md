The Lokanatha Perumal Temple at Thirukkannangudi, set in the Chola Nadu region of the sacred Tamil land, is counted among the 108 Divya Desams sung by the Alwars in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. The presiding deity, the Moolavar, is worshipped here as Lokanatha Perumal, the Lord of all the worlds, a name that proclaims His sovereignty as the supreme protector and master of the universe. The place-name Thirukkannangudi, the holy abode of the dark-hued Lord, points to His serene and gracious presence in this kshetra, where devotees gather to take refuge at His feet. Tradition reveres this shrine as a place where the Lord, though the very ruler of all creation, makes Himself accessible to the humble bhakta who seeks Him. Among the Alwar saints, it is Thirumangai Alwar, whose paasurams illumine so many of the Divya Desams of the Chola country, who sang the glory of Thirukkannangudi and thereby enshrined it within the four thousand sacred Tamil verses of the Prabandham. Through his hymns the sanctity, the form, and the compassion of Lokanatha Perumal were made part of the eternal liturgy of the Srivaishnava tradition, drawing generations of pilgrims to recite those verses before the Lord. Standing in the venerable Chola heartland near the Kaveri delta, the temple continues to be cherished as a Divya Desam where the Lord of all the worlds bestows His grace upon those who worship Him, faithful to the devotion first voiced by the Alwar who sang its praise.

## Festivals

At Thirukkannangudi, the shrine of Lord Lokanatha Perumal follows the festival pattern cherished across the Srivaishnava Divya Desams. The annual Brahmotsavam is the foremost observance, when the utsava deity is borne in procession on Garuda and the other traditional vahanas amid Prabandham recitation. Vaikunta Ekadasi is kept with special devotion, the Lord granting darshan during the sacred days when the Tiruvaymoli and Tamil hymns are chanted. Pavithrotsavam, the rite of annual sanctification, and a Float festival (Theppotsavam) on the temple tank are traditionally held. The Tamil month observances, Pongal, and the tirunakshatram days of the Alvars who hallowed this Chola Nadu kshetra are also marked with reverence.

## Location & Access

The Lokanatha Perumal Temple lies at Thirukkannangudi in the Chola Nadu region of the Cauvery delta, in Nagapattinam district of Tamil Nadu. It is situated not far from Nagapattinam, the nearest major town and transport hub, while Tiruvarur and Thanjavur serve the broader region by road and rail.
