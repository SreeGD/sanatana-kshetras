The Vaikunta Perumal Temple at Thiruparameswara Vinnagaram, in Kanchipuram of Thondai Nadu, is revered as one of the 108 Divya Desams sung by the Alwars. The very name Vinnagaram signifies a celestial city or a temple of the heavens, and the Lord here is worshipped as Vaikunta Perumal, the master of Sri Vaikuntam, evoking for the devotee a foretaste of the Supreme Abode upon earth. The presiding Moolavar grants darshan in this sanctuary as a vision of the Lord who reigns in glory, and the shrine is held among the cluster of sacred abodes that make Kanchipuram a city dense with Divya Desams. Tradition holds this temple to be an ancient and storied foundation, and it is well known for the sculptured panels of its enclosure that narrate sacred lore for the contemplation of the faithful, though pilgrims come above all to adore the Lord in His several aspects within the shrine. The kshetra was hallowed by hymn through Thirumangai Alwar, the most prolific of the twelve Alwars, whose pasurams in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham extol this Vinnagaram and confirm its place within the canonical company of the Divya Desams. By the grace of the Alwar's song, Thiruparameswara Vinnagaram is cherished in the unbroken Srivaishnava tradition, and devotees making the round of the Divya Desams of Thondai Nadu come here to behold Vaikunta Perumal and to meditate upon the bliss of the heavenly city the Lord embodies.

## Festivals

The Vaikunta Perumal temple at Thiruparameswara Vinnagaram in Kanchipuram traditionally observes the Srivaishnava annual festival cycle. The yearly Brahmotsavam, marked by colourful processions of the utsava deity on successive mounts including the Garuda vahana, is the chief celebration. Vaikunta Ekadasi in Margazhi is kept with special reverence, accompanied by the Adhyayana Utsavam during which the Nalayira Divya Prabandham is recited before the Lord. Pavithrotsavam is performed to sanctify the worship of the year, and Pongal, Tamil New Year, Sri Jayanthi and Rama Navami are observed. The festival days commemorating the Azhwars and Acharyas are also honoured here, in harmony with the devotional traditions of the ancient temple city of Kanchipuram.

## Location & Access

The Vaikunta Perumal temple lies in Kanchipuram, in the Thondai Nadu region of northern Tamil Nadu, close to the famed Kamakshi and Kailasanathar shrines of the temple town. Kanchipuram is well linked by road and rail, with Chennai serving as the nearest major city and transport hub. The temple generally observes morning and evening darshan hours like most Vishnu temples, closing for a period at midday. It is conveniently visited alongside the other Kanchipuram Divya Desams.
