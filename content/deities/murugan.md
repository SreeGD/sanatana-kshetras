Murugan (also Kartikeya or Skanda), son of Shiva and commander of the divine army, is especially central to Tamil Shaiva devotion via the Arupadaiveedu ('six abodes') circuit, and is one of the most widely represented deities in the Tamil diaspora's temples across Malaysia, Singapore, South Africa, Mauritius, and beyond.
