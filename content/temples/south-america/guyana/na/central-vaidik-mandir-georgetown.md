The Central Vaidik Mandir is featured as a notable landmark of Georgetown, Guyana's capital, appearing independently in both Wikipedia's general "Hinduism in Guyana" article and in the "Points of interest" section of Wikipedia's own article on the city of Georgetown — placing it alongside colonial-era landmarks such as St. George's Cathedral and Stabroek Market as a recognizable feature of the capital's built environment. It stands at Premniranjan Place in the Bel Air area of the city. Its name ("Vaidik," from the Sanskrit for "Vedic") suggests a connection to Vedic ritual tradition, though this could not be confirmed from available sources this pass, and no founding date, presiding deity, or institutional history was found.
