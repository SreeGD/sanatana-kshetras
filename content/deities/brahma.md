Brahma, the creator deity of the Trimurti, has remarkably few dedicated temples relative to his cosmological stature — a consequence of various Puranic curse-legends explaining his near-absence from active worship. Pushkar's Brahma Temple is by far the most famous; Khedbrahma (Gujarat) is a rarer, older sister site.
