Hamburg holds a founding place in German ISKCON history: German Wikipedia's account of the Hare Krishna movement states that the first Hare Krishna temple in Germany was established in the city, though its two relevant articles disagree on whether this happened in 1969 or 1970. A currently-active center, named the "ISKCON-Tempel im Bhakti-Yoga-Zentrum," continues to operate today in Hamburg's Harburg district, appearing as a distinct entry (separate from the rural ISKCON farm community at Jandelsbrunn/Simhachalam elsewhere in Germany) in German Wikipedia's own actively-maintained directory of Hindu temples in Germany, and Hamburg is separately named among the German-speaking region's current ISKCON centres alongside Munich, Basel, and Vienna.

Beyond this founding significance and current listing, further detail proved difficult to confirm in this research pass: no official temple website could be reached, and no source gives a precise street address, founder's name, or the specific deities worshipped at this centre. Its approximate location, in the Phoenix-Viertel area of Harburg, rests on a reverse-geocode of Wikipedia's own map coordinate for the listing rather than an independently confirmed address, and should be treated as approximate pending further documentation.
