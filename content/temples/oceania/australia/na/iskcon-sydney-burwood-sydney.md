A center of Gaudiya Vaishnava practice serving Sydney's Hare Krishna community since the early 1970s, not long after ISKCON's first Australian temple was established in the country. The temple hosts regular public programs and an annual Ratha Yatra chariot festival in the city, part of the same public-kirtan tradition ISKCON introduced in New York and London in the movement's earliest years.
