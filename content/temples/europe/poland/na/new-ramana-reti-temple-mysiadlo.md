Founded in 1989, nine years after the country's first ISKCON community was established at New Shantipur in Czarnów (already documented elsewhere in this directory), the New Ramana Reti Temple, at ul. Zakręt 11 in the village of Mysiadło just south of Warsaw, is described by ISKCON Poland's own site as the organization's main Warsaw temple — reflecting Warsaw's importance as the country's capital and largest population center for the movement's continued growth beyond its original rural Silesian base.

As at other ISKCON temples founded during this period, including its sister community New Navadvip in Wrocław, worship at New Ramana Reti centers on the Pancha Tattva in the paired form of Sri Sri Gaura Nitai — Chaitanya Mahaprabhu and his close associate Nityananda — central to Gaudiya Vaishnava devotional practice descending from the missionary work of A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. Its annual festival calendar includes Radhastami, Srila Prabhupada's Vyasa Puja, Janmastami, Nrisimha Caturdasi, Rama Navami, Nityananda Trayodasi, Gaura Purnima, and Balarama Purnima, and the temple maintains an on-site shop selling religious books, Indian clothing, spices, and music. Named founders and detailed building history remain unconfirmed in the sources available for this entry.
