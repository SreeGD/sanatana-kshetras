Opened in 2024 in the Taylor suburb of Canberra's Gungahlin district, this BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir is one of ten BAPS temples across Australia and the newest addition to the Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha's national network, which also includes mandirs in Melbourne, Sydney, and elsewhere. Like other BAPS mandirs, it follows the Akshar-Purushottam theology within the Swaminarayan Vaishnava tradition, centering worship on Bhagwan Swaminarayan.

Built over roughly 18 months, the mandir's intricately carved architectural components were manufactured in India from glass and fibre-reinforced concrete and shipped to Australia in about 48 containers, then assembled on-site by volunteers "like a jigsaw puzzle" following scripture-inspired plans — a construction method characteristic of BAPS mandirs worldwide. Its shrine room houses eleven deities, and the complex additionally includes a dining hall, commercial kitchen, and a 500-person capacity meeting room beneath an exterior painted pink to symbolize peace and harmony. Notably, the entire project was funded and physically built by BAPS volunteers with no government or outside organizational funding — reported fundraising examples include local teenagers who raised over $26,000 through baking and craft sales toward the temple's abhishek room.
