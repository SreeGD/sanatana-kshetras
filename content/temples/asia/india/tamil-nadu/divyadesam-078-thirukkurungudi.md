The Azhagiya Nambi Temple at Thirukkurungudi, in the Pandya Nadu country at the foot of the Western Ghats, is one of the most celebrated of the 108 Divya Desams. The presiding deity, the Moolavar, is Azhagiya Nambi, 'the beautiful and perfect Lord,' worshipped here in a unique tradition that venerates Vishnu in three forms across the locality, his beauty and accessibility drawing the devotion of saints and seekers alike. According to the sthala purana, this is a place hallowed by the Lord's intimate presence, and tradition holds it in special esteem as a shrine where the Supreme reveals himself with peculiar grace and approachability. The greatness of Thirukkurungudi is sung by no fewer than four of the Alwar saints in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham: Thirumazhisai Alwar, Nammalwar the foremost of the Alwars, Periyalwar the father-in-law of Andal, and Thirumangai Alwar the prolific singer of the Divya Desams. Their combined hymns mark this abode as one richly extolled within the four thousand verses, a distinction shared by only the most beloved of the sacred shrines. The Lord here is affectionately known as the one who is ever 'beautiful,' and the temple's traditions speak of his delight in the company of his devotees, including the legend in which he himself is said to have received instruction in the path of surrender. Set amid the verdant hills of the Pandya land, Thirukkurungudi remains a great centre of Srivaishnava pilgrimage, where the grace of Azhagiya Nambi is sought as the embodiment of the Lord's perfect and approachable beauty.

## Festivals

Thirukkurungudi, the abode of Azhagiya Nambi, is renowned for a rich and well-attested festival tradition in the Pandya country. Its grand annual Brahmotsavam features splendid processions of the Lord on successive vahanas around the temple and village, with Prabandham recitation and large crowds of pilgrims. Vaikunta Ekadasi in Margazhi is solemnly observed with the Pagal-pathu and Ra-pathu recitations and the opening of the Paramapada Vasal, drawing devotees for the auspicious darshan of the Lord. The temple is especially celebrated for its dance and ritual heritage, including the Vaikasi festival and traditional Araiyar Sevai. Pavithrotsavam, a Float festival on the temple tank, Krishna Janmashtami and Sri Rama Navami are also traditionally kept, making the temple's year a continuous round of devotion.
