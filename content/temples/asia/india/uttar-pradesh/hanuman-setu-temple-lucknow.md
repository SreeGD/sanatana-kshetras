Hanuman Setu Temple, opposite the University of Lucknow, is among the most visited Hindu temples in the Uttar Pradesh capital, dedicated to Hanuman. While independently documented founding history for the temple itself is limited, its civic significance is well attested through its central role in Lucknow's Bada Mangal tradition: on every Tuesday of the Hindu month of Jyeshtha (roughly May/June), devotees queue around the clock for darshan and prasad, and the city as a whole marks the season with free community meals, or bhandaras, now distributed from an estimated 3,000 food stalls citywide.

That scale of communal feasting, drawing volunteers and diners across Hindu, Muslim, Sikh, and Christian communities alike, has made Bada Mangal one of Lucknow's defining annual traditions, and Hanuman Setu Temple one of its principal focal points alongside the city's other prominent Hanuman shrines — a reflection of the temple's place in Lucknow's civic and devotional life even where detailed architectural or founding history remains undocumented in accessible sources.
