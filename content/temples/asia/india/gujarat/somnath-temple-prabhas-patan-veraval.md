Traditionally the first among the twelve Jyotirlingas, Somnath on Gujarat's Saurashtra coast has an unusually well-documented history of repeated destruction and reconstruction: sacked by Mahmud of Ghazni in 1026 CE and rebuilt several times over the centuries, its present structure was reconstructed after 1947 at the initiative of Indian statesman Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, with the temple consecrated in 1951.
