The Sanatan Dharma Mandir on Hagley Park Road in Kingston is described by Wikipedia as Jamaica's only government-recognized Hindu temple, founded by Pandit Munaeshwar Maragh in the mid-1970s. It serves as the central site for Hindu religious life on the island, hosting all of the major Hindu festivals observed by Jamaica's Indo-Jamaican community — descendants of Indian indentured laborers who began arriving in 1845, in a wave of migration comparable to that which shaped Trinidad's and Guyana's Hindu communities.

As Jamaica's sole officially recognized Hindu temple, the mandir carries outsized institutional significance for the island's small but historic Hindu population relative to its counterparts in the larger Indo-Caribbean communities of Trinidad and Guyana. Its exact founding date and the specific deities enshrined were not fully confirmed from available sources in this pass and should be treated as approximate pending fuller documentation.
