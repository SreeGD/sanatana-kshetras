Basukinath, in Jharkhand's Dumka district within the Santhal Pargana region, is a Shiva temple complex of roughly 30 shrines built in the Nagara style, its main sanctum topped by a pyramid-shaped shikhara. The deity here is worshipped as Basukinath, and the temple sits about 45 km from Deoghar, the site of the Baidyanath Jyotirlinga.

The temple's chief significance today comes from its place within the larger Deoghar pilgrimage circuit: devotees (Kanwariyas) who carry Ganges water from Sultanganj to offer at Baba Baidyanath Dham during the monsoon month of Shravan customarily continue on to Basukinath to complete their pilgrimage there as well, making the two shrines a linked two-temple circuit during the Shravani Mela. Unlike Baidyanath, however, no founding date, founder, or origin legend for Basukinath surfaced in the source consulted for this entry, which is markedly thinner than the sourcing available for its better-documented neighbor; this entry is written accordingly as a description of the temple's present form and pilgrimage role rather than any claim about its age or origin.
