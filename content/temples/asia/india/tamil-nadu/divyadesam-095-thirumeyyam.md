The Sathyamoorthy Perumal Temple at Thirumeyyam, in the Pandya Nadu region near Pudukkottai, enshrines the presiding Lord as Sathyamoorthy (Satyagiri Natha), and stands among the 108 Divya Desams celebrated in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. The shrine is renowned for its setting amid a great rock, and the Lord is worshipped here in a majestic reclining posture upon the serpent Adisesha, a form that draws pilgrims to behold the cosmic repose of Vishnu carved into the living hill. The place name itself, associated with truth (satya/mey), reflects the tradition that this is the abode of the Lord who is Truth embodied, Sathyamoorthy. The temple's glory was hymned by Thirumangai Alwar, whose verses in the Divya Prabandham include this shrine among the sacred abodes of the Pandya country, securing its place in the canon sung by the Alwars. Set within and around its rock-cut sanctity, with the Lord reposing upon the serpent-couch, Thirumeyyam offers devotees the vision of Vishnu in his serene, all-sustaining form, and its enduring renown as a Divya Desam rests upon the grace of Sathyamoorthy and the Tamil hymns that consecrated it for the faithful.
