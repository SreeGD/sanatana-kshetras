Nagaland is overwhelmingly Christian, and Hindu temple presence in the state is limited and comparatively recent, concentrated mainly in Dimapur, its main commercial and transport town near the Assam border. The Dimapur Kalibari, a temple dedicated to the goddess Kali, was built in 1956 and marked its golden jubilee in 2006, making it one of the state's few sustained centers of organized Hindu worship, serving the Bengali, Marwari, Nepali and other Hindu communities settled in and around Dimapur.

Unlike many of the older temples in this directory, the Kalibari's history is straightforward and modern rather than legendary: no myth of divine origin or ancient founding is attached to it in available sources, and it functions as a conventional Kali temple observing the standard Shakta festival calendar, most prominently Durga Puja and Kali Puja each autumn. The specific individual or community credited with establishing the temple in 1956 is not documented in the sources checked for this entry.
