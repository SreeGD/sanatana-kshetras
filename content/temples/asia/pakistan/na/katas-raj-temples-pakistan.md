A complex of ancient Hindu temples built around a sacred pond that tradition holds formed from Shiva's tears of grief after the death of his consort Sati, Katas Raj is associated in some traditions with episodes of the Mahabharata (the Pandavas' exile) as well. Largely abandoned by its Hindu community after the 1947 Partition of India, when most Hindus left the region, the site has undergone restoration in recent decades with support from the Pakistani government and Hindu community groups, and now sees occasional organized Hindu pilgrimage from within Pakistan and abroad alongside its status as a heritage site.
