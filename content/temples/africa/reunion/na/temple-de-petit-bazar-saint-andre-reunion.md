Located on Avenue de l'Île de France in the Petit Bazar district of Saint-André, on Réunion's northeast coast, the Temple de Petit Bazar is described by French Wikipedia as one of the commune's tourist attractions. It is a Tamil Hindu temple within the same broader tradition of temple-building by the descendants of Réunion's 19th-century indentured Indian labourers documented at other sites across the island.

The temple is explicitly distinct, per its source article, from the nearby Temple Mardévirin - which is also informally known by the same "Temple de Petit Bazar" name locally, creating a naming overlap the source is careful to disambiguate. No presiding deity, construction date, or heritage/protection status was found for this temple during this research pass.
