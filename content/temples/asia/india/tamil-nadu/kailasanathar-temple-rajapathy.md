Rajapathy, a village in Thoothukudi (Tuticorin) district along the Thamirabarani river corridor, is the eighth of the nine Nava Kailasam temples and is associated with the lunar node Kethu (Ketu) among the "planets" in that group's tradition — a distinct tradition from the separate Navagraha temple circuit near Kumbakonam further north in Tamil Nadu.

The presiding deity is worshipped here as Kailasanathar, in keeping with the naming shared by most temples in the group. Tradition holds that the nine Nava Kailasam sites trace to the sage Agastya's disciple Uromacha Munivar, instructed to set nine flowers afloat on the Thamirabarani river and to install a Shiva lingam wherever each one came to rest, attaining liberation after venerating all nine and bathing where the river meets the sea.
