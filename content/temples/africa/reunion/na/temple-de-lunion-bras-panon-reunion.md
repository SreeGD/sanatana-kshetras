Registered as a French historic monument on 17 September 2010, the Temple de l'Union stands in the commune of Bras-Panon on Réunion's northeast coast - a Tamil ("malbar") Hindu temple within the same wave of 19th- and 20th-century temple-building by the descendants of indentured Indian labourers documented across the island. It is the only currently documented Hindu temple entry for Bras-Panon in this directory.

French Wikipedia's stub article on the temple gives no presiding deity, construction date, or founder; its heritage listing and general location are the only confirmed details available from this research pass.
