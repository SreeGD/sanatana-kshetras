Bhubaneswar's largest and most important temple, Lingaraj enshrines Harihara, a combined form of Shiva and Vishnu, reflecting Odisha's historical syncretism of the two traditions. The present structure dates substantially to the 11th century CE under the Someshvara dynasty, with later additions, and its 55-meter deul (tower) is a landmark of Kalinga-style temple architecture.
