Set in a cave shrine in the Trikuta hills, Vaishno Devi is among the most visited pilgrimage sites in India, drawing millions of devotees annually. The goddess is worshipped here in the form of three natural rock formations (pindies) representing Mahakali, Mahalakshmi, and Mahasaraswati, and the shrine's popularity has grown enormously since organized pilgrim infrastructure was developed from the mid-20th century onward under the Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board.
