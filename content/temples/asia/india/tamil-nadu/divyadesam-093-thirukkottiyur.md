Thirukkottiyur (Thirukoshtiyur), in the Pandya country, enshrines the Lord as Sowmya Narayana Perumal, the serene and gracious form of Narayana, with his consort worshipped as Thirumaamagal Thayar, and ranks among the 108 Divya Desams sung by the Alwars in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. The sanctum is built in a distinctive three-tiered form said to represent Bhulokam (the earth), Tirupparkatal (the Milk Ocean) and Vaikuntham (the highest heaven) stacked as three storeys: Krishna is shown dancing at the ground level, the Lord reclines upon Adishesha at the middle tier, and the standing form of Sowmya Narayana Perumal presides at the top. Built in Dravidian style across roughly two acres within granite walls, the temple's five-tiered gopuram is, unusually for a Dravidian temple, exceeded in height by its own Ashtanga Vimana, which rises some 25 metres (82 feet). Periyalvar, in his Periyalvar Tirumoli, envisions Thirukkottiyur as the birthplace of Krishna, a tradition that lends the kshetra a further layer of sanctity alongside its Divya Desam status. Thirukkottiyur holds a singular place in the later history of Srivaishnavism as the abode associated with the teacher Thirukkottiyur Nambi (Nambigal), from whom the great acharya Ramanuja is traditionally said to have received the Ashtakshara mantra (the Tirumantra) only after seventeen entreaties; when Nambigal forbade its wider disclosure, Ramanuja climbed the temple and revealed the mantra publicly to all people regardless of caste, reasoning that the many souls it could liberate justified whatever personal consequence he might bear -- for which Nambigal honoured him with the title "Emperumanar." This living memory lends the kshetra a profound importance in the sampradaya beyond its Prabandham glory. The shrine is hymned by the early Mudhal Alwars Bhoothath Alwar and Pey Alwar, by Thirumazhisai Alwar, by Periyalwar, and by Thirumangai Alwar, whose verses together establish its standing within the divine corpus.

## Festivals

Thirukkottiyur, hallowed in Srivaishnava tradition as the place where Acharya Ramanuja received and openly shared the sacred Ashtakshara mantra, observes a float festival in the Tamil month of Masi (February-March), Navaratri (September-October), and Vaikunta Ekadasi during Margazhi (December-January) with special darshan. The annual Brahmotsavam is also traditionally kept, with the Lord Sowmya Narayana taken in procession on the customary vahanas amid Divya Prabandham recitation, alongside Pavithrotsavam. Days associated with Ramanuja and the great acharyas hold particular significance here given the temple's role in the disciplic tradition.

## Location & Access

Sowmya Narayana Perumal Temple lies at Thirukoshtiyur (Thirukkottiyur), about 9 km from Tirupathur, in the Sivaganga district of the Pandya Nadu region. Sivaganga and Karaikudi are the closer towns served by road and rail, with Madurai the nearest major city and transport hub. Buses and taxis reach Thirukkottiyur from these centres.
