Matsya, Vishnu's first avatar, took the form of a fish to warn the sage Manu of an impending great flood and guide his boat to safety, preserving the Vedas and the seeds of all life. Dedicated, standalone Matsya temples are extremely rare in the living tradition compared to the other avatars.
