A rural ISKCON farm community modeled on the same self-sufficient, agrarian ideal as New Vrindaban in the United States (also covered in this directory), Nova Gokula ("New Gokula," named for Krishna's childhood village) has served as a hub of Gaudiya Vaishnava practice in Brazil, hosting festivals, a gaushala (cow sanctuary), and educational programs for South America's Hare Krishna community since the 1970s.
