Bahrain's Hindu community, one of the oldest and most well-established in the Arabian Gulf, is served by an existing BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir in Manama, part of the worldwide network of temples maintained by the Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha (BAPS). It operates alongside the historic 1817 Shrinathji Temple (Bahrain's oldest Hindu temple, documented separately in this directory) and an ISKCON-affiliated temple community, together reflecting a long-tolerated and religiously diverse Hindu presence in the country.

In February 2022, Bahrain's King Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa granted additional land for the construction of a second, considerably larger BAPS Swaminarayan temple in the country, explicitly modeled on BAPS's large traditionally stone-carved temple completed in Abu Dhabi. No source consulted in this research pass could confirm that this larger project has since been completed or formally inaugurated; it should be understood as an announced, land-granted project distinct from — and much larger than — the existing mandir this entry documents.
