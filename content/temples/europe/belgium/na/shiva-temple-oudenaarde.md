The Shiva Temple Belgium, in the East Flanders town of Oudenaarde, held its soft opening ceremony on 10 September 2020, under the auspices of a Sanatana Dharma organisation, making it the most recently established of the Hindu temples identified in this directory's Belgium coverage. It is dedicated to Shiva, filling a Shaiva-tradition gap alongside the Shakta-oriented Brussels Mandir (Evere, soft-opened 2012) and the ISKCON Gaudiya Vaishnava community of Radhadesh (Château de Petit-Somme, Durbuy), both already documented elsewhere in this directory.

Beyond its founding date, town, and Shaiva dedication, further detail on the temple's exact address, founders, architecture, and current congregation could not be confirmed in the source available for this entry; fuller documentation would need to be confirmed directly with the temple or its sponsoring organisation.
