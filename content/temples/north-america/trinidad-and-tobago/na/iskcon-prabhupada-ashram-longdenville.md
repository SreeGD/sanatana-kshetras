An ISKCON (Hare Krishna movement) centre on Edinburgh Road in Longdenville, within the Chaguanas district of central Trinidad — an area with one of the island's densest concentrations of Indo-Trinidadian Hindu residents, descended from nineteenth-century indentured laborers. As with ISKCON centers worldwide, it is presumed to center on Radha-Krishna devotion in the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition founded by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, whose name the ashram bears. This specific location's founding date, founders, and institutional history beyond its listing in a community-compiled Trinidadian temple directory could not be independently confirmed this pass.
