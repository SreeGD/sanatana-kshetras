Serving Northern Virginia's Hindu community, the Durga Temple of Virginia was established as a religious organization in 1991 and, after a Bhoomi Pooja groundbreaking in October 1996 financed by a $2.5 million loan from First Virginia Bank, was completed and consecrated on March 21, 1999. Unlike many American temples that house multiple deities under one roof, it is dedicated specifically to the Goddess Durga, making it one of relatively few single-deity Shakta temples in the greater Washington, D.C. area alongside temples such as the nearby Sri Siva Vishnu Temple in Maryland, which take a more ecumenical, multi-shrine approach. By 2010 the temple was serving an estimated 6,500 Hindus in Fairfax County, and beyond regular worship it has hosted a "Hinduism Summit" educational event and offers Indian cultural and spiritual classes along with a Boy Scouts program for the community's youth.
