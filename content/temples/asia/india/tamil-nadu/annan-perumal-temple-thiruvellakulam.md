Among the eleven Divya Desams of Thirunangur, this shrine holds a particularly affectionate place in local devotion. Its presiding deity, Annan Perumal - "Elder Brother Perumal" - together with the festival deity Srinivasa Perumal, is regarded by devotees as the elder brother of Srinivasa of Tirupati, and a long-standing local belief holds that those unable to make the arduous journey to Tirumala can come here instead, to Annan Koil, and have their wishes fulfilled just the same, since the "elder brother" here is understood to carry the same grace as the deity of the seven hills.

The presiding goddess is worshipped as Alarmel Mangai Nachiyar, with Padmavathi Thayar as the festival consort - names that echo, once again, the Tirupati parallel that gives this temple its local character. Thirumangai Alvar's hymns in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham secure the temple's place among the 108 Divya Desams, and, like its ten neighbouring Thirunangur shrines, the temple joins the annual Garudasevai procession during the Tamil month of Thai, when the festival images of all eleven temples are brought together on a Garuda-mounted procession retracing Thirumangai Alvar's own circuit of devotional song through these villages.
