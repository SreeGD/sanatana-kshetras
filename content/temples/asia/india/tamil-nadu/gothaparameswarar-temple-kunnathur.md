Kunnathur, a village in Tirunelveli district along the Thamirabarani river corridor, is the fourth of the nine Nava Kailasam temples and is associated with the lunar node Raghu (Rahu) among the "planets" in that group's tradition — a distinct tradition from the separate Navagraha temple circuit near Kumbakonam further north in Tamil Nadu.

The presiding deity is worshipped here as Kotha Parameswarar (Gothaparameswarar), one of the few names in the Nava Kailasam group that departs from the more common "Kailasanathar" naming used at most of the other eight sites. As with the wider group, tradition holds that the nine temples trace to the sage Agastya's disciple Uromacha Munivar, instructed to set nine flowers afloat on the Thamirabarani and to install a Shiva lingam wherever each one came to rest, attaining liberation after venerating all nine.
