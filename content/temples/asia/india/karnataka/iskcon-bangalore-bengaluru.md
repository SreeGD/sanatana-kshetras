Inaugurated in May 1997 by Shankar Dayal Sharma, then President of India, ISKCON Bangalore is among the largest Krishna temples in the world, notable for a gold-plated flag post (17 m) and spire (8.5 m). The temple complex also houses the Akshaya Patra Foundation, a mid-day-meal program for schoolchildren founded by temple members that has received international recognition for its scale and impact.
