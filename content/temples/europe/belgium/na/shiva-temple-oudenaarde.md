The Shiva Temple Belgium, in the East Flanders town of Oudenaarde, held its soft opening ceremony on 10 September 2020, under the auspices of a Sanatana Dharma organisation, making it one of the more recently established Hindu temples identified in this directory's Belgium coverage alongside the Brussels Mandir (Evere, 2012) and Radhadesh (Château de Petite Somme, Durbuy). It was dedicated to Shiva, filling a Shaiva-tradition gap alongside the Shakta-oriented Brussels Mandir and the ISKCON Gaudiya Vaishnava community of Radhadesh, both already documented elsewhere in this directory.

The temple's sponsoring organisation, Shiva Forum Europe, confirms on its own current website that the Oudenaarde temple ceased independent operations on 1 October 2024. As of February 2025, a much smaller "mini" version of the temple has been folded into the organisation's offices in Eine, a district of Oudenaarde, led by Swami Bhairavananda Saraswati as Executive Director. Beyond this closure and relocation, further detail on the original temple's exact address, founders, architecture, and current congregation could not be confirmed in the sources available for this entry; fuller documentation would need to be confirmed directly with Shiva Forum Europe.
