The Krishna Temple in the Darsait area of Muscat is, alongside the Motishwar Mandir (Shiva Temple) in nearby Muttrah, one of only two officially recognized Hindu temples in Oman's capital, together serving an Omani Hindu community — drawn primarily from long-settled Indian merchant families and the broader Indian expatriate population — estimated at roughly 260,000 people as of 2020.

Little detailed history about this specific temple's founding or architecture is available from the sources checked in this pass; what is clear is that it functions as the Vaishnava counterpart to the Muttrah Shiva temple within Muscat's small but long-tolerated Hindu religious infrastructure, giving the city's Hindu community active places of worship dedicated to both Shiva and Krishna.
