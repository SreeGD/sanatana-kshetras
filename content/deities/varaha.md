Varaha, Vishnu's third avatar, took the form of a boar to rescue the earth (personified as the goddess Bhudevi) after a demon dragged it to the bottom of the cosmic ocean. Dedicated Varaha temples are rare; several sites combine Varaha with Narasimha veneration in a shared complex.
