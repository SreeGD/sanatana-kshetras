ISKCON established a temple in Barcelona in 1976, making it, per Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in Spain, "one of the first minority religions" to organize formally in the country during the years immediately following the end of the Franco dictatorship (1975) and Spain's subsequent transition to religious pluralism. This predates by roughly two decades the founding of New Vraja Mandala in Brihuega, Guadalajara — the ISKCON rural community already documented elsewhere in this directory — making Barcelona's temple the older, urban anchor of ISKCON's presence in Spain.

Today the Barcelona temple offers Bhagavad Gita study classes and other spiritual programs to the city's Gaudiya Vaishnava community, part of a wider Spanish ISKCON network that also includes vegetarian restaurants and additional agricultural communities such as one at Churriana Malanga. Beyond its 1976 founding date and ongoing program offerings, the source available for this entry gives no further detail on the temple's exact address, founders, or building; fuller documentation would need to be confirmed directly with ISKCON Barcelona.
