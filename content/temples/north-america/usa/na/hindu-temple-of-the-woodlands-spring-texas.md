Founded in 2005 when its organizing community purchased land along Woodlands Parkway in Spring, Texas, the Hindu Temple of The Woodlands filled a real gap in Greater Houston: before it existed, Hindu families in the northern part of the metro area had to drive roughly 90 minutes to reach established temples in Pearland or Sugar Land. After a 2008 Bhumi Puja groundbreaking, the 10,000-square-foot, roughly $3-million temple on an 8-acre site was completed in 2011, with its first consecration phase enshrining Venkateswara, Shiva, and Ganesha; a second phase in 2012 added shrines to Maha Lakshmi and Radha Krishna.

Beyond regular worship, the temple has built out community programming — free yoga classes, heritage-language instruction, and public Holi celebrations among them — and by 2018 its prayer and meditation gatherings were drawing 100-200 participants regularly, prompting parking expansion to keep pace with a growing congregation across The Woodlands, Conroe, Tomball, and northern Harris County.
