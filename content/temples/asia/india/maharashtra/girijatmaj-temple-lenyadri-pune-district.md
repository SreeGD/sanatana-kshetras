Unusual among the Ashtavinayak shrines for being a rock-cut cave temple — Cave No. 8 of the ancient Lenyadri cave complex, which also includes a number of earlier Buddhist caves — rather than a free-standing structure, and for being reached only by climbing a long flight of roughly 300 stone steps. The name 'Girijatmaj' ('son of Girija,' an epithet of Parvati) reflects the local tradition that Parvati performed penance at this site to be blessed with Ganesha as her son. Unlike most Ganesha shrines, the cave's Ganesha image is carved directly into the rock face rather than installed as a separate idol, and no ritual immersion is performed.
