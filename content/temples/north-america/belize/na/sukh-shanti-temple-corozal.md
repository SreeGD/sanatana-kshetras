The Sukh Shanti Temple in Corozal Town, in Belize's northernmost district near the Mexican border, is one of only two named Hindu temples documented for the country of Belize (alongside the Belize Hindu Temple in Belize City), per Wikipedia's "Religion in Belize" article. Its name, "Sukh Shanti" ("peace and happiness"), serves a small Hindu community — roughly 0.2% of Belize's population per the 2010 census — composed almost entirely of Sindhi and other Indo-Pakistani families who arrived in Belize as British colonial subjects in the 1950s.

Major festivals observed nationally include Diwali and Janmashtami. No street address, founding date, founder, or specific presiding deity could be confirmed for this temple in this pass, and this entry should be treated as provisional pending fuller sourcing.
