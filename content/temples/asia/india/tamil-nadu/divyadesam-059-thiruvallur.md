The Veera Raghava Swamy Temple at Thiruvallur in Thondai Nadu is one of the 108 Divya Desams glorified in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. Here the Lord is worshipped as Veera Raghava, a form associated with Rama of the Raghu line, and He is famed especially as the divine physician (Vaithya Veeraraghava), to whom devotees turn for healing of bodily and spiritual ills. Tradition holds that the Lord here removes the afflictions of those who seek Him, and the temple is renowned across the region as a place of cure and consolation; offerings made in supplication for health are a long-standing devotional practice at this shrine. The Lord is enshrined in a reclining posture, and the Mother is venerated beside Him as the bestower of grace. The sanctity of Thiruvallur was sung by two of the Alwars, Thirumazhisai Alwar and Thirumangai Alwar, whose hymns wove this kshetra into the sacred tapestry of the Divya Prabandham. Thirumazhisai Alwar, himself closely associated with the surrounding Thondai country, gives the place a special intimacy, while Thirumangai Alwar's verses added to its renown among pilgrims. According to the sthala purana, the Lord abides here as the great healer who, like a skilled physician, diagnoses and dispels the deepest maladies of samsara. Thus Thiruvallur is cherished not only as a station on the Divya Desam pilgrimage but as a refuge of mercy, where the reclining Veera Raghava grants both worldly relief and the ultimate cure of liberation to those who take shelter at His feet.

## Festivals

At Thiruvallur the principal annual observance is the grand Brahmotsavam, traditionally celebrated over many days with the utsava murti of Veera Raghava Perumal taken in procession on the customary vahanas and on the great temple chariot, drawing large gatherings of devotees. Vaikunta Ekadasi in Margazhi is solemnly kept, with the Adhyayana Utsavam and the recitation of the Nalayira Divya Prabandham, and the Lord passing through the Paramapada Vasal. The temple's renown as a place of healing lends special devotion to its theerthavari and tank observances. The float festival (Theppotsavam) on the temple tank, the Pavithrotsavam, and the regular cycle of monthly Ekadasi and Tamil festival days are also observed according to long-standing tradition.

## Location & Access

Veera Raghava Swamy Temple is located in Thiruvallur town, the headquarters of Tiruvallur district in the Thondai Nadu region, to the west of Chennai. The town has its own railway station on the suburban line and is connected by road to Chennai, the nearest major city and transport hub. As with most Vishnu temples, darshan is generally available during morning and evening hours, with a closure through the middle of the day.
