    description: "Goloka Vrindavana is described, principally within the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition founded by Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu (drawing on the Brahma Samhita and Bhagavata Purana), as Krishna's own eternal abode — distinguished from Vaikuntha in this tradition as the highest spiritual realm, where Krishna is worshipped not primarily in his majestic, opulent form (as Narayana is in Vaikuntha) but in the intimate, playful relationships of Vraja: as the cowherd boy among the gopas and gopis of Vrindavana, with Radha as his eternal consort. Other Vaishnava traditions (e.g., Sri Vaishnavism) do not draw this same distinction and generally regard Krishna's abode as encompassed within Vaikuntha itself rather than as a separate, higher realm — a genuine point of difference between Vaishnava sub-traditions presented here on its own terms rather than resolved."
