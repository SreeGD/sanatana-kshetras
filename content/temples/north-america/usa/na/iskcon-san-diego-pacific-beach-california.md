ISKCON of San Diego has operated in the Pacific Beach neighborhood since 1977, offering a full daily cycle of deity worship (arati) from before dawn through evening. Open to the public year-round, the temple observes major festivals including Govardhan Puja, Snana Yatra, and the San Diego Ratha Yatra, and holds an annual remembrance of ISKCON founder A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada.
