The temple of Purushothaman at Thirukkarambanur, popularly known as Uttamar Koil, in the Chola country near the confluence of the Kaveri, is reckoned among the 108 Divya Desams sung by the Alwars. The presiding deity, the Moolavar, is worshipped as Purushothama, the Supreme Person, a title of Vishnu signifying the highest among beings; from this exalted name the shrine derives its familiar appellation of Uttamar Koil, the temple of the foremost one. The temple is unusual among the Divya Desams for sheltering, within a single granite-walled precinct, dedicated shrines to the full Hindu Trimurti and their consorts: Purushothaman with Poornavalli (Lakshmi), Bhikshadanar (Shiva) with Soundarya Parvati, and Brahma with Saraswati. Tradition, per legend recorded separately from the temple's documented history, credits the site's founding to King Janaka. The temple takes its place in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham through the hymns of Thirumangai Alwar, who sang the glory of so many abodes of the Lord across the Chola land and by whose verses Thirukkarambanur is sanctified among the Divya Desams.

Documented history places the temple's traditional founding with the Medieval Cholas in the late 8th century CE, with further contributions from the Vijayanagara kings and the Madurai Nayaks. The central shrine's roof is known as the Udhyoga Vimana, and the reclining (Bhujangasayanam) image of the Lord is stylistically comparable to the celebrated form at nearby Srirangam. The temple tank is named Kathamba Theertham.

## Festivals

Thirukkarambanur's shared dedication to Vishnu, Shiva, and Brahma shapes its most distinctive festival: the annual Brahmotsavam in the Tamil month of Kartikai (November/December), during which festival images of both Purushothaman and Bhikshadanar (Shiva) are carried in joint procession -- an unusual observance reflecting the temple's rare multi-deity plan. The Vaikunta Ekadasi season, with recitation of the Nalayira Divya Prabandham, and the customary Pavithrotsavam and monthly observances common to Perumal temples, are also kept here.

## Location & Access

The temple stands at Thirukkarambanur (Uttamar Koil), a short distance from Srirangam and Tiruchirappalli (Trichy) in the Chola Nadu region of Tamil Nadu. Trichy, with its railway junction, bus services, and airport, is the nearest major transport hub, and the temple is readily reached from the city or from nearby Srirangam. It is administered by the Tamil Nadu Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowments (HR&CE) Board. Like most Vishnu temples, it generally observes morning and evening darshan hours with a midday break. Verify current darshan timings and travel details with the temple or official sources before visiting.
