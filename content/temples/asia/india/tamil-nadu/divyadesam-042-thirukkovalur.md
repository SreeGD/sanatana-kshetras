The temple at Thirukkovalur, honoured as Adhi Thiruvarangam, enshrines the Lord as Trivikraman, with the sanctum also revered under the name Aranganathar, and it is counted among the 108 Divya Desams celebrated by the Alwars in the central Nadu Nadu region. The presiding Moolavar stands in the majestic form of Trivikrama, recalling the cosmic stride of the Lord who, as Vamana, measured the worlds with his feet, and the kshetra is held to be one of supreme significance in the Srivaishnava tradition. Thirukkovalur is famed above all as the sacred place where, according to tradition, the first three Alwars—Poigai Alwar, Bhoothath Alwar, and Peyalwar—came together in a narrow passage during a stormy night and, drawn close in the press of darkness, perceived the presence of the Lord himself among them; from this meeting flowed the very first hymns of divine longing that opened the great outpouring of the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. Poigai Alwar and Bhoothath Alwar sang the glory of this Divya Desam, and Thirumangai Alwar too added his verses to its praise, so that the place is woven inseparably into the origins of the Prabandham corpus. As the abode bearing the title Adhi Thiruvarangam, the original or foremost Srirangam, Thirukkovalur holds a cherished place in devotional memory. Pilgrims revere it as the cradle of the Alwar tradition, where the Lord Trivikraman, who once spanned the universe, remains present to grant his grace to those who seek refuge in him.

## Festivals

At Thirukkovalur, revered as Adhi Thiruvarangam, the Lord Trivikraman (Aranganathar) is celebrated with the traditional Srivaishnava festivals. An annual Brahmotsavam is observed with processions of the deity on the various vahanas. This sacred place is especially renowned in the Mudhal Azhwars' tradition, for it was here that Poigai, Bhutam and Pei Azhwar are said to have first encountered one another and the Lord in the narrow passage during a stormy night, and this episode is recalled with particular devotion. Vaikunta Ekadasi in Margazhi is kept with the recitation of the Nalayira Divya Prabandham, alongside Pavithrotsavam and, where customary, a Float festival. The annual recitation of the Tamil Prabandham through Margazhi is a cherished observance at this ancient shrine.

## Location & Access

Thirukkovalur stands on the banks of the Then Pennai (South Pennar) river in the Villupuram district of Tamil Nadu, within the Nadu Nadu region. It is reached by road from Villupuram, the nearest major town and railway junction, which connects onward to Chennai, Tiruchirappalli and beyond.
