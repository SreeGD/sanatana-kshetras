The ISKCON Balramdesh Temple in Manama serves Bahrain's Hare Krishna community as part of the worldwide Gaudiya Vaishnava movement founded by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. It operates alongside Bahrain's historic 1817 Shrinathji Temple and an established BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir (both documented separately in this directory), reflecting the diversity of Vaishnava devotional communities long present in the country.

Specific founding-date and architectural details for this temple were not available from the sources checked in this research pass; it is documented here on the strength of a named, specific reference in a general survey of Hinduism in Bahrain, distinguishing it from this directory's more thinly sourced "ISKCON presence confirmed, specific building unconfirmed" entries elsewhere in the region.
