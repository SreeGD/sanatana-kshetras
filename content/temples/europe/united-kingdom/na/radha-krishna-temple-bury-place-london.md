Founded in 1968, this was the first ISKCON temple established in Europe, opened not long after the movement's 1966 founding in New York. Its early community became closely associated with the 1969 recording "Hare Krishna Mantra," produced by George Harrison and performed by London devotees, which reached the UK singles chart top ten — an early, high-profile introduction of Krishna consciousness to a wide Western public. The London temple predates the 1973 founding of Bhaktivedanta Manor (also covered in this directory), which became the UK's larger suburban center while this original city-center temple continued as an active congregation.
