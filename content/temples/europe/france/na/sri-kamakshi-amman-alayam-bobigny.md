The Sri Kamakshi Amman Alayam stands on Avenue de la Division Leclerc in Bobigny, a northeastern Paris suburb in the Seine-Saint-Denis department that, along with neighboring La Courneuve, has become home to one of France's densest concentrations of Sri Lankan Tamil Hindu temples. It is dedicated to Kamakshi, a form of the goddess Parvati/Shakti whose principal shrine is at Kanchipuram in Tamil Nadu — the same goddess venerated under her Sri Lankan Tamil name, Kamadchi, at the much larger Sri Kamadchi Ampal Temple in Hamm, Germany, already documented elsewhere in this directory.

Bobigny's status as a prefecture town of Seine-Saint-Denis gives this temple a civic as well as religious significance for the department's substantial South Asian Tamil population.
