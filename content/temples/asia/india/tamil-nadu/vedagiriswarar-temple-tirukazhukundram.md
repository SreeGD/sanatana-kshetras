The Vedagiriswarar Temple at Tirukalukundram, in Chengalpattu district near Mahabalipuram, is a Paadal Petra Sthalam glorified by Appar, Sundarar, and Thirugnanasambandar, with further hymns from Manikkavasagar and Arunagirinathar. Shiva is worshipped here as Vedhagireeswarar, alongside his consort Thirupurasundari Amman, across a hill-top shrine reached by a climb above a foothill temple whose four gopurams are noted for architectural similarity to those of the Annamalaiyar Temple at Tiruvannamalai, already covered elsewhere in this directory.

The temple's best-known tradition, long documented in regional and colonial-era accounts, concerns a pair of birds — popularly identified as Egyptian vultures — reported to visit the hill temple just before noon each day to be fed by the temple's priests; local legend holds them to be eight sages, cursed by Shiva to periodically take vulture form. The reliability of the daily visits in recent times is not something this entry can independently confirm.
