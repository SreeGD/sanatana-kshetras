position_in_cosmology: "The seven lower lokas (Adho Loka), beneath Bhuloka (Earth): Atala, Vitala, Sutala, Talatala, Mahatala, Rasatala, and Patala"
tradition_accounts:
  - tradition: vaishnava
    description: "The Puranas describe seven subterranean realms beneath the earth, collectively called the Patalas, said to be more opulent and pleasurable than the heavens in some respects, and populated chiefly by Nagas (serpent-beings), Daityas, and Danavas (categories of powerful beings often in tension with the Devas). In order from uppermost to lowest they are: Atala, ruled by the demon Bala with mystic powers of illusion; Vitala, associated with Hara-Bhava (a form of Shiva) and treasures of gold; Sutala, home to the pious king Bali, granted this realm by Vishnu in his Vamana avatar; Talatala, ruled by the architect-demon Maya; Mahatala, inhabited by many-hooded Naga serpents; Rasatala, home to the Danavas and Daityas; and Patala (or Naga-loka) itself, the lowest, ruled by the serpent-king Vasuki and adorned with brilliant jewels."
