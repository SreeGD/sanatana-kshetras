The Udhvaganathar Temple at Thirumanancheri, a Kaveri-side village in Mayiladuthurai district, is a Paadal Petra Sthalam glorified by both Thirugnanasambandar and Appar. Shiva is worshipped here as Udhvaganathar, and also, more popularly, as Kalyanasundareswarar — "the beautiful lord of marriage" — alongside his consort Kokhila. That name reflects the temple's long-standing popular association as a place to seek blessings for marriage, arranged or otherwise, though the specific legend behind the association was not independently confirmed in the source consulted here.

The temple sits alongside a related shrine in the same town, Edirkolpadi (also known as Melai Thirumanajeri or the Iyravatheswarar Temple), underscoring Thirumanancheri's standing as a small but doubly-significant node in the Paadal Petra Sthalam network along the Kaveri.
