Established in 1990 as the main US center of Jagadguru Kripalu Parishat (JKP), Radha Madhav Dham — also known as Barsana Dham — sits on more than 200 rural acres in Hays County south of Austin, built as a devotional replica of the sacred Braj region of North India, with representations of Govardhan hill, Radha Kund, and Prem Sarovar on the grounds. Its centerpiece, the Shree Raseshwari Radha Rani Temple, is a 35,000-square-foot structure with a 90-foot golden dome, white and blue granite construction, and 84 columns across five levels, blending North Indian, South Indian, and modern architectural elements. The complex is promoted by its own organization as the oldest Hindu temple in Texas and the largest in North America — claims reported here as the institution's own, rather than independently confirmed facts.

The temple draws roughly 1,000 regularly attending families, with major festivals bringing as many as 8,000 visitors to the site. Its founding organization, JKP, was led for decades by Prakashanand Saraswati, who was convicted in 2011 on child sex abuse charges; JKP publicly distanced itself from him afterward. That history is noted here for completeness and is not further adjudicated by this entry.
