The Navagraha are the nine graha (planetary/celestial) deities of Vedic astrology, worshipped both as an integrated set — as in Tamil Nadu's canonical nine-temple Navagraha circuit around Kumbakonam — and individually at specific shrines dedicated to a single planetary deity.
