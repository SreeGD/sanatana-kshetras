The temple at Thirumanimaadakkoil enshrines Nara Narayanar, worshipped here as Badri Narayanar, and is counted among the 108 Divya Desams celebrated in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. The presiding deity recalls the sacred form of Nara and Narayana, the twin sages who, according to the puranic tradition, undertook great penance for the welfare of the world; in this aspect the Lord is venerated as Badri Narayanar, an association that links this Chola-country shrine in spirit to the great northern abode of Badrinath where Vishnu is adored in His tapasvin form. To behold the Lord here as Nara Narayanar is, by tradition, to be reminded of the divine descent that took the form of austerity and compassion for the sake of all beings. Thirumangai Alwar, the foremost among the Alwars in the number of Divya Desams he sang, glorified this kshetra in his pasurams, weaving its sanctity into the eternal corpus of Tamil Vaishnava hymnody. The name Thirumanimaadakkoil, evoking a shrine of jewelled mansions or maadams, points to the architectural splendour for which such temples are remembered, and the place is honoured as one of the abodes where the Lord made Himself accessible to His devotees. Through the verses of Thirumangai Alwar, the faithful continue to recite the glory of Badri Narayanar at this Divya Desam, holding that the worship of the Lord in His sage-like, penance-bearing form bestows grace and draws the surrendered soul toward liberation. The temple endures as a cherished centre of the Sri Vaishnava tradition within the constellation of Chola Nadu Divya Desams.

## Festivals

Belonging to the eleven Nangur Divya Desams, this temple is closely associated with the famed Garuda Sevai, in which the festival deities of the Nangur shrines are brought out together on Garuda vahanas for a magnificent shared procession that gathers large crowds. An annual Brahmotsavam is traditionally observed with daily processions of the utsava murti through the temple precincts. Vaikunta Ekadasi receives special devotion, accompanied by the recitation of the Nalayira Divya Prabandham during the Adhyayana Utsavam season. Pavithrotsavam, performed to sanctify the year's rituals, and a Float festival (Theppotsavam) are also traditionally kept. As a Badri Narayana sthalam, the temple holds a distinct place of reverence within this devotional circuit.

## Location & Access

The temple is located at Thirumanimaadakkoil within the Nangur group of Divya Desams in the Chola Nadu region, in the Nagapattinam district of Tamil Nadu, near the towns of Sirkazhi and Nangur. Travellers usually approach through the road and rail connections around Sirkazhi, Mayiladuthurai and Nagapattinam, with Chennai serving as the nearest large city further afield. As with most Vishnu temples, it generally observes morning and evening darshan hours and closes during the midday period.
