The Hare Krishna movement (ISKCON) maintains an organized presence across Myanmar, with Wikipedia's account of Hinduism in the country identifying Myitkyina — the capital of northern Kachin State, well outside the more commonly Hindu-associated cities of Yangon and Mandalay — as home to the country's largest Hare Krishna community, numbering around 400 followers. The same source records a wider national network of twelve ISKCON centres and an affiliated school in Zayyawadi providing religious education to brahmacharis, part of the broader Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition founded internationally under Founder-Acharya A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada.

No single named temple building, exact address, or founding date for the Myitkyina community could be confirmed from the source checked in this pass; this entry documents the confirmed fact and scale of ISKCON's presence there pending more specific sourcing on individual center names and histories across Myanmar's wider twelve-center network.
