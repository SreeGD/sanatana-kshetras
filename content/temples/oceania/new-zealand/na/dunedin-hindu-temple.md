The Dunedin Hindu Temple Society, active in the city since at least 2019 per its own social media presence, represents New Zealand's southernmost organized Hindu community — Dunedin being the South Island's second-largest city and home to the University of Otago. Prior to 2025, Dunedin had no dedicated Hindu temple building of its own, with local Hindus relying on rented spaces or traveling to temples elsewhere in New Zealand.

Reporting indexed from the Otago Daily Times indicates the society opened Dunedin's first purpose-built Hindu temple around May 2025, dedicated to Radha-Shyamsunder — a form of Krishna paired with Radha in the Gaudiya and broader North Indian Vaishnava devotional tradition. Beyond this, the temple's precise address, architectural style, and regular festival calendar were not independently confirmed in the sources reachable this pass; this entry should be revisited once the founding news coverage or the temple's own website can be directly accessed.
