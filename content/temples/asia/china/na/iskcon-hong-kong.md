ISKCON has maintained an organized presence in Hong Kong since 1981, part of the international Gaudiya Vaishnava movement founded under Founder-Acharya A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. This center operates independently of, though within the same broader Hindu community as, the pan-Hindu Happy Valley temple also documented in this directory, reflecting Hong Kong's wider South Asian devotional landscape.
