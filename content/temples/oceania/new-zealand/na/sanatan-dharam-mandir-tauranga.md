The Sanatan Dharam Mandir serves the Hindu community of Tauranga, on New Zealand's North Island, built through the efforts of local community members in the Tauriko area on the city's outskirts at 108 Whiore Ave. As with many smaller diaspora mandirs outside the country's larger Auckland, Wellington, Hamilton, and Christchurch communities, it appears to function as a shared, pan-Hindu place of worship for Tauranga's Hindu residents rather than being dedicated to a single sampradaya.

Available sources are limited to a brief reference confirming the temple's name, community origin, and address; its exact founding or inauguration date, the deities installed within it, and its managing trust are not confirmed in sources consulted this pass, so this entry is recorded as pending further verification.
