Consecrated in 1976, the Sri Venkateswara Temple in Pittsburgh is widely regarded as the first traditionally-built Hindu temple in the United States and a model for the many that followed, modeled after the Tirumala Venkateswara Temple. It has served as a focal point for the growing Indian-American Hindu community since the 1970s wave of post-1965 immigration.
