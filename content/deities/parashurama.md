Parashurama, Vishnu's sixth avatar, is a warrior-Brahmin sage credited in tradition with reclaiming coastal land (including, per legend, Kerala and Konkan) from the sea, and is associated with sites across India's western coast and a scattering of northeastern sites linked to his other legends.
