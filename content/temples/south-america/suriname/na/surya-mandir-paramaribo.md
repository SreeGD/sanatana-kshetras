Suriname's Surya Mandir — named for Surya, the Vedic sun deity — was established in 1984 by the Surya Mandir Foundation on a plot in Paramaribo previously occupied by a Christian congregation known as LOGOS. Since its founding the building has been repeatedly expanded and modified while remaining in continuous use, reflecting the gradual, community-funded growth typical of many diaspora Hindu institutions in Suriname.

Today the mandir functions both as an active place of worship and as a venue for religious lectures and instruction, serving Paramaribo's Hindustani Hindu community. Beyond its founding year and site history, detailed institutional records (founders, architecture, presiding-deity confirmation) were not available in the sources checked this pass.
