Mayapur is revered as the birthplace region of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu (the 15th-16th century Bengali saint central to Gaudiya Vaishnavism), with the Yogapith temple marking his traditional birthsite established by Bhaktivinoda Thakur in the 1880s. Mayapur is ISKCON's designated world headquarters, home to the existing Sri Mayapur Chandrodaya Mandir and Srila Prabhupada's Pushpa Samadhi Mandir, and the site of the Temple of the Vedic Planetarium (TOVP) — a vast domed temple, under construction since 2009 and planned for completion around 2027, intended on completion to be among the largest religious monuments in the world and chaired by project leader Alfred Brush Ford (Ambarish Das).
