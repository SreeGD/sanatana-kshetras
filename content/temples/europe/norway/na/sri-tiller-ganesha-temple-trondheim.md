The Sri Tiller Ganesha Temple, in the Tiller district of Trondheim, is dedicated to Ganesha and is one of six Hindu temples that Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in Norway identifies as currently active in the country. Its presence in Trondheim — Norway's third-largest city, roughly 500 km north of Oslo — extends this directory's Norway coverage beyond the capital region, where the already-documented Sanatan Mandir Sabha (Slemmestad) and Sivasubramanayar Alayam (Ammerud, Oslo) are both located, showing that organized Hindu worship in Norway is not confined to Oslo alone.

Beyond its name, city, and a photograph confirming the temple's physical existence, the source available for this entry provides no founding date, address, or further history; fuller documentation of the temple's origins and congregation would need to be confirmed directly with the community.
