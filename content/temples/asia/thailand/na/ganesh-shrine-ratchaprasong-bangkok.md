Within the same cluster of Hindu-derived shrines at Bangkok's Ratchaprasong intersection that includes the Erawan Shrine and the so-called Trimurti (Sadasiva) Shrine — both documented separately in this directory — stands a shrine to Ganesha, known locally as Phra Phi Khanet. Like its neighbors, it draws a mix of Thai Buddhist and folk-devotional visitors seeking blessings, reflecting the broader pattern by which Hindu iconography has been absorbed into Thai popular religious practice at this famously shrine-dense central Bangkok intersection, sometimes nicknamed the "Intersection of Gods."
