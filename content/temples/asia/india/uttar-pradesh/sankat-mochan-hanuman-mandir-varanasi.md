Tradition holds that the 16th-century poet-saint Tulsidas — author of the Ramcharitmanas, the most widely read Hindi retelling of the Ramayana — established this temple at the site of a vision of Hanuman, and composed much of his epic here. The temple grew in prominence through the 20th century alongside Varanasi's southward expansion and the founding of Banaras Hindu University nearby. On 7 March 2006 the temple was the site of a bombing during evening prayers, after which security was permanently increased; the adjoining Sankat Mochan Foundation, founded in 1982, has since gained international recognition for its work on Ganges river conservation.
