Perarulalan, the gracious Lord of boundless mercy, presides as the Moolavar of the temple at Thirucchemponsei Koil in the Chola country, counted among the 108 Divya Desams sung in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. The very name of the place, evoking "refined gold" (sem-pon), reflects the tradition that this sacred ground shone with a golden radiance, and the Lord here is praised as Perarulalan, He of the great and overflowing grace. As one of the divine abodes hallowed by the Alwars, the shrine is venerated as a place where the devotee may seek the Lord's compassion and find refuge at His feet. Thirumangai Alwar, the warrior-poet who sang of more Divya Desams than any other Alwar, glorified this kshetra in his verses, securing its place within the eternal canon of Tamil Vaishnava devotion. Through his pasurams the sanctity of Thirucchemponsei Koil is preserved and recited by the faithful, who hold that the Lord's mercy descended here in fullness for the upliftment of those who surrender to Him. In keeping with the character of the Chola Nadu Divya Desams, the temple stands within the cluster of sacred shrines that the Alwars traversed in their pilgrimages of song, and tradition holds that worship offered here, in the presence of the supremely merciful Perarulalan, draws the soul nearer to the Lord. The temple thus continues to be honoured as a living centre of the Sri Vaishnava tradition, its glory rooted in the hymns of Thirumangai Alwar and in the abiding presence of the all-merciful Lord.

## Festivals

As one of the eleven Nangur Divya Desams, this temple shares in the celebrated collective worship of the region. Its most renowned observance is traditionally the Garuda Sevai, when the processional deities of the Nangur temples are carried together on Garuda mounts for a grand joint darshan that draws great gatherings of devotees. An annual Brahmotsavam is traditionally observed with festal processions of the utsava murti. Vaikunta Ekadasi is marked with the special prominence given throughout Srivaishnava temples, and the recitation of the Nalayira Divya Prabandham during Adhyayana Utsavam is held dear. Pavithrotsavam, undertaken for purification of the year's worship, and a Float festival (Theppotsavam) are also traditionally counted among the temple's yearly observances.

## Location & Access

The temple stands at Thirucchemponsei Koil among the Nangur cluster of Divya Desams in the Chola Nadu region, in the Nagapattinam district of Tamil Nadu, reached most conveniently from the nearby towns of Sirkazhi and Nangur. The wider area is served by the rail and road network around Sirkazhi, Mayiladuthurai and Nagapattinam, with Chennai the principal distant city. Like most Vishnu temples it generally observes morning and evening darshan hours, remaining closed through the midday interval.
