The Sanatana Dharma Paripalana Seva Trust of New Zealand, a registered charity, established this temple in 2018 with support from Wellington's Hindu community, and it became fully operational in November 2019 at 25 Waiu Street in Wainuiomata, part of the greater Wellington region. Dedicated to Lord Venkateswara — also known as Balaji, a form of Vishnu traditionally associated with the hill shrine at Tirupati in India — the temple follows South Indian Vaishnava worship conventions, including regular recitation of the Vishnu Sahasranamam (the thousand names of Vishnu), Harathi (lamp-waving arati), and Archana Sevas (personalized name-chanting offerings).

Beyond its role as a place of worship, the temple functions as a community gathering point for the Wellington region's Hindu population, with volunteer-run food stall services accompanying its regular religious programming.
