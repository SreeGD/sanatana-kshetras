The Adinathar Temple at Alwarthirunagari, the ancient Thirukkurugur of Pandya Nadu, enshrines the Lord as Adinatha, the Primordial Master, and holds a place of singular eminence among the 108 Divya Desams. For this is, by hallowed tradition, the birthplace of Nammalwar, the foremost of the twelve Alwars, whose Thiruvaymozhi is revered as the Tamil Veda and whose verses form the very heart of the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. Nammalwar himself sang the glory of his native shrine, so that Thirukkurugur stands not only as a Divya Desam hymned by an Alwar but as the cradle of the greatest of them. The sthala purana holds that the infant saint, born here, remained in silent absorption and later, seated beneath a tamarind tree associated with the temple, poured forth the four works—Thiruviruttam, Thiruvasiriyam, Periya Thiruvandhadhi and Thiruvaymozhi—through which the whole Prabandham finds its summit. The town accordingly bears the name Alwarthirunagari, "the sacred city of the Alwar," in his honour, and Nammalwar is venerated here in image alongside the Lord. As Adinatha, the Lord is worshipped as the first and original being, fittingly presiding over the place from which the Tamil hymnal tradition itself sprang. Pilgrims on the southern circuit of the Thamiraparani Divya Desams regard Thirukkurugur as the central jewel, returning to honour both the Primordial Lord and the saint who, born in this soil, gave the Srivaishnava world its most cherished songs of surrender and love.

## Festivals

Alwarthirunagari, the birthplace of Nammalvar, observes its most celebrated festival in the Vaikasi (Vasanthotsavam / Garuda Sevai season) honouring the saint, when Nammalvar's procession and the recitation of his Tiruvaymozhi take pride of place. The annual Brahmotsavam of Adinathar Perumal is kept with daily processions on garuda and other vahanas amid Veda and Divya Prabandham parayanam. Margazhi brings Vaikunta Ekadasi with the ten-day Pagal-pathu and Ra-pathu recitations, especially fitting here as the home of the Prabandham's chief composer. Pavithrotsavam and a float festival (Theppotsavam) are observed where custom maintains them, and the famed image of Nammalvar beneath the tamarind tree is venerated throughout the festival cycle.

## Location & Access

Adinathar Temple lies at Alwarthirunagari, also called Thirukkurugur, in the Thoothukudi district of Pandya Nadu, on the bank of the Tamiraparani river amid the regional cluster of Divya Desams. Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi are the nearest major towns and transport hubs, with Alwarthirunagari also served by its own railway station on the Tirunelveli line. Like most Vishnu temples it generally observes morning and evening darshan hours, closing at midday.
