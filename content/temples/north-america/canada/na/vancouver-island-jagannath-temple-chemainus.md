Named in Wikipedia's directory of Canadian Hindu temples, the Vancouver Island Jagannath Temple in Chemainus, British Columbia, is dedicated to Jagannath — the distinctive form of Krishna (regarded in the Vaishnava tradition as a form of Vishnu) whose home shrine at Puri, in the Indian state of Odisha, is one of the four Char Dham pilgrimage sites and the center of the annual Rath Yatra chariot festival. A dedicated Jagannath temple is unusual among North American diaspora temples, which more commonly enshrine Rama, Krishna-Radha, Vishnu/Balaji, Shiva, or Devi forms.

Beyond its name and Chemainus location on Vancouver Island, no founding date, founders, address, or architectural detail could be confirmed for this temple in this pass, and the underlying Wikipedia list entry itself carries no citation. This entry is included as a genuine, specifically named rare-deity find, but should be treated as provisional pending fuller verification.
