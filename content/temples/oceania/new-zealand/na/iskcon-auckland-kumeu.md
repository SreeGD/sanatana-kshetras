Also known by its property name "New Varshana," ISKCON Auckland's Sri Sri Radha-Giridhari Temple occupies a roughly 100-acre rural campus at Kumeu, northwest of central Auckland, and forms part of the worldwide International Society for Krishna Consciousness network. Its presiding deities, Sri Sri Radha-Giridhari, depict Krishna as "Giridhari" — the lifter of Govardhan Hill, a central episode from the Bhagavata Purana in which Krishna shelters the cowherd community of Vraja from Indra's wrath — paired with his eternal consort Radha, reflecting the Gaudiya Vaishnava devotional tradition ISKCON carries forward.

Open to the public free of charge year-round from 4:30am to 9:15pm daily, the campus functions as far more than a place of worship: it maintains a goshala (cow protection sanctuary) consistent with Krishna-conscious agricultural values, and runs a dedicated Hare Krishna School educating children aged 5 to 13. A regular Sunday program combining congregational chanting (kirtan) with a free vegetarian prasadam meal serves as the community's central weekly gathering point.
