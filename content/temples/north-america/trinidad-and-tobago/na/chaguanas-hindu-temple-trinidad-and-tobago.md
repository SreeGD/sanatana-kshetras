The Chaguanas Hindu Temple stands at the corner of Cumberbatch Street and Chaguanas Main Road in Chaguanas, central Trinidad — one of the island's largest towns and among its most heavily Indo-Trinidadian, home also to the annual Divali Nagar grounds where Trinidad's national Diwali festival is centered each year.

Its central, main-road location suggests a role as one of the town's principal congregational mandirs, though — as with many of the numerous individually named Hindu temples clustered throughout greater Chaguanas (including this directory's separately documented Trinidad Sevashram Sangha, in the town's Felicity area) — no founding date, founders, or specific presiding deity could be confirmed for it individually in this pass, and it should not be assumed to share institutional history with any other Chaguanas-area mandir.
