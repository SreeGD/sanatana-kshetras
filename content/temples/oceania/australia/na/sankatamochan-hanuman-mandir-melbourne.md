Sankatamochan Hanuman Mandir is a Melbourne temple dedicated specifically to Hanuman — "Sankatamochan" ("remover of troubles") being one of Hanuman's principal epithets, reflecting his role in Vaishnava devotional tradition as a protector who removes obstacles and afflictions for his devotees. A standalone temple with Hanuman as its primary and namesake deity is comparatively rare among diaspora Hindu temples, which more commonly enshrine Hanuman as a subsidiary shrine within a larger multi-deity complex.
