The Canberra Arrupadai Veedu Murugan Temple, in the Torrens suburb of the Australian Capital Territory, is dedicated to Lord Murugan (Kartikeya/Skanda) represented across his six traditional South Indian abodes — the Arupadai Veedu, comprising Thiruthani, Thiruparankundram, Thiruchendur, Palani, Swamimalai, and Palamuthircholai. Built, per the temple's own description, according to Agama and Shastra guidelines for traditional temple construction, it also houses subsidiary shrines to Durga, Lakshmi, Saraswati, Vinayakar (Ganesha), a Shiva Linga, Amman, Vishnu, Nataraja, Navagraha, and Vairavar.

Beyond regular worship, the temple runs a weekly vegetarian food fair for the community and holds a chariot (ther) festival, both drawing Canberra's South Indian Tamil Hindu population together for communal celebration alongside its regular pooja and abhishekam calendar.
