The Hare Krishna Cultural Centre is ISKCON's (International Society for Krishna Consciousness) presence in central Dublin, listed in Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in the Republic of Ireland as a recognised Hindu temple/centre in the Dublin 1 postal district. It represents a second, distinct node of organized Hindu worship in Dublin alongside the Ananda Sidhi Vinayaka Temple in the suburb of Kingswood, Dublin 24, already documented elsewhere in this directory — together reflecting the growth of Ireland's Hindu community across both the city centre and its suburbs.

As with other ISKCON centres, its practice can be presumed to follow the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition founded in the West by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, centered on the worship of Radha Krishna and the chanting of the Hare Krishna maha-mantra, though the source available for this entry does not independently confirm the centre's specific deity images, founding date, or exact address. Fuller documentation of the centre's history and current activities would need to be confirmed directly with ISKCON Ireland.
