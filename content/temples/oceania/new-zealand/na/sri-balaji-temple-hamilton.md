The Sri Balaji Temple Charitable Trust was founded in Hamilton around 2009, and after six years of fundraising and organizing, the trust opened its temple in March 2015 at 2 Kent Street in the suburb of Frankton, later adding an extension premises on King Street. The temple's own record of a seventeenth Annual General Meeting falling in 2026 independently lines up with that roughly-2009 founding date, corroborating the timeline given in secondary sources.

Modeled after the hill-shrine of Sri Venkateswara at Tirupati in Andhra Pradesh, India — traditionally described as standing on the seventh peak, Venkatachala — the Hamilton temple enshrines Sri Venkateswara (Balaji) as its presiding deity alongside Sri Satyanarayana Swamy and Sri Maha Lakshmi. Operating within the Vaishnava tradition, it serves as a community hub for Hamilton's Hindu population, hosting daily puja and regular cultural and religious programming throughout the week.
