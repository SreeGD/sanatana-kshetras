Founded in 1888 by Pandit Shri Sanjibonlall Ramsoondur — an Odisha-born landowner who had arrived in Mauritius from Calcutta in 1866 and later prospered during the island's sugar-estate land sales — this temple complex in Triolet is centered on a Shiva lingam under the name Maheshwarnath ("the great Lord"). Its architecture draws on Bengal and Odisha temple traditions, arranged in a panchayatna plan with a main Shiva shrine flanked by four subsidiary shrines, and the wider complex today comprises seven temples in total, including shrines to Vishnu, Nataraja, Hanuman, Jagannath, and Kashi Vishwanath. The temple holds particular significance in Mauritian Hindu practice as the starting point of the island's first organized Maha Shivaratri pilgrimage to the sacred crater lake Ganga Talao in 1898, a tradition that continues to draw large crowds each year.
