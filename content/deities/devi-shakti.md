This is a deliberately broad, catch-all category for the very large number of regional goddess ('Amman,' 'Bhagavathy,' 'Mata,' generic 'Devi'/'Shakti,' Parvati) temples in this directory whose presiding deity is not identified with enough specificity to place in the narrower Durga, Kali, Lakshmi, or Saraswati categories. Most South Asian village and regional goddess worship is understood theologically as a form of the single Devi/Shakti without a universally standardized individual name, so this category is intentionally the largest and least precise in this taxonomy — a genuine limitation of computing categories from free-text deity fields rather than a hand-verified classification.
