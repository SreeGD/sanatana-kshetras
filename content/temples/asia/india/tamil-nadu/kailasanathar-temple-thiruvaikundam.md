Srivaikuntam (also known as Thiruvaikundam), on the north bank of the Thamirabarani river in Thoothukudi (Tuticorin) district, is the sixth of the nine Nava Kailasam temples and is associated with Saturn (Sani) among the Navagrahas in that group's tradition — a distinct tradition from the separate Navagraha temple circuit near Kumbakonam further north in Tamil Nadu.

The town is notable for holding two major temples side by side: the Vaishnava Vaikuntanathar (Kallapiran) temple, one of the Nava Tirupathi cluster of the 108 Divya Desams sung by Nammalwar, and — separately — this Shiva Kailasanathar temple of the Nava Kailasam group. Wikipedia's article on the town, consulted for this entry, explicitly distinguishes the two and records that the Kailasanathar temple "contains six inscriptions of the times of Kattabomman," referring to the era of the 18th-century Panchalankurichi chieftain Veerapandiya Kattabomman, which is the most specific historical detail confirmed for this temple among the nine Nava Kailasam sites researched.

As with the wider group, tradition connects the nine temples to the sage Agastya's disciple Uromacha Munivar, said to have installed a Shiva lingam as Kailasa Nathar at each of nine places along the Thamirabarani where a floated flower came to rest, attaining liberation after venerating all nine.
