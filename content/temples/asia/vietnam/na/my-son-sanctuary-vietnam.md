A cluster of Hindu temple ruins built between the 4th and 14th centuries CE by the kings of Champa, an Indianized kingdom of what is now central Vietnam, My Son was the Champa kingdom's religious and intellectual capital, dedicated principally to Shiva under local names such as Bhadreshvara. Heavily damaged by American bombing during the Vietnam War in the 1960s, it survives today as a UNESCO World Heritage archaeological site documenting one of Southeast Asia's major historical Hindu civilizations, though it is no longer an active site of worship.
