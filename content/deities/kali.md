Kali, the fierce time-and-destruction aspect of the Great Goddess, is especially central to Bengal's Shakta tradition (Dakshineswar, Kalighat) but is venerated across the subcontinent and, notably, across the Indian diaspora in the Caribbean, Mauritius, South Africa, and Fiji, often as 'Mariamman' or under a Kali-specific name.
