Guyana has the highest proportion of Hindus of any country in the Western Hemisphere (about 31% of the population per the 2021 census, and historically higher), descended from indentured laborers brought from India between 1838 and 1917 under British colonial rule. Temples such as this one, often affiliated with the Guyana Sanatan Dharma Maha Sabha (the country's main Hindu religious body), anchor a vibrant, continuously-practiced Hindu community on the South American mainland.
