The Toronto Thiruchendur Murugan Temple is documented in Wikipedia's "List of Hindu temples in Canada" as a temple dedicated to Murugan (Kartikeya), the Tamil Saiva god of war and wisdom, son of Shiva and Parvati. Its name references Tiruchendur, in Tamil Nadu, India — one of the six Arupadai Veedu, the traditional "war abodes" central to Tamil Murugan devotion — suggesting the temple serves Toronto's Tamil Hindu community in a devotional lineage tied to that celebrated pilgrimage site, though no formal institutional affiliation could be confirmed.
