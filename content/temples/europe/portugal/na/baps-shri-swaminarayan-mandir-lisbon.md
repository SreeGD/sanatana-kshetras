BAPS (Bochasanwasi Shri Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha), the Gujarati Hindu organization behind landmark temples such as London's Neasden Mandir and the Cardiff Swaminarayan Mandir, both already documented elsewhere in this directory, inaugurated a mandir in Lisbon on 28 September 2014. This is distinct from the city's older Radha Krishna Mandir in the Lumiar district, which opened in 1998 and is also already documented in this directory, indicating that Lisbon — home to the majority of Portugal's recorded Hindu population, concentrated in the greater Lisbon metropolitan area — now supports more than one organized strand of Hindu worship. BAPS's own records show the Lisbon mandir has remained continuously active in the years since, hosting Swaminarayan Jayanti celebrations in 2015, a sixth-anniversary Patotsav celebration in 2020, and Pramukh Swami Maharaj Centennial celebrations in 2023.

As at BAPS's other mandirs worldwide, worship here can be presumed to center on Swaminarayan, the tradition's founding guru venerated within the Swaminarayan Sampraday as a manifestation of the divine, though no source available for this entry gives further detail on the mandir's exact address, building, or congregation size. Fuller documentation would need to be confirmed directly with BAPS Portugal.
