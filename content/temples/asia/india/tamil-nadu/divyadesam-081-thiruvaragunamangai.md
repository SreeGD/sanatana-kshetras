The Vijayasana Perumal temple at Thiruvaragunamangai, known also as Natham, is one of the 108 Divya Desams cherished by Sri Vaishnavas and one of the Nava Tirupathi, the nine sacred shrines clustered along the Tamiraparani river in the Pandya Nadu. The presiding Lord is revered here as Vijayasana Perumal, the Lord seated upon the throne of victory, gracing devotees in a seated posture together with His consort, the Mother revered as Varagunavalli Thayar, from whom the place draws its name Thiruvaragunamangai. The very epithet Vijayasana proclaims the Lord's sovereign triumph and the assurance of victory He bestows upon those who take refuge at His feet. This shrine is hallowed by the hymns of Nammalwar, the chief of the Alwars, whose verses in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham consecrated Thiruvaragunamangai within the eternal Tamil canon and revealed the seated Lord as an object of his rapturous devotion. As a member of the Nava Tirupathi, the temple shares in the sanctity of the Tamiraparani, whose waters the tradition holds as a sacred theertham, and the pilgrimage to these nine abodes is undertaken by the faithful as a single garland of worship binding the Divya Desams of the region. Resting upon the throne of victory in the company of Varagunavalli Thayar, Vijayasana Perumal stands as the gracious bestower of triumph and refuge, drawing devotees of the Pandya country to behold the Lord whom Nammalwar sang.

## Festivals

The Vijayasana Perumal temple at Thiruvaragunamangai (Natham) traditionally follows the customary Srivaishnava festival cycle. The annual Brahmotsavam is the principal celebration, observed over several days with processions of the utsava murti on successive vahanas, the Garuda Sevai, the chariot festival and the closing Theerthavari at the sacred tank. Vaikunta Ekadasi in the month of Margazhi is devoutly kept, with recitation of the Nalayira Divya Prabandham during the Pagal-pathu and Ra-pathu. Pavithrotsavam, performed to sanctify the year's worship, and the Theppotsavam (float festival) are also traditionally observed. Krishna Jayanthi, Andal's Aadi Pooram, Rama Navami, Navaratri and the monthly asterism days of the Lord are marked with special abhishekam, alankaram and Prabandham recitation, drawing devotees to this Pandya Nadu shrine.
