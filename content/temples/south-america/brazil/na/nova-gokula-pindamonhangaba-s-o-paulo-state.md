A rural ISKCON farm community modeled on the same self-sufficient, agrarian ideal as New Vrindaban in the United States (also covered in this directory), Nova Gokula ("New Gokula," named for Krishna's childhood village) was founded in 1978 at Estrada Abílio José de Almeida in the Ribeirão Grande district near Pindamonhangaba, São Paulo state. Described by the community itself as the largest Hare Krishna community in Latin America, it has served for nearly five decades as a hub of Gaudiya Vaishnava practice in Brazil, hosting festivals, a gaushala (cow sanctuary), and educational programs for South America's Hare Krishna community, emphasizing simple living and Vedic-inspired spiritual culture at the base of the Serra da Mantiqueira mountains.
