Under the umbrella of the Hindu Society of Minnesota (HSMN), the Hindu Temple of Minnesota's community dates back at least to 1979, when the group carried out a first major renovation of its facilities under Arun Shirole's direction, and installed its first murti — a Ganesha donated by Drs. Kumud and Sashikant Sane — in 1983. The community's growth culminated in a $9 million construction project completed in June 2006, which expanded the Maple Grove campus to more than 43,000 square feet, with capacity for up to 5,000 worshippers, and added 21 mini-temple shrines built as replicas of well-known temples from across India, making it one of the largest Hindu temple complexes in the United States. Confirmed enshrined deities include Ganesha and Varadaraja, a form of Vishnu; a full deity-by-deity account of all 21 replica shrines was not found in the sources checked for this entry. By the mid-2000s the temple was serving an estimated 40,000-plus Hindus across the Minneapolis-St. Paul metropolitan area, led since 2007 by chief priest Ronur Murali Bhattar and a staff of seven full-time priests.
