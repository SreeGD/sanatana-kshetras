Swaminarayan (Sahajanand Swami), founder of the Swaminarayan Sampradaya, is venerated by his followers as the Supreme Being manifest in human form. This directory's many BAPS (Bochasanwasi Shri Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha) and NarNarayan Dev Gadi temples worldwide fall under this category.
