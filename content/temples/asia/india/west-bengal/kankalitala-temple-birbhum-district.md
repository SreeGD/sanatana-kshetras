Kankalitala, on the bank of the Kopai river some nine kilometres from Bolpur in Birbhum district, is one of Bengal's 51 Shakti Peethas. Tradition holds that when Sati's body was cut apart following her self-immolation, her bones — kankal in Bengali — fell at this spot, giving the site its name. The Goddess is worshipped here as Devgarbha, while the associated Bhairava, called Ruru, is worshipped separately in the adjoining Kanchishwar Temple, so the complex effectively holds two linked shrines rather than a single sanctum.

The site draws pilgrims as one of the more accessible and well-known Shakti Peethas in Birbhum district, being a short distance off the Bolpur-Labhpur road, but the sources checked give no dated inscriptional or architectural record for when the current temple structures were built or by whom. As with several of Bengal's smaller Shakti Peethas, its documented history is thin even though its status within the regional pilgrimage tradition is well established and long-standing.
