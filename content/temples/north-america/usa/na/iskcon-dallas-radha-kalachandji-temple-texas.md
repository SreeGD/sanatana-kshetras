Established in 1971, the Radha Kalachandji Temple is ISKCON's Dallas center, located in a residential pocket of East Dallas. It is locally well known beyond the Hindu community for its long-running vegetarian restaurant on the temple grounds, which local press has covered as one of Dallas's longest-serving vegetarian eateries, alongside its event space and ongoing neighborhood park revitalization efforts. The temple continues regular Sunday evening programs of kirtan, a talk on the Bhagavad Gita, and a free vegetarian feast, in addition to major festivals such as Janmashtami.
