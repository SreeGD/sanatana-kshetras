Not to be confused with the older, smaller Sri Venkateswara Temple in Penn Hills, Pittsburgh, this Bridgewater, New Jersey temple is one of the largest Balaji/Venkateswara temples in the United States. It grew out of the Hindu Temple and Cultural Society of USA Inc. (HTCS), founded in 1989 by Hindu families in the central New Jersey area, which acquired land in Bridgewater in February 1992. Groundbreaking followed in 1995, and a stone-laying ceremony conducted by Chinna Jeeyar Swami in May 1996 marked the start of construction according to Vastu Shastra principles; the completed 11,000-square-foot temple, housing sixteen shrines, was consecrated and inaugurated in 1998. Its main sanctum enshrines Venkateswara flanked by Sridevi and Bhudevi, with subsidiary shrines to Ganesha, a Shiva linga, Durga, Saraswati, Lakshmi, Radha Krishna and Rama serving the religiously diverse Hindu community of the greater New York/New Jersey area. A proposed expansion drew a five-year zoning dispute with Bridgewater Township, ultimately resolved in 2009 with approval of a scaled-down plan.
