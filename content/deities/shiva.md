Shiva, one of the principal deities of Sanatana Dharma, is worshipped across an immense range of local forms and lingam manifestations — most enshrined under a locally specific epithet (typically ending in '-eswarar,' '-nathar,' or '-eshwar' in Sanskrit/Dravidian-language temple names) rather than the name 'Shiva' itself. This category covers general Shiva worship not otherwise classified as Nataraja or Bhairava below.
