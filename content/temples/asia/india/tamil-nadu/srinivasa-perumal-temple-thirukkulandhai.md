Thirukkulandhai, the village of Perungulam on the southern bank of the Thamirabarani a few kilometres from Azhwarthirunagari, is the Saturn (Shani) sthalam among the nine Nava Tirupathi. Vishnu is worshipped here as Srinivasa Perumal, also addressed as Mayakoothar, with his consort Lakshmi honoured as Kulandhaivalli Thayar (also called Alamelu Mangai Thayar), alongside the traditional consort forms Sridevi, Bhudevi, and Niladevi.

The temple's founding legend tells of a devoted couple, Devasaran and Kumudavalli, who were blessed with a daughter, Kamaladevi, through divine favour. Kamaladevi is said to have performed intense penance in a forest at this site in the hope of obtaining Vishnu himself as her husband; the settlement that grew up around her austerities came to be called Thirukkulandhai, from the Tamil for a young girl or maiden, commemorating her devotion.

Nammalvar counted this shrine among the nine Nava Tirupathi he glorified together in his Tiruvaimozhi, securing its place in the fixed list of 108 Divya Desams. The temple today follows the Tenkalai tradition of Sri Vaishnava ritual practice, observes six daily rituals and three annual festivals, and is administered by the Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowments Board of the Tamil Nadu government; its most prominent yearly event is the ten-day Vaikuntha Ekadashi in the Tamil month of Margazhi, alongside the Garudasevai celebrations marking Nammalvar's birth star that are observed jointly across all nine Nava Tirupathi temples.
