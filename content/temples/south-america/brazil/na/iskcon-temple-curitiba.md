Curitiba's ISKCON temple is documented in available sources chiefly through a single reported incident: in October 2018, unknown vandals defaced a painting of Krishna with his mother Yashoda on the temple's entrance gate, an act publicized at the time as targeting a religious minority. Beyond confirming that an active ISKCON temple exists in Curitiba, the capital of Paraná state in southern Brazil, no founding date, founder, or further institutional history could be verified this pass.

Like other ISKCON communities, it belongs to the worldwide Gaudiya Vaishnava movement founded by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada in 1966, and Curitiba is also a stop on Brazil's national circuit of ISKCON-organized Ratha-Yatra festival processions.
