The Sri Ayyappan Temple sits on Rue Philippe de Girard in the La Chapelle district of Paris's 18th arrondissement — the same dense cluster of Tamil-run shops, restaurants, and temples near Gare du Nord that is also home to the already-documented Sri Manicka Vinayakar Alayam. It is dedicated to Ayyappan (also called Ayyappa or Sastha), a deity particularly venerated in Kerala and parts of Tamil Nadu and traditionally held to be the son of Shiva and Mohini (Vishnu's female form), whose major pilgrimage center is Sabarimala in Kerala.

Ayyappan worship is otherwise unrepresented among this directory's European entries, making this Paris temple a distinctive addition to its coverage of South Indian devotional traditions transplanted to the diaspora.
