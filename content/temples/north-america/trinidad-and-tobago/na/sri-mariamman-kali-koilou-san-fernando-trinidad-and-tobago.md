Sri Mariamman Kali Koilou, in the Golconda Settlement area of Cross Crossing in San Fernando — Trinidad's second-largest city — is dedicated, per its own name, to Mariamman and Kali, fierce protective goddess forms central to the South Indian ("Madrasi") Tamil folk-Hindu devotional tradition brought to Trinidad by nineteenth-century indentured laborers, alongside the larger population of North Indian-descended Hindus who form the majority of Trinidad's Sanatan Dharma community.

This Tamil devotional strand — centered on Mariamman (also venerated as "Maliémin" in the French Antilles) — links San Fernando's Sri Mariamman Kali Koilou to a wider Caribbean and circum-Atlantic pattern of goddess-temple worship documented elsewhere in this directory, including Temple Changy in Guadeloupe and the historic Hindu temple of Basse-Pointe in Martinique. No founding date, founders, or further institutional history could be confirmed for this specific temple in this pass.
