Renovation work on an existing building to become Christchurch's BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir was already underway when the magnitude-6.3 earthquake struck the city in February 2011, temporarily halting the project during a period when the wider community was focused on disaster recovery. The mandir at 19 Frank Street in the suburb of Papanui was nonetheless completed and formally inaugurated later that year with a Mahapuja consecration ceremony, becoming — at the time — the only Hindu temple serving Christchurch and New Zealand's entire South Island.

As with other mandirs built under the BAPS (Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha) organization, the temple follows the Swaminarayan Sampraday within the Vaishnava tradition, centering worship on Bhagwan Swaminarayan. It continues to serve the South Island's Gujarati and broader Hindu community as a place of daily worship and festival gatherings such as Diwali and Annakut.
