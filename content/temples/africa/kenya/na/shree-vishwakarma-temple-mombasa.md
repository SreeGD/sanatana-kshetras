Listed among Mombasa's places of worship on the city's Mainland side, the Shree Vishwakarma Temple is dedicated to Vishwakarma, the Hindu deity of architecture, engineering, and craftsmanship, traditionally venerated by artisan, engineering, and construction trades communities. This is a comparatively rare dedication within the broader Hindu temple landscape, though East Africa's Indian-origin community includes a Vishwakarma Temple in Jinja, Uganda (already documented in this directory), suggesting a recognised, if uncommon, devotional tradition among the region's artisan and engineering trades communities.

Beyond its name and Mombasa Mainland location, no dedicated source describing this temple's founding, architecture, or current worship activities was located during this research pass; it is documented here only via a bare listing in Wikipedia's overview of Mombasa's religious institutions.
