A center of Gaudiya Vaishnava practice serving Sydney's Hare Krishna community, tracing back to 1971, when A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada personally visited Australia and founded the country's first Hare Krishna centre in Sydney. Whether today's Bhaktivedanta Swami Mandir in Burwood is a direct continuation of that original 1971 centre, or a later temple established separately as the community grew, is not confirmed in sources consulted so far. The temple hosts regular public programs and an annual Ratha Yatra chariot festival in the city, part of the same public-kirtan tradition ISKCON introduced in New York and London in the movement's earliest years.
