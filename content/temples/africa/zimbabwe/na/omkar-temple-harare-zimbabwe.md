Described on Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in Zimbabwe as the country's first Hindu temple, the Omkar Temple in Harare had its foundation stone laid in 1929, serving a Hindu community descended from indentured and migrant laborers brought from Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu under British colonial administration in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. That community has since shrunk considerably, numbering only around 3,000 people (roughly 500 families) in recent estimates, concentrated mainly in Harare. Beyond the founding date and its status as the country's first Hindu temple, available sources do not detail the temple's founders, presiding deity, or architecture; a second Hindu place of worship, an ISKCON centre, operates separately at Marondera.
