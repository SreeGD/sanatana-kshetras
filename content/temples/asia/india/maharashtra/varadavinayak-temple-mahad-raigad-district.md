A comparatively modest, small-domed shrine whose golden kalash (pinnacle) is a distinctive landmark, Varadavinayak ('Ganesha who grants boons') is associated in local tradition with a lamp said to have been kept continuously burning since the temple's establishment. Tradition also links the site to a legend involving the sage-king Rukmangada. The present temple structure is commonly attributed to 18th-century patronage during the Peshwa era.
