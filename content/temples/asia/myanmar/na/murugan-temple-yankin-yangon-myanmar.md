The Murugan Hindu Temple (also recorded as the Supramanayan Hindu Temple) on Budayon Road in Yangon's Yankin Township serves one of the city's Tamil Hindu communities outside the better-known downtown "Little India" area, where the already-documented Shri Kali Temple is located. Yankin Township is described in available sourcing as home to a large Tamil community and "a rich collection of Hindu temples," most dedicated to Kali and Ganesh, among which this Murugan (Subramaniam) temple stands out by name as a distinct landmark.

No detailed founding history could be confirmed for the temple from the sources checked in this research pass, but its existence, name, deity, and specific location in Yankin Township are corroborated across two independent sources: a Wikipedia township article naming it directly, and a separately geotagged photograph from the same area. Its presence rounds out this directory's coverage of Yangon's Hindu Tamil community beyond the single downtown temple previously documented, reflecting the wider, lesser-known spread of Hindu worship across the city.
