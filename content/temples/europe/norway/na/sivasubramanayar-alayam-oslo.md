The Sivasubramanayar Alayam, also known as the Norwegian Hindu Centre, is a Tamil Hindu temple in the Ammerud neighborhood of northeastern Oslo, dedicated to Murugan in his form as Sivasubramanya (literally "Shiva's own Subramanya/Skanda"). It is one of six Hindu temples that Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in Norway identifies as currently active in the country, alongside the Sanatan Mandir Sabha in Slemmestad — Norway's first registered Hindu religious community, already documented elsewhere in this directory — reflecting the growth of organized Tamil Hindu worship in the Oslo area beyond that first Slemmestad temple.

As a Sivasubramanayar/Murugan temple, it fills a rare-deity gap in this directory's Norway coverage, paralleling similarly named Sivasubramaniar temples founded by Sri Lankan Tamil communities in Switzerland (Adliswil) and elsewhere in Europe. Beyond its name, neighborhood, and general dedication, the source available for this entry gives no founding date, address, or further history; fuller documentation would need to be confirmed directly with the temple.
