Therazhundur, also known as Thiruvazhundur, in the Chola Nadu, is one of the 108 Divya Desams celebrated in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. The presiding deity (Moolavar) of this shrine is Aamaruviappan, a name that tradition connects with the Lord who graced a cow (aa), and He is enshrined here as the gracious Vishnu of this sacred town on the banks of the Kaveri. Therazhundur is especially renowned in Srivaishnava memory as a place dear to the tradition, and it is honoured as the birthplace town associated with the great preceptor Sekkizhar in later Saiva tradition, though for the Vaishnava world its glory rests in its standing as a Divya Desam and in the song of the Alwars. The shrine's place in the Divya Prabandham was secured by Thirumangai Alwar, the warrior-saint among the twelve Alwars, who sang the praise of Aamaruviappan and thereby enshrined this town in the eternal canon of Alwar hymns. According to the sthala purana, the Lord here is approached as a refuge of compassion, and devotees revere His form with the mangalasasanam offered by the Alwar. Set amid the fertile Chola delta, Therazhundur preserves the atmosphere of an ancient temple town where the worship of the reclining and standing forms of Vishnu has continued across the centuries. For pilgrims of the 108 Divya Desams, the darshan of Aamaruviappan at Thiruvazhundur is cherished both for the beauty of the deity and for the living link it holds to the devotional outpourings of Thirumangai Alwar.
