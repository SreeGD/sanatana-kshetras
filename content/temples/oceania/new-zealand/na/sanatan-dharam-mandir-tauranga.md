The Sanatan Dharam Mandir serves the Hindu community of Tauranga, on New Zealand's North Island, built through the efforts of local community members in the Tauriko area on the city's outskirts at 108 Whiore Ave. While the organizing trust/community was inaugurated in 2012, construction of the physical temple did not begin until early 2015, after the trust purchased roughly 2,150 square metres of land in Tauriko for around $400,000; Stage One of the building was completed by mid-to-late 2015. As with many smaller diaspora mandirs outside the country's larger Auckland, Wellington, Hamilton, and Christchurch communities, it appears to function as a shared, pan-Hindu place of worship for Tauranga's Hindu residents rather than being dedicated to a single sampradaya.

Available sources confirm the temple's name, community origin, address, and construction timeline; the deities installed within it and its managing trust's name are not confirmed in sources consulted this pass, so this entry is recorded as pending further verification on those points.
