ISKCON Boston, also known within the movement as New Gundica, occupies a Back Bay brownstone at 72 Commonwealth Avenue. The temple worships multiple deity altars — Sri Sri Gaura Nitai, Sri Sri Radha Gopivallabha, and Jagannath, Baladeva and Subhadra — and runs a broad community program including free weekend yoga classes, food distribution, a Sunday School, and a children's reading club, alongside the standard Gaudiya Vaishnava festival calendar such as Janmashtami, Gaura Purnima, and the Boston Festival of Chariots (Ratha Yatra).
