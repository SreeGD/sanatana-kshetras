Named in Wikipedia's directory of Canadian Hindu temples as being located in Vancouver proper — distinct from the Greater Vancouver-area temples already in this directory, which sit in the suburban municipalities of Burnaby and Richmond Hill (itself actually a Toronto suburb, not Vancouver) — the Sri Ganesh Temple Society of BC is dedicated to Ganesha. Beyond its name and city attribution in that list, no founding date, address, founders, or other institutional detail could be independently confirmed this pass, and the underlying list entry itself carries no citation.
