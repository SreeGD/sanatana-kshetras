A cluster of Hindu temple ruins built between the 4th and 13th centuries CE by the kings of Champa, an Indianized kingdom of what is now central Vietnam, My Son was the Champa kingdom's religious and intellectual capital, founded under King Bhadravarman I (r. 380-413) and dedicated principally to Shiva under local names such as Bhadreshvara. Heavily damaged by American bombing in August 1969 during the Vietnam War, it survives today as a UNESCO World Heritage archaeological site (inscribed 1999) documenting one of Southeast Asia's major historical Hindu civilizations, though it is no longer an active site of worship.
