The Purushothaman Temple at Thiruvanpurushothamam, situated within the sacred precincts of Thirunangur in the Chola Nadu region of Tamil Nadu, is one of the 108 Divya Desams sung by the Alwars in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. The presiding deity (Moolavar) is worshipped here as Purushothama, a revered name of Vishnu meaning the Supreme Person, the highest among all beings, in whom the fullness of the divine is beheld. This shrine forms part of the celebrated group of eleven Vishnu temples gathered at Thirunangur, the Thirunangur Vinnagarams, which are honoured together in tradition and visited by pilgrims as a connected garland of sacred abodes. The name Thiruvanpurushothamam marks this place as one dedicated wholly to the contemplation of the Lord as Purushothama, the perfect refuge of all souls. It was Thirumangai Alwar, the saint most abundant in the Divya Desams he praised and deeply associated with the holy town of Thirunangur, who consecrated this shrine with his Tamil verses, securing its place within the Alwars' sacred corpus. As one of the 108 Divya Desams, Thiruvanpurushothamam draws devotees to worship Narayana under His exalted name, inviting them to surrender to the Supreme Person who stands above the changing world. Within the cluster of Thirunangur abodes, this temple holds its honoured station, contributing to the rich tapestry of divine presence that distinguishes this corner of the Chola country as a land richly blessed by the descent of the Lord.

## Festivals

This temple at Thiruvanpurushothamam, one of the eleven Thirunangur Divya Desams, shares in the famed Thirunangur Garuda Sevai, when the processional deities of the eleven neighbouring shrines are borne together upon their Garuda mounts during the Tamil month of Thai, a celebrated assembly of the Lords that draws devotees in great numbers. The annual Brahmotsavam is traditionally observed over several days with festive processions of the utsavar. Vaikunta Ekadasi in Margazhi is marked with special devotion and the chanting of the Tiruvaymoli, and the temple keeps the broader Srivaishnava cycle of observances, including Pavithrotsavam and a Float festival (Theppotsavam), during which the daily rituals and adornment of the deity take on a heightened splendour.

## Location & Access

The temple lies at Thiruvanpurushothamam within Thirunangur, in the Chola Nadu region of Tamil Nadu, in the Nagapattinam district among the cluster of eleven Thirunangur Divya Desams close to Sirkazhi. It is reached by road from Sirkazhi and Mayiladuthurai, the nearest towns served by railway, with broader transport links through the Nagapattinam and Thanjavur region. Like most Vishnu temples it generally observes morning and evening darshan hours, remaining closed through the midday period.
