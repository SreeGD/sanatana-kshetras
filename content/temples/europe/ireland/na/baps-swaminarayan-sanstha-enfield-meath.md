BAPS (Bochasanwasi Shri Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha), the same Gujarati Hindu organization behind London's landmark Neasden Temple already documented in this directory, maintains a centre in the small town of Enfield, County Meath, in the Irish Midlands — evidence that Ireland's Hindu community, though far smaller than Britain's, has established organized Swaminarayan-tradition worship outside Dublin itself.

As at BAPS's other centres worldwide, worship here can be presumed to center on Swaminarayan, the tradition's founding guru venerated as a manifestation of the divine, though the source available for this entry gives no further detail on the Enfield centre's founding date, building, or congregation size. It appears in Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in the Republic of Ireland only as a name-and-location listing, and fuller documentation would need to be confirmed directly with BAPS.
