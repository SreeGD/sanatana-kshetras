Vamana, Vishnu's fifth avatar, appeared as a dwarf Brahmin to reclaim the three worlds from the demon-king Mahabali by requesting only as much land as he could cover in three steps, then expanding to cosmic size (Trivikrama) to do so. Kerala's Onam festival commemorates Mahabali's yearly return and is closely associated with this avatar; Thrikkakara Temple is the tradition's principal shrine.
