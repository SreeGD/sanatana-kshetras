Krishna, the eighth avatar of Vishnu, is among the most widely worshipped forms across Sanatana Dharma, spanning the Bhagavata-Purana cowherd narratives (Vrindavan, Gokul, Mathura), the Bhagavad Gita's teacher-charioteer form, and the Radha-Krishna devotional focus of the Gaudiya Vaishnava (ISKCON) and Pushtimarg traditions. Regional forms such as Vithoba/Panduranga (Maharashtra's Varkari tradition) are included in this broader category.
