ISKCON Bangkok is the Hare Krishna movement's institutional presence in Thailand's capital, operating under the spiritual authority of Founder-Acharya A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. Rather than a single flagship temple, it runs as a network of several centers spread across the city — including a Thonburi temple and centers on Sukhumwit Soi 71 and Soi 39, a Prachautith center, a Ramkhamhaeng center, and a further location on Sukhumwit Soi 62 that was under construction as of this research. Together these host regular deity worship, kirtan, book distribution, and the Food for Life meal-relief program associated with ISKCON centers worldwide.

Separate, smaller ISKCON temples also exist in the Thai resort towns of Pattaya and Phuket, reflecting the wider spread of Gaudiya Vaishnava practice among Thailand's Hindu and international devotee communities; this entry concerns the Bangkok centers specifically. As with other ISKCON communities, Janmashtami (Krishna's appearance day) is the largest annual public observance.
