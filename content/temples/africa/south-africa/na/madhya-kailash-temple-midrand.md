Serving the growing Hindu community of Midrand — the rapidly developed corridor of Gauteng roughly midway between Johannesburg and Pretoria — the Madhya Kailash Temple is dedicated to Shiva, worshipped here as the formless, timeless Supreme God of the Shaiva tradition. Wikipedia describes it as having been built relatively recently in response to the area's growing Hindu population, though no specific founding year is given; the temple's own website similarly frames its origin as arising from Indian immigrants' desire to fill a "spiritual vacuum" through "the great efforts of Brahma Sri Guru Nadarajah Sarma," without stating a founding date.

Worship follows the Agama Sastras under resident priest Guru Nadarajah Sarma, who has served South Africa's Hindu community for over 25 years. The temple's own materials describe its mission in the words of the Tamil poet Auvaiyar — "Thirai Kadal Odiyum, Thiravyam Thedu" (loosely, cross even the raging sea in pursuit of a worthy goal) — and state that it now serves hundreds of thousands of devotees. Beyond regular prayers and ceremonies, the temple hosts weekly Bharatanatyam (classical dance) and percussion classes, positioning it as both a religious and cultural centre for Gauteng's Hindu community outside the older, historically Indian areas of Johannesburg and Pretoria.
