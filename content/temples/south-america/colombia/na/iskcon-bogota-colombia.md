ISKCON Bogotá traces its founding to devotee Harinam Gauranga Das, who began practicing Krishna consciousness in 2009 and was initiated by Jayapataka Swami in 2013. According to ISKCON's own account of the center's history, he and his wife Isapriya Radha started the Bogotá preaching center with a budget of roughly $100, renting a room in a shared house before the community grew enough to be formally inaugurated in May 2017; by 2022 the center had moved to a more permanent location in the Colombian capital. It belongs to the worldwide Gaudiya Vaishnava movement founded by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada in 1966.

Bogotá's center is part of a broader ISKCON presence across Colombia, which by the mid-2010s reportedly maintained temples or preaching centers in roughly twenty Colombian cities serving a still-small national community of a few hundred devotees. Alongside a temple room, the Bogotá center has developed community features typical of urban ISKCON preaching centers elsewhere, including a vegetarian restaurant, a yoga studio, and public chanting and outreach events such as "Mantra Nights." Because the fullest available account of its history comes from ISKCON's own reporting rather than an independent source, some details — including the precise address and the exact deity murtis installed — remain unconfirmed.
