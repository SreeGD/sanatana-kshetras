One of the most important Shakti Peethas, Kamakhya is a center of Tantric Shakta worship, traditionally marking the site where the yoni (womb/generative organ) of Sati fell after Shiva's grief-stricken wandering with her corpse (per the Daksha-yagna/Sati legend common to all Shakti Peethas). The temple observes no idol; the goddess is worshipped in the form of a natural rock fissure, and the annual Ambubachi Mela marks her yearly menstruation cycle in tantric tradition.
