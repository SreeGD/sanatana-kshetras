Suriname's small but active ISKCON presence traces to Hare Krishna devotees from neighboring Guyana — itself home to one of the world's highest proportions of Hindus — who began visiting in the early 1980s. That contact eventually grew into a standing preaching center in Nieuw Nickerie, Suriname's second-largest town, near the Guyana border on the Corantijn River. Like other ISKCON communities worldwide, it belongs to the Gaudiya Vaishnava movement founded by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada in 1966.

Available sources confirm the center's existence and general location but do not give a precise founding date, official name, or address, so these details should be treated as unconfirmed pending a dedicated source.
