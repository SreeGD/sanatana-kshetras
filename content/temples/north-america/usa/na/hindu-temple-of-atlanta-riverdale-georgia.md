Established on 19 October 1984, the Hindu Temple of Atlanta in Riverdale, Georgia — not to be confused with the BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir in nearby Lilburn — is one of the older South Indian-tradition temples serving Metro Atlanta's Hindu community. Its original complex, centered on Venkateswara (a form of Vishnu) with consort Padmavathi, follows temple-architecture conventions traced to the Pandya Empire of South India. A second shrine dedicated to Shiva, worshipped locally as Ramalingeshwara, was consecrated in 2007, and the grounds also house shrines to Hanuman, Ganesha, Kala Bhairava, and the Navagraha (nine celestial bodies).

Situated close to I-75 and roughly nine miles from Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, the temple draws not only the local Metro Atlanta community but also a meaningful share of visitors from across the southern, eastern-seaboard, and midwestern United States, and has been counted among the more prominent Hindu temples in the country.
