Mwanza, Tanzania's second-largest city and its main port on Lake Victoria, has long supported a substantial Gujarati and wider Indian-origin trading community, reflected today in a cluster of Hindu temples standing close together on the city's own Temple Street. Wikipedia's inventory of Hindu temples in the country names one of them the Shri Sanatan Dharma Mandir; Wikipedia's separate article on Mwanza describes the same street as home to an unnamed "large Hindu Temple built in the late 50's, one of the first such temples in Tanzania," an unnamed "Vaishnava Sanatana Mandir," and a named BAPS Shri Swaminarayan temple, without tying any single proper name to the first two.

Given the matching Vaishnava and "Sanatana" (Sanatan Dharma) description, this entry treats the Shri Sanatan Dharma Mandir as most likely corresponding to the "Vaishnava Sanatana Mandir" on Temple Street, serving Mwanza's broader, non-sectarian Sanatan Dharma Hindu congregation rather than a single narrower sampradaya. Whether this temple, or its unnamed neighbor, is the one dated to construction in the late 1950s - making it one of the earliest purpose-built Hindu temples anywhere in Tanzania - could not be confirmed with certainty from the sources available this pass, though the cluster as a whole reflects a Hindu community established on Lake Victoria's shore well before Tanzanian independence in 1961.
