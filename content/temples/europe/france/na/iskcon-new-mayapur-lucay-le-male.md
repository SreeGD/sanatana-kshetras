New Mayapur is an ISKCON (International Society for Krishna Consciousness) château and farm community in the Indre department of central France, near the town of Luçay-le-Mâle and not far from Châteauroux. Housed in the Château d'Oublaise, it follows the pattern of several other European ISKCON rural properties — such as Belgium's Radhadesh and England's Bhaktivedanta Manor — where a country estate was converted into a combined temple, farming community, and gathering place for Krishna-conscious devotees following the teachings of A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, ISKCON's founder. Its name echoes Mayapur, West Bengal, the birthplace of the sixteenth-century saint Chaitanya Mahaprabhu and the international headquarters of the ISKCON movement.

Public records confirm the community was already operating by the early 1980s: Indradyumna Swami, a long-serving ISKCON guru, is recorded as having served as temple president at New Mayapur during that period. Beyond that, detailed documentation of the property's founding date and early history was not found in the sources checked this session, so this entry should be treated as provisional pending direct confirmation from the temple or a more detailed history.
