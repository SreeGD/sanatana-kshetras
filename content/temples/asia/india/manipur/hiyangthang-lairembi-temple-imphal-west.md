Hiyangthang Lairembi Temple crowns the hill of Heibok Ching at Hiyangthang, in Manipur's Imphal West district, and is one of the clearer surviving examples of how the indigenous Meitei Sanamahi religion and later Vaishnava-era Hindu practice came to coexist in the Imphal valley rather than one simply displacing the other. Worship of the resident goddess, known as Hiyangthang Lairembi or Irai Leima, is traditionally dated to the reign of the Ningthouja king Senbi Kiyamba (1467-1508 CE), and the Sarangthem family, through an ancestor named Sarangthem Luwangba, is credited with establishing her worship at this site; the family has maintained an annual feast, Chaklen Katpa, in her honor ever since.

Local legend explains how the goddess came to reside with the Sarangthem family. Irai Leima was the daughter of Heibok Ningthou, a king said to possess magical power, who turned an unwanted royal suitor, King Kwakpa of the Khuman dynasty, to stone rather than let him take his daughter. The frightened princess fled and took refuge, unrecognized, as a servant in Sarangthem Luwangba's grain storehouse, until she appeared to him in a dream declaring her divine nature and her wish to remain with his household as its guardian goddess; royal maibas and maibis (traditional priests and priestesses) are said to have investigated and confirmed her status as a deity.

Roughly two and a half centuries later, during the 18th-century reign of King Garib Niwaj (Pamheiba), who made Vaishnavism Manipur's state religion, Hiyangthang Lairembi was identified with the pan-Indian goddess Kamakhya, a form of Durga, grafting Shakta Hindu worship onto her older Sanamahi cult without displacing it. The temple today observes both traditions side by side: thousands of devotees gather for the Sanamahist Lai Haraoba festival as well as for Durga Puja, whose third day, locally called Bor Numit ("Boon Day"), is considered especially auspicious for receiving blessings. A dedicated association for the goddess's traditional worship was formally registered on 22 March 1979.
