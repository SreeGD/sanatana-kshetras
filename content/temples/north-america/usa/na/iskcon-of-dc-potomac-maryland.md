ISKCON of DC operates a Hare Krishna temple in Potomac, Maryland, in the Washington metropolitan area, distinct from the region's other (non-ISKCON) Hindu temples. Wikipedia's main article on ISKCON carries a photograph captioned "ISKCON, Potomac, Maryland, US (1974)," suggesting an organized ISKCON community in this area since at least the mid-1970s, in the movement's early expansion across the United States. The temple today runs a weekly Sunday program of kirtan, a talk, discussion group, and a vegetarian lunch open to the public, along with a food court (Govinda's) on site.
