Alachua, Florida, a small town roughly 16 miles northwest of Gainesville, is home to New Raman Reti, an ISKCON farm community and temple that Wikipedia's article on Alachua describes (citing a Gainesville-area tourism guide) as anchoring "the largest Hare Krishna community in the Western Hemisphere," with satellite communities across North Florida. The main ISKCON Wikipedia article separately names New Raman Reti among its list of notable U.S. centers, alongside the Spanish Fork, Utah temple and the Radha Kalachandji Temple in Dallas. The temple itself sits at 17306 NW 112th Blvd in Alachua, a location independently confirmed via two separate geocoding services. This entry documents the community's well-attested significance and now-verified address; a founding date could not be confirmed in this research pass and would be worth adding in a future update.
