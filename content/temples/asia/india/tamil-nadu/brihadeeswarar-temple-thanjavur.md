Built by the Chola emperor Rajaraja I and completed around 1010 CE, the Brihadeeswarar Temple is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the great achievements of Dravidian temple architecture, its vimanam (tower) rising over 60 meters and capped by a single monolithic granite block. Extensive Tamil inscriptions on its walls record the temple's original endowments, administration, and the era's dance and music traditions in unusually precise historical detail.
