Nueva Goloka Vrindavan is a Hare Krishna (Gaudiya Vaishnava) farm community near Cartago, Costa Rica, founded in 1986 after splitting from the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON) — the same devotional movement founded by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada that operates rural "Vrindavan"-themed farm communities elsewhere in the Americas, such as Nova Gokula in Brazil. Its name, meaning "New Goloka Vrindavan," invokes Krishna's eternal abode in Gaudiya Vaishnava cosmology.

Costa Rica's Hindu population is unusually small and, per Wikipedia, is composed today mostly of converts connected to the Hare Krishna movement rather than an Indian immigrant diaspora — a pattern distinct from most other entries in this directory's Central American and Caribbean coverage. Separate ISKCON and Gaudiya Math centers also operate in the capital, San José. This community's founders, exact address, and current institutional status were not independently confirmed beyond the "split from ISKCON" detail found in available sources.
