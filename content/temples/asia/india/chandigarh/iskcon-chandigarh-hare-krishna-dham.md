ISKCON Chandigarh's Hare Krishna Dham, in Sector 36-B, is a center of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness founded by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. The temple maintains a daily program of arati and darshan from 4:30 a.m. to 8:30 p.m., prasadam distribution, personal spiritual counseling, and congregational activities including Bhakti Yoga classes, youth empowerment programs, and summer camps for children. It observes Janmashtami, Ratha Yatra, and Deepotsava as its major festivals, and runs a Food for Life meal-distribution initiative for the surrounding community.
