Kisumu, Kenya's third-largest city and the country's main Lake Victoria port, is home to a Shri Swaminarayan Mandir affiliated with the NarNarayan Dev Gadi branch of the Swaminarayan sampradaya, per Wikipedia's inventory of Swaminarayan temples worldwide. This reflects the same pattern of Gujarati Hindu community-building along Kenya's early 20th-century railway and trading towns that produced Swaminarayan temples in Nairobi, Mombasa, and elsewhere in East Africa.

No founding date, address, or further institutional detail for this specific temple was located during this research pass; it is documented here only via a bare listing in Wikipedia's Swaminarayan temple inventory, but it fills a genuine geographic gap as the only currently documented Hindu temple in this directory for Kisumu.
