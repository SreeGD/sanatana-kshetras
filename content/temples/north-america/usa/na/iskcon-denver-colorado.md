ISKCON Denver was formally incorporated on October 25, 1976, during the final year of A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada's life, and continues to operate today at 1400 Cherry Street in Denver's South Park Hill neighborhood. Its existence is independently corroborated outside ISKCON's own materials by Wikipedia's article on the separate, non-ISKCON Hindu Temple and Cultural Center of the Rockies, which lists it among "other temples in the Denver region" under the same 1976 incorporation date.

The temple houses three deity groups — Sri Sri Radha Govinda; Sri Jagannatha Baladeva Subhadra; and Sri Sri Gaura Nitai — and maintains a full daily schedule of six aratis from early morning (4:30 AM) through evening (7:45 PM). Its own website describes ongoing community outreach through food distribution, bhakti education, festivals, music, meditation, and chanting programs, all free and open to the public, alongside an on-site gift shop.
