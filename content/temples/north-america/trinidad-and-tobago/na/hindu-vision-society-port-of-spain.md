Located on Ariapita Avenue in Woodbrook — an inner-city neighbourhood of Port of Spain known as a commercial and nightlife hub — the Hindu Vision Society is one of the few Hindu temples in this directory's Trinidad and Tobago coverage situated within the capital city itself, rather than in the rural central and southern areas where most of the island's Indo-Trinidadian Hindu community is concentrated. Its existence and address are documented in Wikipedia's list of Trinidadian Hindu temples, though that list's own source is an unofficial community compilation rather than an institutional or press account, so its founding history, founders, and presiding deity could not be independently confirmed this pass.
