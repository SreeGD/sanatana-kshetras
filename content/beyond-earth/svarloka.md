tradition_accounts:
  - tradition: vaishnava
    description: "Svarloka (or Svarga) is the heavenly realm ruled by Indra, king of the Devas, where righteous souls may be reborn temporarily to enjoy the fruits of their good karma before returning to the cycle of rebirth once that store of merit is exhausted. It is described across the Puranas and epics (Mahabharata, Ramayana) as a realm of great beauty and celestial pleasure, distinct from the permanent liberation (moksha) offered by the spiritual world beyond material creation."
