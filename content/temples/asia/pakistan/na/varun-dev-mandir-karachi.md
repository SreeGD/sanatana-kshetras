Shri Varun Dev Mandir, on Manora Island off Karachi's harbor, is described as the only temple in Pakistan dedicated to Varuna, the Vedic deity of water and the seas — a genuinely rare dedication among South Asia's surviving Hindu temples. The shrine also honors Jhulelal, venerated by Sindhi Hindus as an incarnation of Varuna and a central figure of Sindhi Hindu identity, making the temple a focal point for the community's most distinctive regional festival, Cheti Chand.

Tradition traces the temple's origins to a wealthy sailor, Bhojomal Nenshi Bhatia, said to have acquired Manora Island from the Khan of Kalat around the 16th century and commissioned the original shrine, with some accounts extending the site's sacred history back as far as 1,300 years — claims that could not be independently verified for this entry. The temple's current structure was renovated around 1917-1918, and after decades of deterioration from sea-spray and erosion, it underwent a further restoration in 2018 through a US-funded conservation project carried out by the Sindh Exploration and Adventure Society, with the Pakistan Hindu Council now managing the site as an active place of worship.
