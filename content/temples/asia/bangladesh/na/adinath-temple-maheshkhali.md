Adinath Temple crowns Mainak Hill on Maheshkhali Island, a short boat crossing from Cox's Bazar on Bangladesh's southeastern coast, making it one of the country's few significant hilltop Hindu pilgrimage sites and a rare instance of an island-hill shrine in the Ganges-Brahmaputra delta region. Dedicated to Shiva, worshipped here under the name Adinath ("the primordial lord"), the temple complex is reached by a climb up the hill after arriving on the island, with multiple gateways leading to a sanctum housing the deity's idol.

No founding date or origin narrative for the temple was established in the sources consulted for this entry, but its living significance is unmistakable: each year in the Bengali month of Phalgun, the temple hosts a thirteen-day fair that draws thousands of Hindu pilgrims from across Bangladesh, making it one of the largest annual Hindu gatherings in the country's southeast and a continuing anchor of Bengali Shaiva devotion on the Bay of Bengal coast.
