Situated at Thirukkoodalur, also known as Aaduthurai, in the Chola Nadu, this temple enshrines the Lord as Vaiyam Kaatha Perumal and is honoured among the 108 Divya Desams celebrated in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. The name of the presiding deity, Vaiyam Kaatha Perumal, means the Lord who protected the world, and it expresses the central significance of this sacred abode as a place where the Lord stands forth as the saviour and guardian of all creation. Tradition reveres the Moolavar here as the protector who shelters the earth and its beings, and the kshetra is approached by devotees seeking that same all-encompassing divine refuge. Lying in the rich Kaveri delta of the Chola country, Thirukkoodalur belongs to the cluster of Divya Desams that adorn this region sanctified by the great river, and its very location among these holy waters has long marked it as an auspicious place of worship. The glory of this Divya Desam was sung by Thirumangai Alwar, and his hymns within the Prabandham established the temple firmly among the divine abodes extolled by the Alwars, ensuring that the praise of Vaiyam Kaatha Perumal would be remembered and recited through the centuries. Pilgrims who come to this shrine venerate the Lord as the one whose protecting grace upholds the world, finding in his presence the assurance of shelter and the abiding mercy that the Alwar celebrated in his sacred verses.
