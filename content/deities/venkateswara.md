Venkateswara (also called Balaji or Srinivasa), the presiding deity of the Tirumala Venkateswara Temple, is one of the most widely replicated and devotionally significant Vishnu forms worldwide, with diaspora temples across nearly every continent this directory covers explicitly modeled on or dedicated to this specific form.
