This category covers Vishnu forms that are neither a specific named avatar (Rama, Krishna, Narasimha, etc.) nor Venkateswara or Jagannath — including the widespread reclining Ranganatha/Anantashayana form, Padmanabha, and the many Tamil 'Perumal' temple names that are Vishnu shrines without a distinct avatar identity, such as most of the 108 Divya Desams.
