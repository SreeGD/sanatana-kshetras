Opened in 2012, the BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir in Rotorua was, per available sources, the city's first Hindu temple and remains described as the only Hindu temple serving New Zealand's central North Island region. It stands on Holland Street in the city's Glenholme suburb. Like the country's other BAPS mandirs in Auckland, the Wellington area, Hamilton, and Christchurch, it follows the Swaminarayan Sampraday within the Vaishnava tradition, centering worship on Bhagwan Swaminarayan under the Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha organization.

The temple extends BAPS's New Zealand network beyond the country's largest cities, serving the smaller Gujarati and wider Hindu community in and around Rotorua with regular worship and festival observances such as Diwali and Annakut. Its precise address and consecration ceremony are not confirmed in the source consulted this pass, so key details beyond the 2012 opening remain to be verified.
