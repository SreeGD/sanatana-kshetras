Enshrines Hanuman in his Panchamukha (five-faced) form, an iconographic tradition representing a fierce composite aspect of the deity: an eastward Anjaneya (Hanuman) face said to grant purity of mind, a southward Narasimha (man-lion) face, a westward Garuda (eagle) face, a northward Varaha (boar) face, and an upward Hayagriva (horse) face. This composite form is recited in the Panchamukha Hanuman Kavacham, a protective hymn used in Hanuman worship across South India.
