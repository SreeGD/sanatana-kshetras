ISKCON Calgary is operated by the Radha Madhav Cultural Association (RMCA) at 313 4th Street NE in Calgary, Alberta, and forms part of the worldwide network of temples founded under the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON), established by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada in New York in 1966. Like other ISKCON centers, it belongs to the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition and centers its worship on regular arati, kirtan, and scriptural teaching. The RMCA's own website states the Calgary center was "founded in the late 1980s," making it, if confirmed, one of the older ISKCON communities in Western Canada, though no more precise year has been located.

The temple's altar enshrines Sri Sri Radha Madhava alongside Srinathji and Gaura Nitai, receiving daily worship and garland offerings from the community. Beyond daily worship, RMCA runs Food for Life and community-kitchen meal programs, supports newcomer settlement, and operates the Calgary Vedic Academy for youth education; it is currently fundraising to build a larger dedicated temple complex ("Radha Madhav Dham") at a separate site in northeast Calgary, though the existing 313 4th Street NE location remains the active temple at time of writing.
