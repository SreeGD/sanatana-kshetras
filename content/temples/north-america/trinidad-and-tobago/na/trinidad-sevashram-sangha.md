The Trinidad branch of the Bharat Sevashram Sangha, a Hindu monastic and humanitarian order founded in Kolkata, India in 1917 by Swami Pranavananda, stands at 32 Nolan Street in Felicity, within the Chaguanas area of central Trinidad — one of the island's major centers of Indo-Trinidadian Hindu settlement. The parent Sangha, with more than three hundred branches worldwide including in Guyana, Canada, the United Kingdom, the United States, Fiji, and Bangladesh, is organized around karma yoga: combining traditional Hindu worship and religious education with active humanitarian service such as healthcare camps, disaster relief, and support for pilgrims, rather than centering on a single sectarian deity.

As with many of the order's ashrams internationally, the Trinidad Sevashram Sangha likely combines a place of daily worship with community and social-service programming for Trinidad's large Indo-Caribbean Hindu population, descended largely from nineteenth-century Indian indentured laborers. This specific branch's own founding date, local founders, and the particular deities enshrined at its shrine were not independently confirmed in this pass and should be treated as unverified pending fuller local sourcing.
