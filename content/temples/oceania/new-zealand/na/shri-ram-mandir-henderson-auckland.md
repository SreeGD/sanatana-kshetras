Established in September 2011 by the Shri Ram Mandir Charitable Trust, this West Auckland temple describes itself as the first community initiative of its kind in the area — combining a dedicated Hindu place of worship with a broader community welfare mission. Its primary dedication to Rama, the Ramayana's central hero and one of Vishnu's principal avatars, is a comparatively distinctive focus among New Zealand's Hindu temples, most of which center on Krishna, Venkateswara/Balaji, or the Swaminarayan tradition; the temple additionally houses shrines to Shiva, Parvati, and Ganesha.

Open daily from 7:30am to 8:30pm at 11 Brick Street in Henderson, the temple runs daily prayers alongside weekly programs featuring Ramayana recitations and devotional singing, and hosts annual celebrations tied to its festival calendar. Beyond worship, the trust emphasizes educational programs and social services for West Auckland's Hindu community as part of its founding welfare mission.
