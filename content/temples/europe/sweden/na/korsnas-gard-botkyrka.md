Korsnäs Gård is a rural ISKCON (Hare Krishna) community and temple in Botkyrka Municipality, part of Stockholm County, Sweden. Unlike most other ISKCON farm communities documented elsewhere in this directory, its distinguishing role is publishing rather than agriculture: Korsnäs Gård's main function, per Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in Sweden, is to house the Bhaktivedanta Book Trust (BBT) operation that translates Srila Prabhupada's Hare Krishna books into languages used around the world, making it a significant institutional node for the movement's literature distribution well beyond Sweden's own borders.

The Swedish ISKCON movement's own history traces back to 1973, when its founder, A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, visited Sweden in person — around the same period he was also establishing ISKCON's presence in Switzerland, Italy, and elsewhere in Europe. Beyond this general historical context and its BBT publishing role, the source available for this entry gives no specific founding date for Korsnäs Gård itself, nor a precise address; fuller documentation would need to be confirmed directly with ISKCON Sweden.
