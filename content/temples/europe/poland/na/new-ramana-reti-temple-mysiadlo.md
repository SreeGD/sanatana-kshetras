Founded in 1989, nine years after the country's first ISKCON community was established at New Shantipur in Czarnów (already documented elsewhere in this directory), the New Ramana Reti Temple in Mysiadło, near Warsaw, is described by Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in Poland as ISKCON Poland's "main" temple — reflecting Warsaw's importance as the country's capital and largest population center for the movement's continued growth beyond its original rural Silesian base.

As at other ISKCON temples founded during this period, including its sister community New Navadvip in Wrocław, worship at New Ramana Reti centers on the Pancha Tattva in the paired form of Gaura-Nitai — Chaitanya Mahaprabhu and his close associate Nityananda — central to Gaudiya Vaishnava devotional practice descending from the missionary work of A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. Beyond its 1989 founding date, location, and deity dedication, further detail on the temple's founders, building, and current congregation size could not be confirmed in the sources available for this entry.
