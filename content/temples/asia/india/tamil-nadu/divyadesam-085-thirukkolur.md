The Vaithamanidhi Perumal Temple at Thirukkolur, in the Pandya Nadu country of the Tamil land, enshrines the Lord as Vaithamanidhi Perumal and is counted among the 108 Divya Desams, the sacred abodes of Vishnu hymned by the Alwars. The very name Vaithamanidhi, traditionally understood as "the treasure that is laid up," expresses the Lord's character as the imperishable wealth safeguarded for His devotees, a refuge richer than any worldly fortune. Lying close to the cluster of nine Divya Desams gathered along the banks of the Thamiraparani river in the deep south, Thirukkolur is intimately linked with the life of Nammalwar, the foremost of the Alwars, who was born nearby and whose verses pervade this whole region. Nammalwar alone sang the glory of this shrine in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham, the collected four thousand verses of the Alwars, and his benediction has shaped its devotional fame ever since. Tradition cherishes Thirukkolur especially for its association with the celebrated saintly woman remembered as Thirukkolur Ammal, whose poignant utterances—recalling the great surrendered souls of scripture and lamenting her own lack of their fervour—have become a beloved expression of prapatti, loving self-surrender to the Lord. In her words the village stands as a teacher of humility before the divine treasure that Vaithamanidhi embodies. Pilgrims who tread the southern circuit of Divya Desams come here to behold the Lord who is the hidden, unfailing wealth of the soul, and to drink in the atmosphere of total dependence on grace that the place has long preserved.

## Festivals

As a Srivaishnava Divya Desam celebrated in the Nalayira Divya Prabandham, Thirukkolur traditionally observes the great annual Brahmotsavam, when Vaithamanidhi Perumal is taken in procession on the garuda, hanumantha and other vahanas amid Vedic chanting and recitation of the Tamil Prabandham. Vaikunta Ekadasi in the month of Margazhi is kept with special reverence, the Lord granting darshan to devotees, alongside the ten-day Pagal-pathu and Ra-pathu recitations characteristic of the region. The annual Pavithrotsavam of purification and a float festival (Theppotsavam) are observed where local custom maintains them. The village is also remembered through the saintly Thirukkolur Ammal tradition, and the Tamil months of Aadi, Purattasi and Margazhi draw special worship and Prabandham recitation.

## Location & Access

The temple stands at Thirukkolur in the Thoothukudi district of the historic Pandya Nadu, on the southern bank of the Tamiraparani river near Alwarthirunagari, a short way from Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi, the nearest major towns and transport hubs. The site lies along the cluster of Tamiraparani Divya Desams in this region. Like most Vishnu temples it generally observes morning and evening darshan hours, closing through the midday interval.
