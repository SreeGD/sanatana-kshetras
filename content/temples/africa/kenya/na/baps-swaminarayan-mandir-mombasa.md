Inaugurated on 25 April 1955, the BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir in Mombasa holds a distinctive place in the history of the Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha (BAPS): Wikipedia describes it as "Africa's first Akshar-Purushottam temple," making it the sampradaya's original foothold on the continent, predating its later, far larger Nairobi mandir by over four decades.

Its consecration came during Yogiji Maharaj's first-ever foreign tour, an East Africa trip that carried him across the region's Gujarati diaspora communities — Nairobi, Nakuru, Kisumu, Tororo, Jinja, and Kampala inland, and Mwanza and Dar es Salaam further south — of which the Mombasa mandir was the earliest fruit. It continues to serve Mombasa's Gujarati Hindu community as a place of Swaminarayan worship, though no further detail on its address, architecture, or current activities was located during this research pass.
