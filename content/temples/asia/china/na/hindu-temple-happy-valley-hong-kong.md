The Hindu Temple in Hong Kong's Happy Valley district, situated at 1B Wong Nei Chong Road directly opposite the Happy Valley Racecourse, functions as the central religious and community institution for Hong Kong's Hindu population — estimated at roughly 100,000 people as of 2016, up from about 40,000 in 2010, predominantly descendants of migrants from the Indian subcontinent. Managed by the Hindu Association of Hong Kong, the temple hosts traditional poojas, weddings, and other religious ceremonies serving the wider community.

Beyond its role as a place of worship, the temple operates a crematorium at Cape Collins and serves as gatekeeper to an adjacent Commonwealth War Graves Commission memorial honoring eight Hindu and Sikh soldiers who died in the First World War, reflecting the long history of South Asian communities in Hong Kong. No confirmed founding date or details of the temple's specific enshrined deities or architecture were available from the source checked in this pass.
