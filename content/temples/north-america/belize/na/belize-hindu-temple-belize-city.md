The Belize Hindu Temple on Albert Street in Belize City is one of only two named Hindu temples documented for the country of Belize (alongside the Sukh Shanti Temple in Corozal), per Wikipedia's "Religion in Belize" article. It serves a small Hindu community, roughly 0.2% of Belize's population per the 2010 census, composed almost entirely of Sindhi and other Indo-Pakistani families who arrived in Belize as British colonial subjects in the 1950s — a distinct migration stream from the nineteenth-century plantation-indenture history that shaped larger Hindu communities elsewhere in the Caribbean, such as in Trinidad, Guyana, and Jamaica.

Major festivals observed include Diwali and Janmashtami. No founding date, founder, or specific presiding deity could be confirmed for this temple in this pass, and this entry should be treated as provisional pending fuller sourcing.
