Standing on Banjara Hills in central Hyderabad, the Hare Krishna Golden Temple was inaugurated in 2018 by then Vice President of India M. Venkaiah Naidu and is described as the first Golden Temple built in Telangana. The complex is marked by a 50-foot gold-plated dhwaja stambha (flagstaff), a 4,600-square-foot Maha Mandapam, and a five-tier gold-plated Rajagopuram entrance tower, alongside a Japa Mantapa with 108 steps for chanting. Four shrines house its presiding deities: a swayambhu (self-manifest) Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy, Sri Sri Radha-Govinda, a swayambhu form of Shiva as Panchajanyeshwara Swamy, and Hanuman as Japa Anjaneya Swamy. The temple, run by the HKM Hyderabad charitable society within the wider Hare Krishna movement founded by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, also preserves a Shaligram shila said to have been found in the Gandaki River near Muktinath, Nepal, and supports Akshaya Patra-linked free food distribution.

Separately, in the wider Hyderabad region, ISKCON proper also runs a Krishna-Balarama temple and goshala at Dabilpur village near Medchal, home to some 150 cows and organic farming, and visited annually by thousands of devotees — distinct from both this Banjara Hills temple and from Hyderabad's well-known Birla Mandir.
