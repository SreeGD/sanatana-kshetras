Vishnu Mandir is a large Hindu temple complex on Yonge Street in Richmond Hill, just north of Toronto, operated by the Voice of Vedas Cultural Sabha alongside affiliated community institutions on the same property, including a school and cultural museum. Its central shrines are dedicated to Vishnu and his forms (Balaji/Venkateswara and Narsimha) alongside Shiva, Radha-Krishna, and Hanuman, reflecting the pan-Hindu, multi-deity worship style common among many temples serving the Indo-Caribbean and broader South Asian diaspora in the Greater Toronto Area. Beyond its role as a place of daily worship, the temple has served as a venue for interfaith and cultural programming for the region's Hindu community; a precise founding date could not be independently confirmed in this pass and should be verified against additional sources before being treated as settled.
