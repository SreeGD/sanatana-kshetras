Lakshmi, goddess of prosperity and Vishnu's consort, is independently enshrined as the principal deity at a number of major temples (distinct from the vastly more common role as a secondary consort-shrine within a Vishnu temple complex) — this category covers temples where Lakshmi/Mahalakshmi is the presiding deity in her own right.
