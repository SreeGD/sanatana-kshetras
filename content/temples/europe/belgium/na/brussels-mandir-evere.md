Brussels Mandir traces its origins to around the year 2000, when Indian families who had settled in Brussels began gathering informally in a borrowed church or school hall to worship together, most notably to celebrate Maa Durga Jaagran in honor of the goddess Durga. As the community grew over the following decade, it moved from these informal gatherings toward establishing a dedicated place of worship, holding a soft opening ceremony for the Brussels Mandir in the municipality of Evere on 19 February 2012.

Since opening, the Mandir has held weekly Sunday services and marks the major Hindu festivals with special events, alongside yoga and classical Indian dance classes that give it a role as a cultural as well as religious hub for Brussels' Indian Hindu community. Documentation of the temple beyond these facts is limited in publicly available sources; further detail on its founders, its building, and its full religious calendar would need to be confirmed directly with the community.
