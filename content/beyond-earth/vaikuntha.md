    description: "Vaikuntha is described in Vaishnava scripture as the eternal, self-luminous spiritual realm beyond the material cosmos altogether, where Vishnu-Narayana resides permanently with Lakshmi and is attended by ever-liberated beings (nityasuris) such as Vishvaksena, Garuda, and Ananta Shesha. Liberated souls (muktas) who attain moksha through devotion are held, in this tradition, to reside there eternally in loving service to the Lord, free from the cycle of birth and death that governs even the highest material planes like Satyaloka. This same abode, under the name Sri Vaikuntam or Paramapadam, is also catalogued as the 108th and final Divya Desam in the Sri Vaishnava Naalayira Divya Prabandham tradition (see content/divya-desam/108-paramapadam.md), where it is glorified in dedicated verse by eight Alvars."
