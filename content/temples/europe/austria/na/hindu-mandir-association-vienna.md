Founded in 1980 by Dr. Bimal Kundu, an engineer originally from Bengal, the Hindu Mandir Association was the first organized Hindu religious group established in Vienna, initially operating out of the city's Afro-Asian Institute. Described by Wikipedia's overview of Hinduism in Austria as "one of the oldest organisations in Vienna," it has long played what the same source calls "a major role in presenting Hinduism to the public" in the Austrian capital, operating a mandir (temple) alongside its public-facing educational and cultural activities.

In 2006 the association sought formal state recognition as a religious confessional community in Austria, a legal status that would have placed it alongside Austria's officially recognized religious bodies; it withdrew that initial application and reapplied instead under the name Hindu Religious Community. Beyond this institutional history, the sources available for this entry do not describe the mandir's building, its specific deity images, or its current address, leaving Austria's only confirmed Hindu temple entry in this directory a genuinely thin one pending fuller documentation directly from the association.
