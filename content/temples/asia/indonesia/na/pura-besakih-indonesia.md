The largest and holiest temple complex of Balinese Hinduism (Agama Hindu Dharma), Pura Besakih is a 'mother temple' comprising over twenty separate temples terraced up the slope of the sacred volcano Mount Agung. Balinese Hinduism represents a living, continuously-practiced Hindu tradition outside India with its own distinct ritual calendar and cosmology, sharing core Vedic and Puranic deities while incorporating local Balinese custom (adat).
