ISKCON Calgary is operated by the Radha Madhav Cultural Association (RMCA) at 313 4th Street NE in Calgary, Alberta, and forms part of the worldwide network of temples founded under the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON), established by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada in New York in 1966. Like other ISKCON centers, it belongs to the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition and centers its worship on regular arati, kirtan, and scriptural teaching.

The temple's altar enshrines Sri Sri Radha Madhava alongside Srinathji and Gaura Nitai, receiving daily worship and garland offerings from the community. Available sources confirm the temple's current address, presiding deities, and operating association, but no dedicated secondary source giving the Calgary center's specific founding year or early history was located in this pass, so that detail should be treated as unconfirmed pending further research.
