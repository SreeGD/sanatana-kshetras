ISKCON Chicago was incorporated in Illinois in 1974 and, per Wikipedia's list of U.S. Hindu temples, moved from an earlier Evanston location to its present Rogers Park address in 1980. The temple, home to the presiding deities Sri Sri Kishora Kishori, holds a regular Sunday Feast program and a Monthly Sankirtan Festival, situating it among the several hundred ISKCON centers worldwide founded in the movement's first decade after Prabhupada's arrival in the United States.
