Sri Nilacala Dhama is ISKCON's temple in Jakarta, located in the Pasar Baru area of Sawah Besar, Central Jakarta. It worships a set of deities typical of larger ISKCON centers: Radha Krishna as the presiding pair, together with Jagannath, Baladeva, and Subhadra (the deity-forms associated with the Rath Yatra chariot festival tradition), and Gaura Nitai (Chaitanya Mahaprabhu and Nityananda). ISKCON's presence in Indonesia traces back at least to the 1970s, when the Bhaktivedanta Book Trust distributed Indonesian translations of Srila Prabhupada's writings from Jakarta, part of the movement's broader push to spread Gaudiya Vaishnava literature across Southeast Asia.

As with many ISKCON centers, the temple's day-to-day life centers on deity worship, kirtan, and public festivals, with Janmashtami as the most prominent annual observance. A precise founding date for the present Sri Nilacala Dhama building was not available from the sources consulted during this research pass and should be confirmed before being stated as fact.
