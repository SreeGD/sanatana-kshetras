Appakkudathaan Temple at Koviladi (Thirukkudalur) is one of the 108 Divya Desams — the sacred abodes of Vishnu glorified in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. It is sung in mangalasasanam by 4 Alvars: Thirumazhisai Alwar, Nammalwar, Periyalwar, Thirumangai Alwar. It belongs to the Chola Nadu group of Divya Desams.
