In the Albuquerque neighborhood of Teresópolis — a mountain town in Rio de Janeiro state known for a cooler climate than the coast — stands Hare Krishna Ashram Vrajabhumi, a Gaudiya Vaishnava temple and ashram whose name recalls Vrajabhumi, the Braj region of North India associated with Krishna's childhood. It is a separate institution from Brazil's better-documented Nova Gokula farm community, elsewhere in São Paulo state, also covered in this directory.

The ashram's existence and location are corroborated independently by both the English and Portuguese Wikipedia articles on Teresópolis, each illustrating the city's religious landscape with a photograph of the temple. Beyond that, institutional details — founding date, founders, and whether it operates formally under ISKCON or as an independent Hare Krishna-affiliated group — could not be confirmed this pass.
