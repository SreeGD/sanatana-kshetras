Palm Ridge, a suburb within the Ekurhuleni Metropolitan Municipality in Gauteng's East Rand (part of greater Johannesburg), is home to the Shree Sivasubramaniar Alayam, a Tamil Hindu temple dedicated to Murugan (Subramanya). It is named in Wikipedia's overview of the Thaipusam festival as one of South Africa's celebration sites for the annual Kavady procession, alongside temples in Durban and Clairwood.

Beyond its role as a Thaipusam Kavady venue and its general location in Palm Ridge, no dedicated source describing the temple's founding, architecture, or day-to-day worship life was located during this research pass; it stands as the greater Johannesburg area's only currently documented Hindu temple outside Lenasia in this directory, filling a genuine geographic gap even with thin sourcing.
