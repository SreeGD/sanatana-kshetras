Near the village of Akkur, a short distance from the main Thirunangur cluster, this temple enshrines Vishnu as Nanmadhiya Perumal, worshipped alongside his consort Thalaichanga Nachiyar. The temple's Tamil name, Thalai Sanga Nanmadhiyam, is traditionally read as referring to the "four-storeyed" (nanmadi) structure with which the shrine was originally associated, and, like the other temples of this stretch of the delta, it is built in the Dravidian style characteristic of the Kaveri basin's Divya Desams.

The temple is glorified in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham through the hymns of Thirumangai Alvar, part of that saint's larger devotional circuit through the villages around Sirkazhi. Most traditional enumerations count Thalaichangadu among the eleven Thirunangur Divya Desams and it duly takes part in the shared Thirumangai Alvar Mangalasasana Utsavam each Tamil month of Thai; however, a minority of sources describe it as a related but separately administered shrine on the edge of the Thirunangur group rather than one of the "core" eleven, a point of traditional variation that this entry flags rather than resolves.
