Serving Lenasia, a Johannesburg suburb established for Indian residents under apartheid-era group areas policy, this temple is dedicated primarily to Lakshmi Narayan (Vishnu with Lakshmi), with subsidiary worship of Hanuman and Ram. Its own promotional materials reference a 50th-anniversary milestone around 2026, suggesting the temple was founded in the mid-1970s, though no source consulted gives an explicit founding date or founder's name. The temple today runs daily aarti, weekly Vedic-chanting and Bhagavad Gita study classes, a Sunday children's school, and a large online library of Hindu philosophy texts, alongside observance of major festivals such as Ram Navami and Hanuman Jayanti.
