In the Blauwgrond neighborhood of northern Paramaribo, the Shri Radha Krishna Mandir has stood on Narainastraat since 1991, described in local sources as a colorful example of the Sanatan Dharma mandir style common among Suriname's Hindustani community — descendants of Indian indentured laborers brought to the Dutch colony beginning in 1873. As with many Sanatan Dharma temples in Suriname, it most likely serves as both a place of daily worship and a venue for community festivals such as Diwali and Holi Phagwa, though a fuller institutional history (founders, architects, complete deity roster) was not available in the source consulted this pass.

The temple's name is shared with at least one other Radha Krishna Mandir listed elsewhere in Paramaribo, and it has not been confirmed whether these refer to the same building under two different addresses or two separate temples serving different parts of the city — a detail that should be resolved before treating this entry as fully settled.
