Pura Mandaragiri Sumeru Agung, completed in July 1992 on the slope of Mount Semeru — Java's highest peak and an active volcano — is documented in Wikipedia's account of Hinduism in Indonesia as one of several new Hindu temples built in eastern Java during a modern revival of Hindu practice there, constructed with substantial support from Balinese donors even though, at the time of its completion, only a small number of local families in the area formally identified as Hindu.

The temple's mountainside setting on Semeru situates it within a broader Javanese and Balinese tradition of associating sacred mountains with the cosmic Mount Meru of Hindu cosmology, echoing the older mountain temples of Bali such as Pura Besakih on Mount Agung, already documented elsewhere in this directory. Beyond its 1992 completion date, general location, and role in eastern Java's modern Hindu community, further architectural and historical detail could not be confirmed from the source checked in this pass.
