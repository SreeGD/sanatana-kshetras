Shree Laxmi Narayan Temple serves the Hindu community of Burbank, a suburb south of Brisbane, Queensland, dedicated to Lakshmi and Narayan (Vishnu) — a paired form emphasizing prosperity and preservation central to Vaishnava household worship. The temple gained brief national attention in Australia on 4 March 2023, when its boundary wall was vandalized in an incident temple president Satinder Shukla and devotees reported to Queensland Police that morning; Australian Hindu-community media coverage attributed the incident to pro-Khalistan extremists as part of a wider pattern of attacks on Hindu temples and symbols across Australia around that period, including a separate reported threat against an unnamed "prominent" Brisbane temple the previous month.

Beyond this documented incident, detailed institutional history for the temple — its founding date, architectural style, and regular festival calendar — is not confirmed in the sources consulted this pass, so this entry is recorded as pending further verification.
