Prabhupada's followers established a Krishna-consciousness mission in Honolulu in 1969, and Hawaii went on to hold particular personal significance for A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada himself: according to the temple's own account, he personally stayed there and translated Vedic texts between 1974 and 1976, a period the temple now commemorates as the "Srila Prabhupada Memorial Tirtha." The temple operates today at 51 Coelho Way in Honolulu's Nu'uanu area, alongside its long-running Govinda's Hawaii vegetarian buffet restaurant at the same address.

Described by at least one independent travel-review source as "the main Hindu temple in Honolulu," the temple continues to operate as an active ISKCON center, listed in ISKCON's own current worldwide directory. Beyond its founding date, address, and the Pancha Tattva devotional tradition centered on Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu that it follows, further specifics — individual deity names, a founding leader, and a detailed festival calendar — could not be confirmed from the sources available for this entry.
