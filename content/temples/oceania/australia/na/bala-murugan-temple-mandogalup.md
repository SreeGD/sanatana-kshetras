Ground was broken for this Dravidian-style temple in Mandogalup, south of Perth, in 1996, but planning and construction delays meant it did not open until 11 May 2008, when its first Kumbhabhishekham consecration ceremony was performed. It is run by the Saiva Maha Sabai of Western Australia and staffed largely by volunteers, serving the South Indian Tamil Hindu community in particular, though it welcomes devotees of all backgrounds.

The temple's principal deity is Murugan (Karthikeya), Shiva's son and the focus of its name, but the complex also houses shrines to fourteen other deities, including Ganesha, Shiva, Ayyappan, Venkateshvara, Lakshmi, Durga, and Hanuman, making it a broad multi-deity worship site typical of diaspora Hindu communities. Thaipusam, the major Tamil festival honoring Murugan, is its most significant annual observance, alongside Ayyappan Poojai, Pradosham, and Lakshmi Pooja throughout the year.
